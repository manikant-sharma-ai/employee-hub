# Listing compliance in India

A rejected catalogue is a week lost and a suppressed listing is invisible. Compliance is
checked before publishing, every time.

## The declaration block

Every physical product listing carries, at minimum:

| Declaration | Where it goes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Country of origin | Attribute, and on the pack | Importer or manufacturer |
| Net quantity | Title or attribute, and on the pack | Pack specification |
| MRP inclusive of taxes | Price attribute | Pricing decision |
| Manufacturer or importer name and address | Detail page and pack | Supplier |
| Consumer care details | Detail page | Your own |
| HSN code and GST rate | Commercial attributes | CA |
| Shelf life or best before | Attribute, where applicable | Supplier |

Legal metrology rules apply to pre-packaged goods: the net quantity, the MRP, the
manufacturer's details and the consumer care contact are not optional, and the ecommerce
entity has its own declaration duties. Confirm the current form of the rule with a
compliance consultant or the CA - the shape changes.

## Licences by category

| Category | What is needed | Who issues |
|---|---|---|
| Food and beverages | FSSAI licence, and the licence number on the label | FSSAI |
| Health supplements, nutraceuticals | FSSAI licence in the right category | FSSAI |
| Cosmetics | Cosmetics licence under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules | State drug authority |
| Medical devices | CDSCO registration or licence, by class | CDSCO |
| Electricals and electronics | BIS registration for notified products, ISI mark | BIS |
| Toys | BIS conformity for notified toys | BIS |
| E-waste items | EPR registration for the producer | CPCB |
| Seeds, pesticides, fertilisers | Licence under the relevant control order | State agriculture authority |
| Packaged drinking water | BIS and FSSAI | BIS, FSSAI |
| Ayurvedic products | AYUSH licence | State AYUSH authority |
| Gold and jewellery | BIS hallmarking | BIS |

If you cannot confirm which applies, the answer is "confirm with the CA or the compliance
consultant before listing", not a guess. A licence number invented on a label is a legal
exposure, not a listing error.

## Claims that get a listing suppressed

| Claim type | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Superlative | "Best in India", "No. 1" | Unsubstantiated; platform policy |
| Medical | "Cures", "treats", "prevents disease" | Drug claim on a food or cosmetic |
| Absolute safety | "100% safe", "no side effects" | Unprovable |
| Certification you do not hold | "ISO certified" without the certificate | Misrepresentation |
| Organic without certification | "Organic" on an uncertified product | India Organic requires certification |
| Comparative | "Better than <brand>" | Disparagement risk |
| Guarantee of outcome | "Guaranteed weight loss" | Both policy and law |

Rewrite to what the product can prove: "cold pressed below 40°C" instead of "healthiest",
"FSSAI licensed" instead of "100% safe".

## Images and labels: the rules that cause rejections

- **Main image on white**, product filling most of the frame, no text, no watermark, no
  props, no borders.
- **No seller contact details** in any image - phone, website, address. This is the single
  most common rejection.
- **No before-and-after** imagery in categories where it implies a medical outcome.
- **No other brand's logo** anywhere in the frame.
- **Label artwork must match the listing** - net quantity, licence number, MRP. A mismatch
  is a return and a complaint.

## Documents to keep ready for a platform audit

1. The licence or registration, valid on the date of listing.
2. The label artwork, as printed, front and back.
3. The supplier invoice for the batch, showing the source.
4. The test report or certificate, where the category needs one.
5. The country-of-origin declaration from the supplier, for imports.

Keep them in one folder per SKU. A platform audit asks for all five at once, with a
deadline.

## The pre-publish checklist

```
[ ] Category confirmed, and its mandatory attributes known
[ ] Licence identified and the certificate seen (not assured)
[ ] Country of origin, net quantity, MRP, manufacturer details on the listing
[ ] HSN and GST rate confirmed with the CA
[ ] Main image on white, no text, no watermark, no contact details
[ ] No superlative, medical, absolute-safety or unheld-certification claim
[ ] Label artwork matches the listing exactly
[ ] Restricted-category approval obtained, where the category needs it
[ ] Batch and expiry handled, where the product expires
```

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Cost |
|---|---|
| "The supplier says it is certified" | No certificate, no listing; a platform audit ends the account |
| Claim copied from a competitor | Suppression, and a legal letter |
| MRP on the listing different from the label | Return, complaint, and a policy flag |
| Contact details in an image | Immediate rejection |
| Expired licence left on a live listing | Delisting and a refund obligation |
| One compliance check at launch, never again | The licence expires and nobody notices |
