# Channel rules

Four channels, four different buyers, four different sets of rules. These are the
rules that reject or suppress a listing, which are the ones worth memorising.

## The comparison

| | IndiaMART | Amazon.in | Flipkart | GeM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer | B2B bulk, wholesale, institutional | Retail consumer | Retail consumer | Government departments |
| Unit of sale | Quantity, MOQ, price breaks | Single unit, variations | Single unit, variations | Unit with specification compliance |
| Title limit (practical) | 15-100 characters, keyword-led | 200 characters, brand + product + key attribute | 120 characters | Specification-led, category template |
| Mandatory attributes | Brand, material, MOQ, price | Brand, material, size, colour, HSN | Brand, model, colour, HSN | Brand, model, warranty, HSN, OEM status |
| Images | 1 minimum, 3+ better | 3 minimum, white background main image | 3 minimum | 1 minimum, specification sheet often required |
| Statutory | GSTIN, sometimes Udyam | HSN, GSTIN, category licence | HSN, GSTIN | HSN, GSTIN, OEM/authorisation, often ISO |
| Rejection causes | Wrong category, missing MOQ, unverified GST | Incomplete attributes, HSN mismatch, prohibited claims, image quality | Category mismatch, brand approval missing, title spam | Specification mismatch, missing OEM certificate, price above L1 |
| Who approves | Mostly instant, verification for some | Category gating for some brands | Brand approval for many categories | Department acceptance after bid |

## IndiaMART

Enquiry-driven, not transaction-driven. The listing's job is to produce a phone
call or an enquiry, so the title carries the trade term ("wholesale", "bulk",
"lot of 50") and the price is indicative. MOQ and price breaks are part of the
listing, not a negotiation afterwards. Verified GSTIN and a real address move a
seller up the response order more than any copy change.

## Amazon.in

Attribute completeness drives both search placement and whether the listing is
suppressed. The main image must be the product on white; a banner or a lifestyle
shot as image one loses the buy box position. Variation families (size, colour)
must be created as a family, or the reviews split. Brand approval (gating) applies
in several categories and must be cleared before listing.

## Flipkart

Brand approval is required in many categories and is the most common first
blocker. Titles are shorter and Flipkart penalises keyword stuffing more visibly
than Amazon. Model number is mandatory and is the key that links the listing to
the catalogue; a wrong model number creates a duplicate listing that cannot be
merged easily.

## GeM

Government procurement. The specification comes from the buyer's category
template, and a listing that deviates from it is rejected at bid stage, not at
listing stage. OEM status, authorisation letters, ISO certificates and warranty
terms are checked. Price is compared against the L1 across sellers, so a listing
is also a pricing decision.

## The rule that applies to all four

**The channel's own validation is the specification.** Read the category template
for the exact category, not the general help page. Category templates change, and
the change is what breaks a bulk upload that worked last quarter.
