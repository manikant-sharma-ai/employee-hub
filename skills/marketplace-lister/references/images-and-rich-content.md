# Images and rich content

The image is the listing. On a phone, the main image is 200 pixels wide and it decides
whether anything else is ever read.

## The stack

| # | Image | Job | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main | Identify the product instantly | White background, product at 85% of frame, min 1000 px on the longest side, 1:1 |
| 2 | Pack or scale | Show what arrives, and the size | In hand or beside a familiar object |
| 3 | Infographic | The three specifications that decide the purchase | Text legible at thumbnail size |
| 4 | In use | The product doing its job | Real context, not a stock photo |
| 5 | Detail | Material, texture, build quality | Macro, honest |
| 6 | Comparison or range | The variants side by side | Prevents the "which size" question |
| 7 | Video, where supported | 15-30 seconds, the product in use | No music-only; show the function |

Amazon allows up to nine images and a video per listing; Flipkart has its own count and
spec; IndiaMART is more forgiving but a clear product photo still outperforms a catalogue
scan; GeM requires images that match the catalogue specification. Confirm the current spec
in the seller panel - do not carry a number from memory into a brief.

## Rules that get images rejected

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| White background on the main image | Off-white and light grey are usually accepted; shadows and props are not |
| No text on the main image | No slogans, no "50% off", no "free shipping" |
| No watermarks or logos that are not the brand's | Including the photographer's |
| No contact details | Phone, website, email, address - the commonest rejection |
| No borders or frames | Full-bleed product photography |
| Product must fill the frame | A small product in a large white field reads as a cheap listing |
| No other brand's product | Even in a "compatible with" shot, in some categories |

## Legibility at 200 pixels

The test: shrink the main image to a thumbnail on a phone and look at it. If the product
is not identifiable in one second, the image fails, whatever the resolution.

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Product is a small object in a big white square | Crop tighter, fill 85% of the frame |
| Two products in the main image | One product, one pack |
| Dark product on a dark background | White background, better light |
| Text-heavy infographic as the main image | Move the infographic to position 3 |

## The infographic that works

Three lines, maximum, in the order the buyer cares:

1. **The specification** - "500 ml | Cold pressed | Glass bottle"
2. **The differentiator** - "Unrefined, below 40°C"
3. **The trust mark** - "FSSAI licensed" or "1-year warranty"

Font size at least 5% of the image height. Left-aligned, top-third, on a clean band. No
more than three claims - a fourth is unread.

## A+ or rich content (Amazon) and its equivalents

| Module | Use |
|---|---|
| Brand story | Who makes it and why - only if true |
| Comparison chart | Your own variants, side by side |
| Three-image feature row | Specification, use, what is in the box |
| Lifestyle full-width | The product in the buyer's world |
| FAQ text block | The four questions in the reviews and the messages |

Rules: no pricing, no promotions, no contact details, no "buy now", no reviews or
testimonials in A+ modules, no claims you cannot support. A+ content lifts conversion on
considered purchases and does nothing for a ₹199 impulse item.

## The brief to `graphic-designer`

Send a shot list, not a mood board:

```
SKU: <name> | Platform: <Amazon.in> | Category: <...>
Deliver: 7 images, 2000x2000 px, sRGB, JPEG, main on pure white (#FFFFFF)
1. Main - front, 85% fill, no props
2. Pack - with a hand for scale
3. Infographic - "500 ml | Cold pressed | Glass" (copy attached)
4. In use - kitchen counter, morning light
5. Detail - texture macro
6. Range - 200/500/1000 ml side by side
7. Label close-up - front and back, declarations legible
Do not: add text to image 1, add contact details, use stock imagery, retouch the label
```

## Video, where it helps

15-30 seconds, no voiceover needed if the product is visual: open with the product in use,
show the one thing a photo cannot (texture, movement, fit), close with the pack. Vertical
for mobile-first placements.

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Cost |
|---|---|
| One image, straight from the supplier's catalogue | No differentiation, poor thumbnail |
| Main image with a promotional banner | Rejection |
| Infographic with eight claims | Nothing is read |
| Stock lifestyle photo the product is not in | Returns, and a review that says "not as shown" |
| Low resolution, no zoom | The buyer cannot check quality and leaves |
| No video on a considered purchase | Lower conversion than the competitor who has one |
