# Titles, attributes and search

On a marketplace, the title is the search query and the attributes are the filter. Copy
that ignores either is decoration.

## How the search works

```
Buyer types a query
  → platform matches title, backend keywords and attributes
  → filters narrow by category attributes (size, colour, material, pack)
  → ranking sorts by relevance, price, fulfilment, reviews, account health
  → the main image and price decide the click
```

Three places to be found, three places to fail. Most listings fail at the attribute step,
before ranking is even reached.

## The title formula

| Platform | Structure | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon.in | Brand + product + key searched words + variant + pack size | Category-dependent, keep under 200 characters, first 80 visible |
| Flipkart | Brand + product + key attribute + pack size | Shorter than Amazon; front-load the product |
| IndiaMART | Product + specification + capacity + material + certification | Long and technical is correct here |
| GeM | Exact catalogue name + specification as defined by the category | Follow the category template literally |

Rules that hold on all four:

- **The first three words carry the most weight.** Put the product, not the brand story.
- **No keyword stuffing.** Repeating "oil oil oil" gets a listing suppressed and reads as
  spam to the buyer.
- **One variant per title.** "500ml, 1L, 2L pack of 3" in one title is a variation problem
  disguised as a title.
- **No promotional words.** "Best", "sale", "free", "guaranteed" are policy risks.
- **No all caps**, no emoji, no seller name.

Worked example:

| | |
|---|---|
| Weak | "Pure Natural Cold Pressed Coconut Oil Best Quality For Hair Skin Cooking 100% Organic" |
| Strong | "Cold Pressed Coconut Oil 500 ml - Virgin, Unrefined, Glass Bottle, Single Origin Kerala" |

The strong title carries the searched phrase ("cold pressed coconut oil"), the variant
("500 ml"), the specification a buyer filters on ("virgin, unrefined"), and the trust
signal ("glass bottle, single origin"). No superlative, no repetition.

## The five bullets

Each bullet answers one objection, in this order:

| Bullet | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What it is, in one line, with the number | "500 ml virgin coconut oil, cold pressed below 40°C, unrefined" |
| 2 | The problem it solves | "Retains aroma and nutrients that refined oil loses in chemical processing" |
| 3 | The specification a buyer checks | "Glass bottle, food-grade, 12-month shelf life, FSSAI licensed" |
| 4 | The use case | "For cooking, hair and skin; solidifies below 24°C, which is normal" |
| 5 | What is in the box, and the guarantee | "1 × 500 ml bottle; replacement within 7 days for damage in transit" |

Bullet 4 is the one that prevents the review that says "it was solid, is it fake?" -
answering the objection before it becomes a return.

## The attribute sheet

Every platform exposes a different set. Fill all of them, including the optional ones.

| Attribute type | Examples | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Brand, model, SKU, pack size | Matching and variations |
| Physical | Dimensions, weight, material, colour | Filters and shipping class |
| Functional | Capacity, power, compatibility, grade | The filters buyers actually use |
| Compliance | Country of origin, licence number, net quantity | Suppression risk if missing |
| Commercial | MRP, tax rate, HSN, warranty | Pricing and invoicing |

A listing with 8 of 22 attributes filled appears in no filtered search. The buyer who
filters "500 ml" and "glass" never sees it.

## Backend keywords (Amazon and Flipkart)

- Use the words a buyer types that are not already in the title: synonyms, regional names,
  common misspellings, use cases.
- No brand names you do not own, no competitor terms, no repetition of title words.
- Refresh quarterly from the search-term report: the report tells you which queries
  actually reached the listing and which converted.

## The search-term report loop

| Report column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Customer search term | The actual words used |
| Impressions | Whether the listing was matched |
| Clicks | Whether the title and image worked |
| Conversion | Whether the listing and price worked |

Four diagnoses:

| Pattern | Diagnosis | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low impressions | Not indexed for the term | Title, attributes, backend keywords |
| Impressions, low clicks | Title or main image | Rewrite the first 80 characters, change the main image |
| Clicks, low conversion | Bullets, images, price, reviews | Fix the detail page, not the title |
| Conversion, high returns | Expectation mismatch | Fix the specification and the images, honestly |

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Cost |
|---|---|
| Title written as a brand slogan | The listing is never matched to a search |
| Half the attributes empty | Invisible in every filtered search |
| Five bullets of adjectives | The buyer's objection is unanswered and they leave |
| Keyword stuffing | Suppression, and a listing that reads as spam |
| Copy-pasting a competitor's bullets | Copyright risk and no differentiation |
| Never reading the search-term report | The same wrong title for a year |
