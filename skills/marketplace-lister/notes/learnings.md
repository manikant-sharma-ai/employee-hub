# Learnings - marketplace-lister

1. **Most listings are invisible at the attribute step, not the ranking step.** A listing with
   eight of twenty-two fields filled appears in no filtered search, and no title rewrite
   fixes that.
2. **The title is written for the search, the bullets for the objection.** Brand copy on a
   marketplace is read by nobody, because nobody reaches it.
3. **Diagnose from the report, in one order**: low impressions means indexing, impressions
   without clicks means title or main image, clicks without conversion means the detail page
   or the price. Never change all three at once.
4. **Price is landed cost plus fees plus a returns provision plus a margin.** A price copied
   from a competitor is a margin someone else chose, and the commission is usually the hole.
5. **Returns and RTO are a category number, not a company average.** Fashion at 30% and
   industrial at 2% cannot share one provision.
6. **Compliance is checked before publishing, with the certificate in hand.** "The supplier
   says it is certified" is the sentence that precedes a delisting.
7. **Contact details in an image is the commonest rejection**, and the cheapest to avoid.
8. **One platform, one listing, one buyer.** The same product on IndiaMART and Amazon needs
   two titles, two price points and two compliance positions.
9. **A listing is never done.** 72 hours, 14 days, then quarterly against the search-term
   report.
