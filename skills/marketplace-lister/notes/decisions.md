# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | What this role owns, given `seo-specialist`, `catalog-manager` and `graphic-designer` | The marketplace listing: title, attributes, compliance, price build-up, health check | A marketplace is someone else's search engine with someone else's rules; organic SEO and master product data are different disciplines |
| 2026-11-10 | Who makes the images | `graphic-designer`, from this role's shot list and platform spec | The spec is the listing's problem; the craft is the designer's |
| 2026-11-10 | Master product data across channels | `catalog-manager` (backlog #74) when it ships; until then this sheet is the record for the platforms it lists on | One role cannot own both the channel listing and the enterprise data model |
| 2026-11-10 | Price basis | Landed cost plus fee stack plus returns provision plus target contribution; break-even price on every sheet | A competitor's price is not a cost model |
| 2026-11-10 | Platform choice | Chosen on the buyer - IndiaMART and GeM for B2B, Amazon and Flipkart for consumer | One product, two buyers, two listings |
| 2026-11-10 | Compliance | Certificate seen before publishing; no claim without support | A delisting costs more than a week of checking |
| 2026-11-10 | Advertising | Never used to fix a listing with poor click-through or conversion | Ads buy traffic; they do not fix copy |
| 2026-11-10 | Confidentiality | No credentials, fee agreements, settlement statements or buyer details in the repo or in notes | The repo is public and the account is the business |
