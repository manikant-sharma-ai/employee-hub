# Risk register and escalation

Compliance risk is not a feeling about the law; it is exposure with a number and a
date. This file turns a gap into a decision the founder can take.

## The two axes

| Axis | Levels | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Likelihood | Low / Medium / High | How likely is this to be found in the next twelve months? Inspection drive, a portal mismatch, an employee complaint, a bank query |
| Impact | Low / Medium / High | Penalty plus interest plus the cost of the fix plus the operational stop |

| | Impact low | Impact medium | Impact high |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Likelihood high** | fix this month | fix this week | fix now, tell the founder today |
| **Likelihood medium** | schedule | fix this month | fix this week |
| **Likelihood low** | accept, record | schedule | decide with the CA |

Every cell resolves to one of four actions: fix now, fix this week, fix this month,
or accept and record. There is no fifth option called "hope".

## Putting a number on exposure

For each open gap, write four lines:

1. **The penalty, as a range.** The statute's range, not the maximum; most
   adjudications land well below the ceiling, and quoting the ceiling ends the
   conversation.
2. **Interest or late fee, per month of delay.** This is usually the larger number
   and the one that grows while everyone debates.
3. **The cost of the fix** - the professional's fee, the rework, the registration.
4. **The operational stop** - what cannot happen while this is open: an invoice
   cannot be raised, a shipment cannot move, a licence cannot be renewed.

Example shape, not a rate: "late filing of a monthly return attracts a per-day
late fee and interest on the tax, both of which accrue until filed; at three
months the late fee alone is likely to exceed the professional's fee for a year of
filing. Fix this month." Verify the actual rates at source before quoting them.

## Severity levels for escalation

| Level | What it is | Who is told | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Search, seizure, arrest, prosecution notice, licence suspension, a demand above the business's monthly revenue | Founder, lawyer, CA, same hour | Immediately, by phone, not email |
| S2 | Show-cause notice, a large mismatch, a missed statutory payment, a lapsed licence in active use | Founder and CA, same day | Same day |
| S3 | Late filing, a missing acknowledgement, an approaching threshold, a new state obligation | Founder, in the weekly review | This week |
| S4 | A change that does not apply, a documentation gap with no exposure yet | Register only | This month |

S1 and S2 are never handled in a chat thread with three people guessing. Name the
professional, name the deadline, and stop.

## The decision that belongs to the founder

Some gaps are a choice, not an oversight: a registration that costs more than the
exposure, a small vendor TDS that is administratively expensive, a compliance
calendar that assumes a CA who is slow. Put it in writing:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| The gap | In one sentence |
| Why it exists | Cost, capacity, ignorance, or a deliberate trade-off |
| Exposure if found | Range, likelihood, timeline |
| The fix and its cost | Money and hours |
| Recommendation | Fix, phase, or accept |
| Decision, by whom, on what date | The founder, in writing |

A recorded decision to accept a risk is defensible. An unrecorded one is just a
gap that will be discovered by someone less sympathetic.

## Anti-patterns

- **The maximum penalty as the estimate.** It ends the conversation instead of informing it.
- **The single severity.** If everything is urgent, nothing is, and the founder stops reading.
- **Risk without a date.** "We should register eventually" is not a register entry.
- **Escalating to a group chat.** S1 and S2 go to named people, by phone, with the document attached.
- **The vendor's assurance as closure.** "They said it is handled" is not evidence; the acknowledgement is.
- **Fixing quietly and not recording it.** The next person repeats the analysis, and the pattern is never learned.
