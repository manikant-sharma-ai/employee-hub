# The obligation register

The register is the whole job in one sheet. Everything else - the calendar, the
evidence folder, the change log - is derived from it.

## The five profile lines

Every row in the register exists because of one of these. If a line is wrong, the
register is wrong, and the errors are expensive ones.

| Line | Values that matter | What it switches on |
|---|---|---|
| Entity | Private limited, LLP, partnership, proprietorship | ROC filings, audit, board meetings, DIN KYC |
| Location | Each state and each premises, separately | Shop and Establishment, professional tax, labour welfare, stamp duty |
| Headcount | Total including contract staff, and the contract headcount separately | PF, ESI, POSH committee, gratuity, contract labour licensing |
| Turnover | Last year and projected, per entity | GST registration, e-invoicing, tax audit, CSR, internal audit |
| Activity | Goods or services, online sales, imports, exports, food, drugs, packaging | Sector licences, IEC, FSSAI, legal metrology, EPR |

Record the date each line was verified and the source. A register built on
last year's headcount is a register of last year's obligations.

## The register columns

| Column | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Obligation | The plain name: "monthly GST return (GSTR-3B)" |
| Trigger | The threshold or event: "turnover above Rs 40 lakh, goods" |
| Frequency | Monthly, quarterly, annual, event-based, one-time |
| Form / instrument | GSTR-3B, 24Q, AOC-4, Form 5, licence renewal |
| Authority | CBIC, MCA, EPFO, ESIC, state labour, FSSAI, MeitY |
| Due | The statutory date, and the work-back date |
| Evidence | Challan, ARN, acknowledgement, certificate, register extract |
| Owner | One named person |
| Consequence | Interest rate, penalty, cancellation, tender ineligibility |
| Last verified | Date, and who checked it |

## The standard set, for a small Indian private limited

Use this as a starting skeleton and then delete what does not apply - never ship
it whole. Thresholds must be checked against the current rule before they go to
a client.

| Area | Typical obligations |
|---|---|
| GST | Registration per state, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, annual GSTR-9, e-invoice above threshold, e-way bill, ITC reconciliation |
| Income tax | Advance tax quarterly, TDS deduction and deposit, 24Q/26Q quarterly returns, Form 16/16A, ITR, tax audit where applicable |
| ROC | AOC-4 and MGT-7 annually, DIR-3 KYC for each director, board meetings, statutory registers, auditor appointment |
| Payroll statutory | PF registration and monthly ECR, ESI registration and contribution, professional tax per state, labour welfare fund |
| Employment | Appointment letters, wage register, attendance and leave records, POSH policy and internal committee, gratuity |
| Premises | Shop and Establishment registration and renewal, trade licence, fire NOC, signage permission |
| Sector | FSSAI, legal metrology, drugs licence, BIS, pollution consent, factory licence, EPR registration for packaging |
| Cross-border | IEC, AD code, shipping bill process, FEMA reporting for inward remittance |
| Data | DPDP obligations: notice, consent records, grievance officer, breach process, retention |
| MSME | Udyam registration, 45-day payment discipline to micro and small suppliers |

## Reading the register back

Once a quarter, ask three questions of the sheet:

1. **Which rows have no owner?** Those are the ones that will be late.
2. **Which rows have no evidence column filled?** Those are the ones that will be argued about.
3. **Which rows have a trigger that has changed?** Headcount crossed a line, turnover crossed a line, a new state, a new product. Those are the new obligations, and they arrive without a reminder.

## Anti-patterns

- **The internet checklist.** Forty obligations, half of them inapplicable, and the three that matter buried.
- **Headcount without contract staff.** The threshold that surprises most firms.
- **One row per act instead of one row per filing.** "GST" is not an obligation; GSTR-3B on the 20th is.
- **A register with no consequences.** Everything on it looks equally urgent, so nothing is.
- **A register nobody updates.** The date of the last review belongs at the top of the sheet, in large type.
