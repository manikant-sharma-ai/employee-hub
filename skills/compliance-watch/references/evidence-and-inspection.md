# Evidence and inspection readiness

An inspector does not ask whether the business is compliant. They ask for a
document, and the answer is either a folder or an afternoon of searching.

## The evidence folder

One folder per authority, one file per obligation per period, named so it sorts:

```
/compliance
  /gst/2026-10/GSTR-3B_2026-10_ARN-AA123456789.pdf
  /gst/2026-10/challan_2026-10_CPIN-1234.pdf
  /tds/2026-Q2/24Q_2026-Q2_acknowledgement.pdf
  /pf/2026-10/ECR_2026-10_challan.pdf
  /roc/2026/AOC-4_FY2025-26_SRN.pdf
  /licences/shop-establishment_Karnataka_expiry-2028-03-31.pdf
  /registers/wage-register_2026-10.xlsx
```

Three rules: the period is in the filename, the acknowledgement is stored next to
the return, and nothing lives only in an inbox.

## What inspectors ask for, by inspection type

| Inspection | Usually asked for |
|---|---|
| GST | Registration certificate, returns and challans, invoice series, e-way bills, ITC register, stock statement |
| PF / ESI | Registration, monthly ECR and challans, wage register, attendance, appointment letters, contractor list |
| Labour / shops | Shop and Establishment certificate, wage and leave registers, working hours record, employee list |
| Income tax | TDS returns and certificates, books of account, ledger, bank statements, fixed asset register |
| Food safety | FSSAI licence, water test report, pest control record, temperature logs, medical certificates of handlers |
| Factory / pollution | Factory licence, consent to operate, safety register, accident register, stack and effluent monitoring reports |
| Data protection | Privacy notice, consent records, grievance officer details, breach log, processor agreements |

## The readiness pack

Keep one folder that answers 80% of any inspection in under five minutes:

1. **Entity pack** - incorporation, PAN, TAN, GST registration per state, Udyam, IEC, bank details letter.
2. **People pack** - employee list with join dates, appointment letters, PF and ESI registration and latest challans, wage register.
3. **Money pack** - last 12 months of GST returns with challans, TDS returns with certificates, audited accounts or the ITR.
4. **Premises pack** - shop registration, trade licence, fire NOC, lease or ownership proof, and each certificate's expiry.
5. **Process pack** - the registers the law requires, current as of last month, with the person who maintains each.

## During an inspection

- **Ask for the authority and the purpose**, politely, and write both down.
- **Produce what is asked, not everything.** A folder dumped on a table invites wider questions.
- **Never hand over originals** without a receipt; offer copies.
- **Never estimate a number** to an inspector. "I will confirm and send it today" is always better.
- **Record every document shared**, the date, and the officer's name.
- **Escalate immediately** if a statement is being recorded, a seizure is proposed, or a penalty is mentioned.

## After an inspection

Write a note the same day: what was asked, what was produced, what was missing,
what was said, and what the next step is. That note is the only reliable record
three months later, and it goes to the founder and the CA unedited.

## Anti-patterns

- **Evidence in an inbox.** The filing exists; the proof is buried in a thread.
- **Expired certificates nobody noticed.** The expiry column in the register is the fix.
- **A readiness pack built during the inspection.** It is built in a quiet month.
- **Unsigned registers.** A wage register without signatures is a draft.
- **Handing over more than asked.** It converts a routine visit into an inquiry.
