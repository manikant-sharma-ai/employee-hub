# Evidence and inspection

The work is invisible; the evidence is what survives. This file is the difference
between "we filed it" and "here it is".

## The folder, one structure, no exceptions

```
compliance/
  <obligation>/
    <YYYY-MM or YYYY-YY>/
      01-return-filed.pdf
      02-acknowledgement.pdf
      03-challan-or-payment-reference.pdf
      04-working-paper.xlsx
      05-correspondence.pdf
```

Numbered prefixes so the folder sorts into the order an inspector reads it. One
obligation, one period, one folder - never a folder called "misc".

## What counts as evidence

| Claim | The evidence that proves it |
|---|---|
| The return was filed | The acknowledgement with the ARN or reference number and the date |
| The tax was paid | The challan with the CIN, the amount and the date, matched to the return |
| The employee was paid correctly | The payslip, the bank statement line and the PF and ESI challan for the same month |
| The policy exists | The signed and dated policy, and the board or partner resolution adopting it |
| The notice was received | The notice itself, with the date and mode of receipt, and the envelope or portal screenshot |
| The notice was answered | The reply, the acknowledgement of submission, and the date |

A screenshot of a portal dashboard is not evidence on its own; the downloaded
acknowledgement is.

## Retention, as a working rule

Keep the statutory records for the period the law requires, and when in doubt keep
longer than the shortest rule you can find. The working rule most small businesses
should adopt:

| Record | Working rule |
|---|---|
| GST returns and challans | Eight years from the due date of the annual return for the year |
| TDS statements and certificates | Eight years |
| Books of account and vouchers | Eight years, and longer where a dispute is open |
| Payroll, PF and ESI records | Eight years, and for the service period of any employee with an open gratuity question |
| ROC filings and statutory registers | Permanent - the registers are corporate memory |
| Contracts and property documents | Permanent, plus the limitation period after the end of the relationship |
| Notices and replies | Permanent, in a separate folder |

Verify the current rule at source before quoting a number to a founder; the table
is a working default, not a legal opinion.

## The inspection pack, ten minutes

One index page, kept current, listing:

1. Entity documents: incorporation, PAN, TAN, GST registration certificates per state, shop registration, sector licences.
2. The obligation register with the current profile table.
3. The last twelve months of returns and challans, by obligation.
4. Payroll and statutory contribution records for the same period.
5. Notices and replies, in date order.
6. The board and partner resolutions for the policies that need adoption.
7. The named people: who files what, and who holds which credential - names only.

## When a notice arrives

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Log it: date received, authority, reference, the demand or the question, the reply deadline. Do not reply today. |
| 1 | Send it to the CA or the lawyer with the full file for that obligation and period |
| 2 | Establish the facts: was it filed, was it paid, is the demand arithmetic or allegation |
| 3 | Decide the response: pay, contest, or seek an adjournment - with the professional, in writing |
| Before deadline | Reply filed, acknowledgement saved, calendar entry for the next step |

The single most expensive habit is replying to a notice the same day, from memory,
without the file.

## Anti-patterns

- **The shoebox.** Everything in one Drive folder named after the year, sorted by nothing.
- **The vendor-held evidence.** The payroll vendor has the challans; the business has a promise. Export a copy every quarter.
- **The photographed document.** A photo of a signed page is a copy of a copy; keep the PDF the signer sent.
- **The missing acknowledgement.** The commonest gap in any inspection, and the easiest to close on the day.
- **Evidence collected in March** for a year that ended in March. The two documents that were never saved are the two that are asked for.
- **The index nobody updates.** An index that is right in April and stale in December is a trap for the person who trusts it.
