# Change watching

Rules change quietly. The business finds out from a notice, a penalty or a
consultant's invoice three months later. This is the discipline that closes that
gap.

## The sources that count

| Area | Primary source |
|---|---|
| GST and customs | CBIC site: notifications, circulars, press releases |
| Corporate and LLP | MCA: notifications, circulars, e-forms updates |
| Income tax and TDS | Income tax department: notifications, circulars, press releases |
| PF and ESI | EPFO and ESIC circulars |
| Data protection | MeitY: DPDP Act rules and notifications |
| Food, packaging, weights | FSSAI, CPCB (EPR), legal metrology department |
| Labour and state taxes | State labour department, state commercial tax portal, state gazette |
| Sector regulators | RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, state pollution board, as applicable |

A news article, a LinkedIn post or a consultant's newsletter is a **lead**. It
becomes a finding only when the notification or circular is read.

## The monthly pass, 30 minutes

1. Open each source for the areas in the register. Not all of them - the ones that apply.
2. Scan the titles for the last 30 days. Most will be irrelevant; that is expected.
3. For each candidate, answer four questions:

| Question | If the answer is no |
|---|---|
| Is it a final rule, or a draft for comment? | Note it, do not act |
| When does it take effect? | Put the date in the calendar with a work-back |
| Does it apply to this entity, state, turnover and headcount? | Close it, with one line on why |
| What changes in the register? | Update the row, the form, the evidence |

4. Write one line per applicable change in the journal, and one line per
   significant inapplicable change too - the second saves the next review from
   re-deriving it.

## Reading a notification in five minutes

| Part | What to extract |
|---|---|
| Number and date | The citation you will quote later |
| Effective date | Often not the publication date |
| Scope | Which entities, which states, which thresholds |
| The operative change | Rate, form, date, threshold, procedure |
| Transition | What happens to periods already filed or pending |

## The quarterly note

One page, sent to the founder, whatever the answer:

- **Changed and applies** - what, effective when, what we do, owner, date.
- **Changed and does not apply** - one line each, so it is not re-litigated.
- **Under consultation** - draft rules worth watching, and the likely date.
- **No change** - say so plainly. A quiet quarter is information.

## Anti-patterns

- **Watching everything.** Twelve regulators, no attention. Watch the register's areas only.
- **Acting on the headline.** "Government makes e-invoicing mandatory" without the threshold is a wasted panic.
- **Ignoring the effective date.** A rule published in September may bite in April.
- **No record of inapplicable changes.** The same circular gets re-read and re-argued every quarter.
- **Treating the consultant's silence as confirmation.** Silence means nobody looked.
