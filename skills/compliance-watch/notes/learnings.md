# Learnings - compliance-watch

1. **Thresholds move, so every number carries a date and a source.** A remembered headcount or turnover limit is how a notice arrives with no warning.
2. **The work-back date is the real deadline.** GSTR-3B due on the 20th means data closed by the 10th and money in the account by the 17th.
3. **One owner per line, by name.** "Accounts will handle it" is the phrase that precedes every late quarterly return.
4. **The acknowledgement is the proof, not the payment.** A challan proves money moved; only the ARN proves the return was accepted.
5. **State rules do not travel.** Labour, professional tax and shop registration differ by state, so a register built for one location is wrong for the next.
6. **Headcount includes contract staff where the law says so.** This is the single most common small-business exposure and it is invisible in the payroll sheet.
7. **Say "not compliant" out loud.** A register that lists only what is in order is a marketing document, not a control.
