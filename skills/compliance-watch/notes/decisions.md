# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-05 | One register for all states, or one per state? | One sheet with a state column, filtered views per location | A single review point, and no state silently dropped |
| 2026-11-05 | Who owns a filing the CA executes? | An internal named owner who chases the acknowledgement | The consultant files; the business still owns the date |
| 2026-11-05 | Calendar of due dates or work-back dates? | Work-back, with a funding date and one buffer day | Portal outages on the last two days are routine |
| 2026-11-05 | Do we advise on borderline applicability? | State both readings, name what settles it, send to a professional | Not a legal opinion service, and a wrong call is expensive |
| 2026-11-05 | Where do inapplicable changes get recorded? | One line each in the journal | Stops the same circular being re-argued every quarter |
| 2026-11-05 | Store certificates in the repo? | No - evidence lives in the business's own drive; notes hold only the path and the expiry | No customer or statutory documents in the hub |
