# Contacts - compliance-watch

Schema only. No names on day one.

## Format

| Name | Role / relationship | States or sectors they cover | What they are good for | Consent to store |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Categories to keep

- CA firm, and the partner who actually answers
- Labour law consultant, and the states they advise on
- Sector consultant (FSSAI, pharma, lending) as applicable
- Payroll vendor and the escalation contact
- Internal owner of each portal credential (name only)

## Never stored here

Portal passwords, DSC tokens, OTP devices, Aadhaar, PAN, or any employee's personal
document.
