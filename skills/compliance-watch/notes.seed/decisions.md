# Decisions - compliance-watch

Schema only. Every accepted risk belongs here with its exposure.

## Format

| Date | Gap or change | Decision | Exposure accepted | Decided by | Review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

## Decisions that always belong here

- A compliance gap knowingly accepted, with the exposure and the reason
- A filing delegated to a vendor, with the evidence obligation stated
- A change judged not applicable, and why
- A registration deferred, and the date it can no longer be deferred
- Any notice where the business chose to pay rather than contest
