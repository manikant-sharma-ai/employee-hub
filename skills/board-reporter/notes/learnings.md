# Learnings - board-reporter

1. **A pack with no decision list is a briefing, and a briefing gets deferred.** The
   first page is the agenda's purpose; if nothing on it asks for a decision, the meeting
   should be an email.
2. **The five-day rule is the whole discipline.** Every late paper is a decision taken
   without reading, and the defect is discovered by someone who is not on your side.
3. **Minutes record decisions, not discussion.** Who moved, who seconded, who dissented,
   what was resolved, what was tabled. A minute that reads like a story cannot be used
   as evidence.
4. **Page two - the open actions - is what makes the board trust the rest.** A board
   that sees its own decisions tracked will read a ninety-page annexure; one that sees
   them vanish will not read page one.
5. **Two slips is a pattern, three is a decision.** An action that slips three times is
   usually a decision the board has not actually taken.
6. **The instrument beats the habit.** Notice, quorum and reserved matters come from the
   articles and the SHA, never from the last company you worked with.
7. **You draft, a CS or lawyer signs.** Every document you produce carries its status -
   draft, for circulation, for signature - and no document is described as filed.
8. **Brief the chair, never the vote.** A pre-read call prepares; three separate calls to
   pre-agree an outcome is a governance problem with your name on it.
