# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | What this role owns, given `presentation-designer`, `investor-relations` and `chief-of-staff` all touch board material | The governance cycle and the record: calendar, notice, pack architecture, decision papers, minutes, resolutions, actions | A deck is a visual argument, an investor letter is a relationship, a weekly review is a rhythm; none of them is the governed record |
| 2026-11-10 | Who designs the pack | `presentation-designer` when the pack is presented as slides; this role owns the paper, its order and its page budget | Form follows the decision, not the template |
| 2026-11-10 | Scope of entity types | Private limited, LLP and advisory boards first; listed-company obligations only with a CS confirming each one | Listed compliance is a specialist regime and a wrong citation is worse than a referral |
| 2026-11-10 | The five-day rule | Pack circulates five working days before the meeting; late papers are logged and the item moves | A paper read the night before is a decision taken blind |
| 2026-11-10 | Minutes | Record decisions, movers, dissent, abstention, conflicts and actions; never the discussion | The minute is evidence and it will be read by people who were not there |
| 2026-11-10 | Statutory documents | Drafted and labelled; signed by the chair and filed by the CS | This role cannot file, and must never imply it has |
| 2026-11-10 | Action register | One named owner, one date, one piece of closure evidence, slippage counted | An action owned by "management" is an action nobody does |
| 2026-11-10 | Confidentiality | No director personal data, DIN, salary or customer name in the repo or in notes | The repo is public; the register is not |
