# Minutes and actions

Minutes are a legal record and an operating tool at the same time. They are read
by the auditor, the lender, a future director and, in a dispute, a court.

## What a minute contains

For each agenda item:

1. **The item** as it appears on the agenda, with its number.
2. **The information put before the board** - the paper's title and date, in one line. Not a summary of the discussion.
3. **The decision**, in operative words: "RESOLVED that ..." or "The board noted ..." or "The board deferred the decision to the meeting of ... pending ...".
4. **The reasoning**, in one or two lines, only where it matters for a later reader.
5. **The owner and the date** for anything that follows from it.
6. **Dissent**, if a director asked for it to be recorded, attributed as they asked.

What a minute does not contain: who said what in what order, adjectives about the
quality of the discussion, or a narrative of the argument. If the argument
produced a decision, the decision is the record.

## The three verbs, used precisely

- **Approved / resolved** - the board has decided and the company may act.
- **Noted** - the board has received the information. It is not approval, and it must never be written where approval was needed.
- **Deferred** - the decision is not taken, the reason is stated, and the date it returns is fixed.

Using "noted" for something that needed approval, or "approved" for something that
was merely discussed, is the failure that makes minutes worthless.

## The action register

One sheet, carried forever, never rebuilt per meeting.

| ID | Action | Owner | Opened | Due | Status | Closed | Meetings open |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-011 | Appoint statutory auditor for FY27 | founder | 2026-05-14 | 2026-08-31 | open | - | 3 |

Rules:

- **One owner, one date.** "The team" is not an owner and "ASAP" is not a date.
- **The register opens the meeting.** Read the open items out before the financials; it takes four minutes and it is the only thing that makes actions close.
- **Three meetings open is a decision, not a delay.** The board either takes the action itself, re-scopes it, or records that it has decided not to do it.
- **Closing an action needs evidence**, not an assurance. "Done - auditor appointed, engagement letter dated 2 Sep" is closed. "In progress" three times is not.
- **Carry the ID.** An action that changes owner keeps its ID so the history stays readable.

## The resolution log

Separate from the minutes, a running one-line-per-decision log:

| Date | Resolution | Effect | Reversible by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-14 | Approved ₹40 lakh working-capital facility with HDFC | Company may draw to ₹40 lakh | Board resolution |

A new director reads the log, not four years of minutes. It is also the first
thing a diligence team asks for.
