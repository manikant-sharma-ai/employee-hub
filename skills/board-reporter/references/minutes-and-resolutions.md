# Minutes and resolutions

Minutes are evidence. They are read years later, by a bank, an auditor, a buyer doing
diligence or a court, and none of those readers care what the meeting felt like.

## The minute structure

```
<Entity> - Board Meeting
Date, time, venue or video link
Present: <name> (DIN, category), ...
In attendance: <CS, CFO, auditor - by invitation for item 4>
Leave of absence: <name>
Quorum: <n> of <total>, present at commencement
1. Notice and quorum - notice dated <date> issued to all directors; quorum confirmed.
2. Confirmation of previous minutes - minutes of <date> confirmed / confirmed with
   amendments <text>.
3. Matters arising and open actions - <register reference>, items 2, 5 open.
4. <Item title>
   Conflict: <director> declared interest in item 4 under s.184 and did not vote.
   Discussion: <three to five sentences - the substance, not the transcript>
   RESOLVED THAT <operative words>.
   Moved by <name>, seconded by <name>. <n> in favour, <n> against, <n> abstained.
   Action: <owner> to <do the thing> by <date>.
...
<n>. Meeting closed at <time>.
Signed: <chair>            Date:
```

## The operative words

| Situation | Wording |
|---|---|
| Approval | "RESOLVED THAT the proposal for <specific thing> at a cost not exceeding Rs <amount> be and is hereby approved." |
| Authority | "RESOLVED THAT <name>, <designation>, be and is hereby authorised to execute <document> and to do all such acts as may be necessary." |
| Appointment | "RESOLVED THAT <name> be and is hereby appointed as <role> with effect from <date>, on the terms set out in the paper circulated." |
| Noting | "NOTED the <report> for the quarter ended <date>, circulated with the agenda." |
| Ratification | "RESOLVED THAT the action taken by <name> in <thing>, as recorded in the paper dated <date>, be and is hereby ratified." |
| Bank mandate | "RESOLVED THAT the <bank> be and is hereby authorised to honour instructions signed by any two of <names>." |

Rules that hold in every wording: name the specific thing, cap the amount, name the
person by designation as well as name, and put a date on the effect.

## What goes in, and what does not

| Belongs in the minute | Does not belong |
|---|---|
| The decision, in operative language | The full discussion, in order |
| Who moved, who seconded, the vote | Who said what, unless a director asks for it |
| Dissent and abstention, recorded plainly | Characterisations of a director's motive |
| Conflicts declared, and the item they touch | The contents of privileged advice |
| Actions with owner and date | "The board discussed various matters" |
| Papers tabled and their reference | Numbers repeated from the pack |
| Time quorum was lost, if it was | Anything not said in the meeting |

## A director asks for their dissent recorded

Record it in their words if they give you words, otherwise: "Mr X recorded his dissent
on item 6 on the ground that <stated ground>." Then stop. Do not argue, do not
summarise it away, and do not add your view.

## Circular resolutions

For a decision between meetings, when the instrument allows it.

- Draft the resolution in the same operative language.
- Circulate to every director entitled to vote, with the paper.
- Record each signature with date and time; the resolution passes on the date the
  required majority signs.
- Enter it in the minute book and table it for noting at the next meeting.
- Never use it for a related-party item, a matter the articles reserve to a meeting, or
  anything that needs a discussion on the record.

## The minute book and the resolution register

| Register | Fields |
|---|---|
| Minute book | Meeting type, date, attendees, decisions, signed by the chair |
| Resolution register | Number, date, subject, operative summary, form filed, annexure |
| Director register | Name, DIN, category, appointment, cessation, other directorships |
| Interest register | Director, entity, nature of interest, date disclosed, items affected |

Number resolutions consecutively per financial year. A resolution with no number is a
resolution that cannot be found.

## Timeline

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Draft minutes to the chair | Within 3 working days |
| Comments closed, final circulated | Within 7 working days |
| Signed by the chair | Before the next meeting, ideally |
| Entered in the minute book | Same week as signing |
| Statutory forms prepared | Within the statutory window; the CS files |

## Common drafting errors

| Error | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| "The board approved the proposal" with no amount | Unenforceable authority; the bank asks for the cap |
| Resolution names a person without designation | Ambiguous when the person changes role |
| No record of a declared interest | The related-party transaction is challengeable |
| Action with no owner or date | It will reappear, unresolved, next quarter |
| Minutes rewritten months later | An altered record is worse than a thin one; use an addendum |
| Vote count omitted | Unanimity cannot be proved when it is questioned |
| Circular resolution for a meeting-only matter | Defect that surfaces in diligence |
