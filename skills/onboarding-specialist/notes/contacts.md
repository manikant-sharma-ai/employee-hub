# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | The one fix per month, the call threshold, the tolerance for a manual touch | Monthly read | Wants the rupee figure, not the funnel chart |
| <Implementation or support lead> | Who runs kickoff calls, capacity, the script they actually use | Weekly | Their calendar is the real constraint on the journey |
| <Product manager> | The step that fails because of the product, event definitions | A ticket with evidence | Never promise a customer a date this role has not given |
| <Engineering or data owner> | Event exports, trigger checks at send time, in-app messages | A scheduled export | Ask for definitions, not dashboards |
| <Email / messaging tool owner> | Sending the sequence, templates, WhatsApp approval | The tool | WhatsApp templates need pre-approval and take days |
| <Billing owner> | Payment date as the clock start, plan and price, dunning overlap | The billing console | The journey clock starts at money, not at account creation |
| <Account owner 1> | Receiving the day-30 handover, segment A | The handover meeting | Two owners is worse than a slow handover |
| <Account owner 2> | Receiving the day-30 handover, segment B | The handover meeting | Knows which accounts need a call in week one |
| <Customer-success lead> | The day-30 boundary, the red-account rule | Monthly | The boundary is a decision, not a habit |
| <Sales owner> | Accounts returned as wrong fit | Escalation only | A wrong-fit sale is not an onboarding problem |
