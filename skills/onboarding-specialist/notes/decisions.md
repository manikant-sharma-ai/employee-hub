# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `customer-success` | The first 30 days and the activation score; the account relationship from day 30 stays theirs | One is a journey, the other is a relationship |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `hiring-manager` | Customer activation only; employee onboarding, 30-60-90s and appraisals are theirs | Same word, different person, different craft |
| 2026-11-18 | When onboarding starts | At payment captured, not at signup or trial start | The paid cohort is the one the business is accountable to |
| 2026-11-18 | The activation milestone | One primary event with a count, a window and exclusions, versioned and re-tested quarterly | A milestone without a threshold drifts into "seems engaged" |
| 2026-11-18 | Nudge triggers | Behaviour-checked at send time, never a pre-scheduled calendar | The customer who already did the step must not be asked again |
| 2026-11-18 | Sequence length | Ends on activation, reply, opt-out or day 30, and the stop rule is stated in the first message | A hidden stop rule destroys trust when it fires anyway |
| 2026-11-18 | Who may contact a customer | The account owner or the founder sends; this role drafts and names the sender | Two employees contacting one customer is how a journey becomes noise |
| 2026-11-18 | Kickoff calls | Offered above a contract-value threshold or where setup needs a customer decision | A mandatory call on a low-value self-serve plan is a cost centre |
| 2026-11-18 | Red accounts at day 30 | Handed over as red with a first contact inside 48 hours, never extended silently | A 60-day onboarding is an owner problem |
| 2026-11-18 | Personal data | Account IDs, plan and segment only in reports, notes and the repo | DPDP Act obligations and a public repo |
