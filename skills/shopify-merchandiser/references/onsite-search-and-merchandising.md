# On-site search and product-page merchandising

Site search is the only place a shopper tells you, in their own words, what they came to
buy. It is also where most small stores lose the sale silently.

## Read the report first

From Shopify analytics, GA4 or the search app, pull 90 days:

- **Top 100 search terms** by volume - the demand you actually have.
- **Search terms with zero results** - the demand you are refusing.
- **Search terms with results but no click** - the demand you are answering badly.
- **Search exit rate** - shoppers who searched and left are the most expensive loss in
  the funnel, because they told you what they wanted.

## Fixing zero results, in this order

1. **Synonym or misspelling** - "kurti" for "kurta", "chappal" for "slipper", "trolley
   bag" for "cabin luggage". Add the synonym; it is a five-minute fix with a direct
   revenue effect.
2. **A word you do not stock** - do not add a synonym to an unrelated product. Show a
   closest-match collection and a "notify me" if it is a real gap in the range.
3. **A phrase the catalogue does not carry** - "gift under 1000" is a collection you do
   not have. Build it.
4. **A brand or model you do not sell** - answer honestly and offer the alternative.

## Synonyms and the vocabulary sheet

Keep one sheet: the shopper's word, the catalogue's word, the mapping. It is the highest
return-per-minute document in the shop.

| Shopper types | Catalogue says | Map to |
|---|---|---|
| kurti, kurtis | kurta set | both, with the kurta set first |
| joggers, track pants | activewear bottom | both |
| purse, handbag, bag | sling bag | the sling collection |
| gift, gifting, present | - | the gift collection |

## The product page, in decision order

A shopper decides in a sequence; the page should answer in that sequence.

1. **Is this the thing I want?** - one clear hero image, the product in use, the scale
   reference.
2. **Will it fit / work for me?** - size guide, compatibility, dimensions, material, the
   answer to the top support question.
3. **When will it arrive?** - the delivery date for the shopper's pincode, not "5-7 days".
4. **Can I trust this?** - reviews where the doubt is, the return policy in one line, the
   payment options including COD if offered.
5. **What else do I need?** - one add-on, priced, one click.

Everything else - the brand story, the care instructions, the long description - goes
below, where the shopper who wants it will scroll.

## Recommendations

- **Related products** - same collection, similar price, complementary, not the same item
  in another colour.
- **Frequently bought together** - from real orders, not from a guess.
- **Recently viewed** - useful, cheap, and never a substitute for the two above.

A recommendation block with a 1% click rate is costing page weight and attention. Measure
it or remove it.

## The weekly merchandising review

One page, same day each week:

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Sessions and conversion by collection | where the traffic is wasted |
| AOV and units per order | whether the offer work is landing |
| Top 10 search terms and zero-result share | the cheapest next fix |
| Stock-outs on the top 20 SKUs | a sold-out hero is a broken promise |
| Gross profit per session | the only number that pays for the traffic |

## Anti-patterns

- **Search left on the default relevance setting** with no synonyms.
- **A "no results" page that says "no results"** and offers nothing.
- **Filters built from the catalogue's attributes** rather than the shopper's vocabulary.
- **A hero banner that pushes a product with two weeks of stock.**
- **Ten recommendations per page**, which is the same as none.
