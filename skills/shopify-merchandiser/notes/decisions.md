# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | What this role owns, given `marketplace-lister`, `seo-specialist` and `pricing-analyst` | The storefront surface: catalogue structure, product pages, offers, calendar, on-site search | The marketplace role sells where buyers already search; this one decides what the shop itself shows |
| 2026-11-12 | Offer discipline | Every offer has a margin floor, a cap and an end date, written before launch | An open-ended discount becomes the price |
| 2026-11-12 | Stacking | Priced on the worst-case basket, with the cap recorded here | Codes and automatic discounts combine by default |
| 2026-11-12 | Clearance placement | Its own collection, never the hero page | A clearance badge on the hero resets the price reference |
| 2026-11-12 | Price setting | `pricing-analyst` and the founder; this role builds offers inside that frame | A merchandiser who sets list price argues for the sale, not the margin |
| 2026-11-12 | Publishing | Specified, built and live are three different statuses, always stated | A change described as live that was never published destroys trust in every later report |
| 2026-11-12 | Testing | One variable, a written baseline, checks at 7 and 28 days, and a note of what else moved | Small-store results are easy to fake and hard to defend |
| 2026-11-12 | Confidentiality | No customer data, order exports or gateway credentials in the repo or notes | The repo is public; aggregates and product decisions only |
