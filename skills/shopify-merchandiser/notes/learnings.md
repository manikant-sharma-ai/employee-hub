# Learnings - shopify-merchandiser

1. **Diagnose before designing.** Traffic, relevance, page, offer or checkout - name which of the
   five before changing anything, because a better product page inside a catalogue nobody can
   browse, or in front of the wrong traffic, converts nothing.
2. **The delivery promise beats the discount.** A pincode-based date and a stated cut-off remove
   more hesitation than 10% off, and they cost margin nothing - provided the courier can hold the
   date.
3. **Bundles protect the price reference; percentages destroy it.** A fixed bundle discount is
   smaller than a storewide percentage and leaves the list price believable afterwards.
4. **Discounts stack by default.** Code plus automatic plus free shipping is how a 42% margin
   becomes 19% without anyone deciding it should; price the worst-case basket before launch.
5. **Prepaid incentive is usually the cheapest offer available in India.** The COD fee is small
   next to the RTO rate it causes, so 3-5% off prepaid frequently pays for itself.
6. **Collection sorting is a merchandising decision, not a default.** Alphabetical order puts the
   worst product first in every collection nobody curated.
7. **A result needs a baseline written before the change, and a check for what else moved.** A
   festive week compared to a normal one is seasonality read as a lift.
8. **Never merchandise on a stock figure that was not read today.** Featuring a hero that sold out
   costs more than the campaign earns.
