# Platform mechanics

Each platform rewards a different behaviour. The same idea, cut differently, is
the whole job. Numbers here are conventions, not guarantees - read the account's
own analytics before arguing.

## What each surface wants

| Platform | The unit | The hook has | What kills it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 7-20s vertical video, loopable | 1 second of motion, text on screen | A slow logo intro, a landscape crop, a watermark from another app |
| Instagram feed / carousel | 4:5 static or 5-8 slide carousel | Slide 1 readable as a thumbnail | Slide 1 that is a title card, text under 24pt |
| Stories | 9:16, ephemeral | A question, a poll, a poll-style CTA | A feed post dropped in unchanged |
| LinkedIn | Text-first, 1:1 or 4:5 image, document carousels | First two lines before "see more" | Links in the body, hashtag walls, engagement bait |
| YouTube Shorts | Under 60s vertical, 3s hook | The promise in the first frame | Reused TikTok watermark, no context |
| YouTube long | Title and thumbnail decide the click | Face plus one claim | A thumbnail that repeats the title |
| X | Text, image optional | The first clause | Threads with no payoff |
| WhatsApp Status / channel | 9:16, personal tone | A price, a date, a favour | Broadcast spam, no opt-in |
| Google Business posts | Local, offer-led | The offer and the locality | Posting offers that expired |

## Format rules that are not optional

- **Vertical is not a crop.** 9:16 means the subject is composed for a phone held upright; cropping a landscape frame cuts the product or the face.
- **Safe areas.** Keep text out of the top 15% and bottom 20% of a reel - the UI eats it.
- **Captions on all video.** Most views are silent. Burn them in, do not rely on auto-captions for numbers and brand names.
- **The first frame is the thumbnail.** Choose it deliberately; do not let the platform pick.
- **One CTA per post.** "Save it, share it, DM us and comment" produces none of them.

## Caption mechanics

- The visible fragment before truncation is roughly the first 120 characters on Instagram and the first two lines on LinkedIn. Write that fragment as the whole hook.
- Links in the caption do not get clicks on Instagram; use "link in bio" and keep the bio current.
- Hashtags: three to five specific ones beat thirty generic ones. On LinkedIn, two at most.
- Prices, dates and sizes in the caption, not only in the image - people search captions.

## Posting time, honestly

There is no universal best time. There is the account's own best time, visible in
the analytics of the last 20 posts. If the analytics are not reachable, default to
the customer's commute or lunch and say it is a default, not a finding.

## Anti-patterns

- The same file posted to five platforms with the same crop and the same caption.
- A watermark from one platform on another - both platforms suppress it and it looks careless.
- Boosting a post that did not work organically; you are paying to show a weak hook.
- Judging a reel by likes when its job was saves or DMs.
