# Repurposing

One good idea is worth a week. The craft is cutting it so each surface gets
something that stands on its own, without the seams showing.

## The cut map

From one 20-minute founder video, in order of yield:

1. **Three 15-second vertical clips** - the three strongest statements, each with its own first frame.
2. **One 45-second reel** - the same three statements in a sequence, for the account's own feed.
3. **One LinkedIn text post** - the argument, written out, with the clip as an optional attachment.
4. **One carousel** - five slides, one claim each, from the same transcript.
5. **Two stories** - a poll on the controversial line, a question box on the practical one.
6. **One newsletter section** - the full argument, for the list that opted in.
7. **Five reply templates** - the questions the video answers, usable in DMs and comments.

## From a written piece

- **Carousel**: one sentence per slide, the last slide is the CTA. Never paste paragraphs.
- **Reel script**: the strongest claim plus one proof plus the CTA, 20 seconds.
- **Quote card**: only if the sentence survives without context. Most do not.
- **Thread**: only when the argument has steps. A thread of observations is a blog post in disguise.

## From a customer conversation

- The question, the answer, the proof. That is a post, and it is the highest-converting kind because someone actually asked it.
- Anonymise: no name, no order number, no location so specific it identifies them.

## Rules of the cut

- **Never stretch one asset across platforms unchanged.** Re-caption, re-crop, re-hook.
- **Keep the watermark off.** Re-export from the source, not from a downloaded file.
- **Each cut needs its own CTA**, appropriate to the surface.
- **Say what you reused.** "Same idea as Tuesday's reel, cut for LinkedIn with the argument spelled out" is honest and the audience does not see both anyway.
- **Kill the weak cut.** If the clip has no hook, it does not go out because the source was good.

## The reuse ledger

Keep a one-line ledger per idea: date, source asset, cuts made, platform, result.
After a month you can see which source material actually yields - usually two or
three formats, not seven. Write that into `notes/learnings.md` and stop producing
the rest.

## Anti-patterns

- The same caption on four platforms with the emoji changed.
- A 40-minute video chopped into eight fragments nobody can follow.
- Repurposing a competitor's post into your brand's voice. That is not repurposing.
- Reusing last year's festival creative without checking the offer.
