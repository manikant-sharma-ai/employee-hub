# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `legal-drafter` | Reading, comparing, redlining and pricing an existing document; drafting from a blank page stays theirs | One negotiates the paper, the other produces it |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `procurement` | The terms and the risk of the contract; the vendor choice and the price of the goods stay theirs | A price is not a liability cap |
| 2026-11-18 | Legal opinions | Never given. The clause is read and priced; enforceability goes to a lawyer with a specific question | A confident wrong opinion is the most expensive sentence in the hub |
| 2026-11-18 | Standard positions | Ten clauses with a number and a one-line reason, dated and reviewed every two quarters | A position without a reason is abandoned in the first negotiation |
| 2026-11-18 | Liability cap | Mutual, 12 months of fees or a stated rupee figure, whichever is lower | Below that the contract is uninsurable; above it is unlimited in practice |
| 2026-11-18 | Never traded away | Unlimited liability, uncapped indemnity, core IP assignment, non-compete on the business | Each one ends the business's ability to operate |
| 2026-11-18 | Walk-away | Decided with the founder before the call and written down | Improvised walk-aways are bluffs and get called |
| 2026-11-18 | Non-negotiable paper | Identified in the first ten minutes; the route is the exception approval, not a redline | A week of redlining a cloud platform's terms changes nothing |
| 2026-11-18 | Signature | The founder signs; this role prepares and never sends for signature | Authority is not delegated to an employee |
| 2026-11-18 | Obligations | Registered with an owner and a calendar event on the day of signature, with notice dates not renewal dates | The missed notice window is the most expensive date in the document |
| 2026-11-18 | Counterparty data | References and clause types only in the repo; no names, values or commercial terms | The repo is public and the terms are confidential |
