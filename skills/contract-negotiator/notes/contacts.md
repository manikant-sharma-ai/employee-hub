# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | The walk-away, anything above the approval limit, the signature | A one-page summary | Wants the decision and the largest exposure, not the clause list |
| <Lawyer on call> | Unlimited liability, uncapped indemnity, IP assignment, non-compete, foreign jurisdiction | Email with the specific clause and question | Ask a question, not "please review this" |
| <CA> | Place of supply, TDS, GST on fees, treatment of credits | Email | Tax treatment is theirs; this role does not opine |
| <Finance owner> | Committed spend, payment terms against the cash cycle, indexation | Monthly | A 90-day term is a working capital decision |
| <Data privacy officer> | Processing terms, sub-processors, breach notice window | On signature day | The DPA is part of the contract, not an annexure to ignore |
| <Counterparty procurement contact> | The negotiation, the exception route, the timeline | Their preferred channel | Ask who approves a deviation and what they need to see |
| <Counterparty legal contact> | Redline exchange, definitions, version control | Email with tracked changes | One version, numbered, never edited in place |
| <Delivery owner> | Deliverables, SLAs, acceptance criteria after signature | The register | Performance failures start with an unread schedule |
| <Compliance watch> | Reporting or filing obligations the contract creates | On signature day | A contract can create a regulatory date |
