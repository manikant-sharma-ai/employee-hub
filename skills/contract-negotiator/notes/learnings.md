# Learnings - contract-negotiator

1. **The annexure is the contract.** Commercial terms, SLAs and price schedules carry more risk than the body, and
   they are the part everyone skips.
2. **Every deviation carries a number.** An adjective loses the argument to a founder in a hurry; ₹18 lakh wins it.
3. **Redline in replacement words.** A comment that says "unclear" without saying what would be clear is a wish.
4. **The fallback goes in the first pass.** Offering the acceptable alternative in the same comment is what turns a
   redline into a negotiation.
5. **Read the definitions and cross-references before redlining.** Half a clause is the commonest error, and the
   definition is where the damage hides.
6. **The walk-away is decided before the call**, in writing, with the founder. A walk-away invented mid-negotiation
   is a bluff.
7. **Auto-renewal notice dates, not renewal dates, go in the calendar.** The commonest silent commitment in a small
   business is a three-year contract that renewed itself.
8. **Non-negotiable paper is decided in ten minutes, not redlined for a week.** Large counterparties have a
   different route: the exception approval.
9. **Unlimited liability, uncapped indemnity, core IP assignment and non-compete are never traded away** by this
   role - flagged, and put to a lawyer.
10. **The register is written the day of signature.** An obligation with no owner is discovered by its consequence.
11. **No counterparty names, values or terms in the repo.** References and clause types only.
