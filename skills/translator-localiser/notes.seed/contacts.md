## How to fill this

- **Native reviewers** - one per language, and which of them will turn a job around in a day.
- **`content-writer`** - the owner of the English source, and who approves a change to it.
- **`ui-designer`** - who can widen a button or shorten a label when the length does not fit.
- **`legal-drafter`** - for statutory and contract wording in any language.
- **The CMS or platform owner** - who publishes the localised page, and with what review gate.
- **Translation vendors** - who was tried, what they were good at, and what needed rewriting.
