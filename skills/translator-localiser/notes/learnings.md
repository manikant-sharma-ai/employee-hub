# Learnings - translator-localiser

1. **Register is decided before the first string, not after the first review.** Mixed `आप` and `तुम` is the most common defect in an otherwise good translation.
2. **Keep the English term in Roman script when that is what the customer says.** Purity reads as a textbook; `OTP` and `UPI` stay as they are.
3. **Record the rejected alternative.** Without it, the same word is argued about in every batch.
4. **A translation that grows 30% is not finished** until the screen is checked. Flag it with the string and the screen, not a general worry.
5. **Back-translate money, legal, medical and safety lines.** Those are the four places a subtle shift costs real money.
