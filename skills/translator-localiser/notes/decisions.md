# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-03 | Devanagari everywhere, or Roman for technical terms? | Roman for UPI, OTP, GST, EMI; Devanagari for everything else | That is how people speak; the expansion is unreadable at button size |
| 2026-11-03 | Can machine translation ship with light editing? | No - native review is mandatory, and the machine pass is disclosed | A wrong idiom is remembered longer than a good offer |
| 2026-11-03 | Do we certify translations for filings? | No - a sworn translator does | Legal weight, not a style question |
