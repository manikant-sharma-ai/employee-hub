# Learnings - procurement

1. **The specification is written before the quotes are read.** A vendor who writes the requirement writes it around what they stock.
2. **Compare landed cost and TCO, never the quoted number.** Freight, GST credit, payment terms and consumables reorder the ranking more often than price does.
3. **Never pay an advance when the GSTIN name, the bank account name and the letterhead disagree.** That mismatch is the most common advance-payment fraud.
4. **Three quotes from one distributor network is one quote.** Source from different supply chains or say the benchmark is weak.
5. **The renewal cap is negotiated at signature, not at renewal.** "Lower of 8% or WPI" in writing is worth more than any first-year discount.
6. **Splitting a purchase to stay under an approval limit is a control failure,** and auditors test for it first.
