# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-04 | Three quotes for every purchase? | Three quotes above ₹25,000; a rate contract reference below that | The control has to be cheap enough to be followed |
| 2026-11-04 | Advance to a new vendor? | Maximum 30%, balance against GRN; security for anything more | Advance-payment fraud is the largest single loss in this role |
| 2026-11-04 | Ranking basis? | Three-year TCO with named assumptions, not quoted price | Cheapest quote is frequently the most expensive purchase |
| 2026-11-04 | Auto-renewals? | Capped increase and a 60-day review owned by a named person | The renewal is where the price actually rises |
