# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | Refund approvals above the threshold, write-offs, precedent-setting cases | Email, with the numbers | Wants the decision and the clause, not the thread |
| <CA or accounts person> | Correction form, credit-note treatment, place of supply, return periods | Email with the document | Decides the tax treatment; this role prepares the question |
| <Bookkeeper> | The ledger line for every refund and credit note | The daily queue sheet | If they have to ask, the record failed |
| <Finance ops> | Reconciliation against gateway settlement, the monthly summary | Monthly | Needs reason codes, not narratives |
| <Gateway account manager> | Retry settings, mandate status, chargeback disputes, descriptor | Console and phone | The descriptor is why customers do not recognise a charge |
| <Dunning settings owner> | What is sent, when, from which address | The billing console | A ladder nobody owns drifts into daily reminders |
| <Helpdesk admin> | Queue routing, tags, the shared inbox | The helpdesk | Billing tags must separate the three triage routes |
| <Account owner> | Enterprise and annual accounts, and any pause decision | Direct | Their relationship outranks the ladder |
| <Collections agent> | The handoff for B2B invoices on credit terms | Weekly | Different problem: agreed terms, not a failed mandate |
| <Product manager> | A failure pattern caused by the product, e.g. a bad proration rule | A ticket with counts | Three refunds for one reason is a product bug |
