# Payroll and TDS

Payroll is the most predictable outflow and the one most often processed late,
because it needs six inputs from four people. Build the calendar once and it stops
being a monthly crisis.

## The monthly calendar

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 20th (previous month) | Attendance, leaves, overtime inputs closed | HR |
| 22nd | Variable pay: incentives, reimbursements approved | Manager |
| 25th | Payroll register computed and reviewed | Finance |
| 28th-30th | Salary credited, payslips issued | Finance |
| 7th (next month) | TDS deposited | Finance |
| 15th (next month) | PF/ESI deposited | Finance |
| Quarterly | 24Q/26Q returns filed | CA |

## The register, line by line

Gross → statutory deductions (PF, ESI where applicable, professional tax by state)
→ TDS on salary → other deductions (advance, loan, canteen) → net pay.

Rules: one row per employee, one column per component, and a control total that
matches the bank file exactly before anything is paid.

## TDS on salary, the practical version

- **Ask for the investment declaration in April**, not in January. Late declarations produce a March deduction that feels like a penalty and damages trust.
- **Regime choice** is per employee and per year; record which one each person elected.
- **Reimbursements** with bills are treated differently from allowances without; the structure matters more than the amount.
- **Full-and-final settlements** need a separate computation, and often a revised 24Q in the next quarter.
- **Never quote a slab or a rate as fact** - the numbers change with each Finance Act. The mechanism is stable; the numbers are the CA's.

## Contractor payments

- **TDS under 194C or 194J** depends on the nature of the work, not the label on the invoice.
- **Get a valid PAN.** No PAN means a higher deduction rate, and the vendor will argue about it after the fact.
- **Keep the invoice, the PAN and the payment proof together** - that is the file the assessment asks for.

## Controls

- **Two-person rule**: whoever prepares the register does not release the payment.
- **A change in bank details requires a phone call** to a number from the original contract, not from the email that requested the change.
- **No employee is paid from an unapproved addition.** Every ad-hoc payment has a written approval.

## What the pack contains

Register, bank file, payslips, statutory challans, the TDS working and the variance
against last month. The variance is the page that catches the error.

## Anti-patterns

- Paying salary late and depositing TDS on time - the order is backwards.
- Deducting PF on the wrong wage base.
- Cash payments to staff with no record.
- A March surprise deduction nobody explained in April.
- Bank detail changes accepted by email alone.
