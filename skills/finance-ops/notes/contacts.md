## How to fill this
- **The CA** - who decides tax positions and files returns, their turnaround, and what format they want working papers in.
- **The bookkeeper or accounts assistant** - who posts entries, and which categories they get wrong most often.
- **The bank relationship manager** - for statement access, cheque issues and payment limits.
- **The gateway account manager** - for settlement reports, fee queries and chargebacks.
- **The payroll provider** - for PF, ESI and professional tax filings.
- **The signatory** - who approves payments and signs off the close, and their approval threshold.

Keep names and roles only. No bank account numbers, no portal credentials, no personal data in this file.
