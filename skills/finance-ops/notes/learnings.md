# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-02 - Book the gross sale and the gateway fee separately. Booking the net payout understates revenue, hides the cost, and the error compounds monthly.
- 2026-11-02 - Never plug a difference. An honest unexplained ₹4,000 is worth more than a balance that ties, because the plug survives every future reconciliation.
- 2026-11-02 - A vendor bank-detail change is verified by phone against the original contract, never by email. This is the single most common successful fraud against an SMB.
- 2026-11-02 - Prepare, do not file. Every statutory paper leaves this employee labelled "prepared for review", because the CA owns the position and the filing.
