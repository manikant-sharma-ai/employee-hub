# Learnings - tender-bidder

1. **Eligibility is checked before a word is written.** Four days of writing on a tender the business
   was never eligible for is the most expensive habit in this job.
2. **The ATC overrides the notice and the corrigendum overrides both.** Reading only the notice is how
   a bid is submitted against terms that changed a week earlier.
3. **A dealer's OEM letter must name the tender.** A generic authorisation is rejected, and the reissue
   takes longer than the deadline allows.
4. **The MSE exemption is clause-specific.** It waives the EMD only where the notice says so; an EMD not
   paid on an assumption is an automatic rejection.
5. **Upload 24 hours early and open every file.** Portals fail on the last afternoon, and a corrupt PDF
   is invisible until evaluation.
6. **The price floor is decided before the reverse auction, in writing.** Decided live, it becomes the
   last bid plus one step.
7. **LD is priced, not read.** 0.5% a week with a 10% cap can consume the whole margin in ten weeks of
   delay.
8. **The completion certificate is the asset.** Ask at acceptance; it is the document every future
   eligibility test demands and the hardest one to obtain a year later.
9. **A poor GeM rating travels.** It is read by the next buyer before your bid is opened.
