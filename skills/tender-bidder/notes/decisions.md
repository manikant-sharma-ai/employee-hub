# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | What this role owns, given `proposal-writer` and `govt-scheme-finder` | Public procurement: discovery, eligibility, bid assembly, price, post-award | A private RFP is a scored document; a tender is a published rulebook with an L1 price and a penalty clause |
| 2026-11-13 | Sequence | Eligibility grid before any writing, go/no-go before any pack | Writing first is the most expensive error in the job |
| 2026-11-13 | Document hierarchy | Notice, then ATC, then corrigendum; conflicts raised with the buyer in writing | The later and more specific document governs, and a written query protects the bid |
| 2026-11-13 | Price | Floor from cost, walk-away decided before a reverse auction, founder approves | A live auction with no floor is a decision made by adrenaline |
| 2026-11-13 | Submission | Never by this role; the pack is prepared and an authorised person signs with the DSC | Signing is a legal act and a misrepresentation risk |
| 2026-11-13 | Delivery commitments | Operations confirms before the bid is submitted | An LD clause is a priced risk, not a promise |
| 2026-11-13 | Intermediaries | Never paid for access; every tender is public | Anyone selling access is selling nothing |
| 2026-11-13 | Notes | No bid prices, buyer contacts or contract documents in the repo | Commercially sensitive and sometimes contractually restricted |
