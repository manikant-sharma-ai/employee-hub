# Learnings - logistics-coordinator

1. **Volumetric weight, not the base rate, is where freight goes wrong.** The box size explains most "why is
   shipping up" questions, and the fix is a packing decision with a damage consequence.
2. **A comparison on base freight is wrong by 20-40%** in the direction of the carrier with the most surcharges.
   Fuel, COD, ODA and the return leg all belong in the landed number.
3. **ODA pin codes are discovered on the invoice unless they are checked at booking.** The charge is real and the
   quote was not.
4. **The NDR window is 48 hours and it is the highest-value time in this role.** One customer contact inside 24
   hours saves the order and the return leg.
5. **The cut-off is the carrier's, not the business's**, and it moves during peak weeks.
6. **An unscanned parcel has no chain of custody** and becomes "not received" with no way to prove otherwise.
7. **Reconcile COD per order, never per total.** A total that matches can hide two orders swapped and one missing.
8. **A 5-10% error rate on freight invoices is normal where nobody audits.** It is margin, and it is recoverable
   inside the dispute window.
9. **The dispute window is a deadline with money attached.** An audit done after month-end can be too late.
10. **Some lanes are not fixable by changing the carrier.** A remote lane is slow because it is far; the honest
    move is a longer promise and an ODA charge.
