# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Carrier A account manager> | Rates, escalations, disputes, volume commitments | Phone and email | The one person who can unstick a stuck consignment |
| <Carrier B account manager> | Same, for the second carrier | Phone and email | Kept live for leverage and for lane coverage |
| <Pickup contact, each carrier> | Daily pickup, manifest discrepancies, cut-off changes | The app or the phone | Knows before the control room does |
| <Aggregator account manager> (Shiprocket or similar) | Multi-carrier rates, remittance reports, dispute resolution | The dashboard | Remittance cycles and reports live here |
| <Marketplace logistics support> | Late-delivery penalties, claims, their own logistics | The seller panel | Penalty windows are shorter than they look |
| <Packaging supplier> | Box sizes, volumetric impact, void fill, lead time | Email | A box change is a freight change |
| <Bulk transporter> | Pallets, full-truck, B2B consignments | Phone | Different rate logic from parcel carriers |
| <CA or tax consultant> | E-way bill thresholds, freight GST treatment, reverse charge | Email | Route the tax question here, never answer it from memory |
| <Warehouse or packing lead> | Weights, dimensions, packing standard, short dispatches | Daily | The weight slip originates here |
| <Returns desk> | RTO intake, grading, disposition | Daily | `returns-manager` owns the disposition |
| <Finance owner> | Freight expense, COD receivable, dispute recovery | Monthly | The books must match the operations numbers |
