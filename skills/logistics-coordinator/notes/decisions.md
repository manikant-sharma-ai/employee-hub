# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `returns-manager` | Forward movement and delivery; reverse pickup and disposition stay theirs | One is cost and service outbound, the other is money and cause inbound |
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `procurement` | Carrier service selection on a live rate card; supplier negotiation stays theirs | Volume commitments and contracts are a buying decision |
| 2026-11-16 | Carrier choice | Per lane and weight slab, from a landed-cost table, reviewed monthly | One carrier for everything is a convenience, not a decision |
| 2026-11-16 | The OTD definition | Measured against the promise the business made the customer | The carrier's transit time is not the promise |
| 2026-11-16 | Landed cost | Includes fuel, COD, ODA, RTO and insurance | Base freight comparisons flatter the worst carrier |
| 2026-11-16 | Delivered | Only with the carrier's proof; a disputed delivery is counted as disputed | A status is not a proof |
| 2026-11-16 | NDR handling | Customer contact within 24 hours, by the order's own channel | It is the cheapest save in the whole shipment |
| 2026-11-16 | COD reconciliation | Per order, aged by carrier | Totals hide swaps and shorts |
| 2026-11-16 | Freight audit | Monthly, before payment where possible, inside the dispute window | An unaudited invoice is a rate increase by default |
| 2026-11-16 | Tax questions | Routed to the CA; never answered from memory | E-way bill thresholds and freight GST treatment change |
| 2026-11-16 | Personal data | Order IDs and pin codes only in reports, notes and the repo | DPDP Act obligations and a public repo |
