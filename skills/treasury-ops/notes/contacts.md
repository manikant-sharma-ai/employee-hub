# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Relationship manager, main bank> | Limits, renewal, charges, sweep setup | Phone, then email to confirm | Ask for the renewal document list 60 days ahead |
| <Relationship manager, second bank> | A competing quote at renewal | Email | Two quotes turn a formality into a negotiation |
| <Accounts head / founder's office> | Payment approvals, payee master changes | The approval sheet | Never approve from a screenshot |
| <CA> | Statutory due dates, advance tax instalments, TDS rates | A specific question with the numbers | Confirm the challan date, not just the return date |
| <Payroll owner> | Net pay, pay date, statutory split | The payroll register | Payroll date drives the whole week |
| <Key supplier - credit controller> | Payment terms, holds, part payments | Phone before the due date | Knows exactly how long supply survives |
| <AD bank forex desk> | Forward cover, rates, cancellation charges | Email with amount and date | Get the cancellation terms in writing |
| <Key customer - accounts payable> | Promise dates, remittance advice | Their AP portal or email | Record what they actually paid against what they promised |
| <Insurance broker> | Cover required by a lender, asset insurance | Before a sanction | Some limits are conditional on it |
| <Company secretary / legal> | Board approvals for borrowing, guarantees | Before signing | Borrowing resolutions take longer than expected |
