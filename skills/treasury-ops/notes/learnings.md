# Learnings - treasury-ops

1. **The minimum week is the answer, not the average.** Every position that starts with a monthly
   total ends with a founder surprised by a bounce in the one week nobody looked at.
2. **A customer's promise is booked at their historical delay, never at their words.** The forecast
   error on the four-week minimum falls by more than half after this rule is applied honestly.
3. **Statutory dues are not working capital.** GST, TDS, PF and advance tax are the cheapest-looking
   money in the business and the most expensive to be caught short on - the interest is small, the
   record is not.
4. **Never lock cash for longer than the horizon in which it is needed.** The rate difference is
   almost never worth the break penalty and the paperwork.
5. **A payee bank-detail change is a phone call, not an email.** Callback on a number already on
   file, every time, whoever the vendor is and however urgent the message.
6. **The cost of money is an annualised all-in number.** 2% for 38 days early is 21% a year; the
   overdraft at 11.5% is the comparison, not zero.
7. **Three accounts is one position.** Reading one account and calling it cash is how a business
   bounces a payment while holding a balance.
8. **Renegotiate before the due date, in writing, with a date.** A vendor told on the day stops
   supply; a vendor told a week early usually waits.
