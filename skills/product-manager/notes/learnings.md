# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-03 - The cut list is the value. Every plan arrives 40% too big, and the deferred column with a trigger is what stops the same request returning next quarter.
- 2026-11-03 - A request is a hypothesis with a sponsor. It needs one real conversation before it becomes a plan.
- 2026-11-03 - Define the metric and the decision rule before launch, including the number that means "this failed". A rule written after the numbers is a rationalisation.
- 2026-11-03 - Most requests fail the "is a product change the cheapest fix" test. A help article or a policy change is often the whole answer.
