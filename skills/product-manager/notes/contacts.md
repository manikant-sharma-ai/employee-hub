# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

| Name | What for | Last useful | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## How to fill this
- **The engineer or team lead** - who estimates, and what they push back on; note the difference between their estimate and the date promised.
- **The `ui-designer` employee** - for flows that need screens; note the states they always ask about.
- **`qa-tester`** - who verifies acceptance criteria, and what their definition of done adds.
- **The loudest customer's account owner** - so a request arrives with revenue context rather than volume.
- **Support lead** - the source of theme counts, and the person who sees the workarounds first.
- **Whoever approves pricing and money-touching changes** - a pricing change is a product change with a tax clause.

Keep names and roles only. No phone numbers, no personal email, no customer personal data in this file.
