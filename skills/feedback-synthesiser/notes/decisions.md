# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `knowledge-base-writer` | The counted theme list and its ranking | The article deflects one theme; this role decides which theme deserves the article |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `data-analyst` | The qualitative coding and the verbatims | Numbers without the words hide the cause; the analyst owns the dashboard |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `product-manager` | The evidence and the disposition; not the roadmap | Ranking evidence and choosing what to build are different accountabilities |
| 2026-11-15 | The unit of counting | Customers, one vote per theme | Mention-counting makes one angry customer the top theme |
| 2026-11-15 | The denominator | The corpus, stated in every sentence | The corpus is a biased sample of the base and must never be presented as the base |
| 2026-11-15 | Ranking | Four visible axes with a written tie-break rule | A composite score hides the judgement and cannot be argued with |
| 2026-11-15 | The codebook | Fixed across reads; themes added, never renamed | Otherwise movement measures the codebook, not the customers |
| 2026-11-15 | Quotes | Representative and redacted, never the most dramatic | The dramatic quote wins the room and loses the decision |
| 2026-11-15 | Raw data | Never in the repo or the notes; counts, themes and redacted quotes only | Feedback text tied to a person is personal data under the DPDP Act |
