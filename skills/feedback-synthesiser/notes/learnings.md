# Learnings - feedback-synthesiser

1. **The corpus is not the customer base.** Every read says "of those who wrote", never "of our customers". The
   silent majority is a limit, not an inconvenience.
2. **Count customers, not mentions.** One angry customer with forty messages is the top theme in every read that
   counts messages, and the wrong decision every time.
3. **The channel decides the bias before the data decides the theme.** Tickets skew to the broken, reviews to the
   extremes, WhatsApp to the paying. State it in the first three lines.
4. **Name themes in the customer's words.** "The app logs me out mid-order" can be fixed; "authentication
   friction" is a meeting.
5. **An "other" bucket above 15-20% means the codebook is wrong**, not that the customers are unusual.
6. **Show the weighted and unweighted count side by side.** The gap is the finding: it says whether this is a
   small-customer problem or everyone's.
7. **A theme that shrinks may have churned, not been fixed.** Check the churn list before claiming a win.
8. **The most under-used disposition is "say differently".** A third of "not as described" is a photo, a size
   chart or a delivery promise.
9. **Keep the codebook stable between reads** or the movement is the codebook. A new theme is added, never a
   renamed old one.
10. **A theme with no owner and no date does not exist.** The read's only product is a decision.
