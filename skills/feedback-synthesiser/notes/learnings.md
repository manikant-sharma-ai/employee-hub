# Learnings - feedback-synthesiser

1. **The coverage sentence goes first, every time.** "142 of 1,180 tickets plus 96 reviews" survives
   scrutiny; "customers are saying" does not, and the first sceptic ends the readout.
2. **Read 30 records cold before writing a single theme.** A theme list invented before reading finds
   exactly what the team already believed, which is the definition of a useless synthesis.
3. **Count records, not mentions.** One ticket that says "slow" four times is one record, and the
   difference between the two counting methods is the difference between a finding and an exaggeration.
4. **An unmatched share above 15% is a finding, not noise.** It usually means the newest problem is
   the one missing from the taxonomy.
5. **Frequency and severity are different axes, and the fourth cell is where the money is** - the rare
   theme that quietly loses customers never surfaces in a volume ranking.
6. **Trend needs two comparable periods.** One period is a snapshot, and calling it a trend is the
   fastest way to lose the founder's confidence.
7. **A quote is verbatim or it is a paraphrase, and the two are never mixed.** Tidying a quote into
   better English is the small dishonesty that undermines the whole readout.
8. **A theme with no owner is a paragraph, not a finding.** The readout is finished when a decision is
   assigned, not when the themes are counted.
