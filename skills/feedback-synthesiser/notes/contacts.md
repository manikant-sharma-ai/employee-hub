# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Support lead> | Ticket export, tags, volume context | The desk admin panel | Ask for body text and tags, not a dashboard screenshot |
| <Product owner> | What shipped in the period, and the roadmap slot | The readout, not the raw file | Wants counts and quotes, not cost estimates |
| <Marketing owner> | Review exports, survey results, customer language | The platform accounts | The exact wording is the most reusable output |
| <Customer success> | Cancellation reasons, churn context, account notes | The CRM | The silent churners only appear here |
| <Finance> | Cost per occurrence: refunds, freight, support hours | The ledger | Needed for the cost-weighted ranking |
| <Data / analytics owner> | Volume denominators, segment definitions | A query, not a favour | The share is only as good as the denominator |
| <Legal / privacy owner> | Whether a quote may be published | Before any external use | DPDP and consent live here |
| <The founder> | The decision on the top three themes | The one-page readout | Reads five lines and the quote, not the table |
