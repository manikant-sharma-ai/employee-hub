# journal.schema - shape only, no days recorded

One file per month, `notes/journal/YYYY-MM.md`. One bullet per synthesis, written before the reply.

```
- **YYYY-MM-DD** - <period, records read of available> | <top three themes and who they went to> |
  <what the next synthesis should do differently, or "nothing">
```

## Rules

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Date | dd/mm/yyyy |
| Numbers | Records read against records available, always both |
| Next time | A change in method, or "nothing" |

## What does not belong here

- Customer quotes, names or ticket ids.
- A repeat of the theme table. That belongs with the readout.
