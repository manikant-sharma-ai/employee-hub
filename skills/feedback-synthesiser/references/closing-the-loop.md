# Closing the loop

A theme with a disposition and no owner is a slide. This file is what happens after the read, and it is the part
that makes the next read trusted.

## The six dispositions

| Disposition | Meaning | Goes to | The artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build | A real gap; something must be made | `product-manager`, founder | A spec or a roadmap line with the counts attached |
| Fix | Something is broken; no new capability needed | Engineering, ops | A defect with the theme count and the example tickets |
| Document | The answer exists and nobody can find it | `knowledge-base-writer` | An article, with the theme as its brief |
| Say differently | The product is fine; the promise is wrong | `content-writer`, `shopify-merchandiser`, `marketplace-lister` | Changed listing, label, photo or email |
| Price | The complaint is about value, not the product | `pricing-analyst` | A packaging or plan change |
| Watch | Real but small, or not decidable this quarter | This role | A line in the tracker with a re-read date |

The most under-used disposition is "say differently". In most Indian D2C businesses, a third of "not as
described" complaints are a photo, a size chart or a delivery promise, not a product.

## Routing, in one table

| Theme shape | First stop | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot complete the job | Engineering, via `product-manager` | It is a defect or a blocker, and it has a fix |
| Asked the same question repeatedly | `knowledge-base-writer` | The article stops the next forty tickets |
| Wants something that does not exist | `product-manager` | Needs prioritisation against everything else |
| Wrong expectation set before purchase | `shopify-merchandiser`, `marketplace-lister` | The listing is the fix |
| Too expensive | `pricing-analyst` | Value, packaging, or the segment |
| Angry about a specific case | `support-agent` | One customer, one reply, now |
| Lost a deal over it | `sales-outreach`, `sales-engineer` | The objection is in the pipeline |
| Delivery or packaging | `logistics-coordinator` (#72), `packaging-designer` (#78) | Operational, with a measurable outcome |
| Cancellation for a reason | `customer-success`, `churn-analyst` (#49) | A save play and a cohort read |

## Telling the customers

The loop closes in public only when the business can do it honestly.

| Situation | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| A defect was fixed | Tell the customers who reported it, individually | Announce a fix for a bug most never saw |
| A feature was built from feedback | Name the customers who asked, with permission | Claim "you asked, we built" when nobody did |
| The answer was documentation | Reply in the thread with the article | Publish an article nobody links to |
| The business decided not to act | Say nothing publicly; record it in `decisions.md` | Promise a roadmap item that will not ship |
| A price change responds to feedback | Explain the change once, plainly | Attribute it to feedback as marketing |

## The re-read, and how to run it

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Same codebook | Do not change the themes between reads, or the movement is the codebook |
| Same channels, or the difference stated | A new channel changes the corpus and the comparison |
| Same period length | Quarter against quarter, month against month |
| Report movement, not just the snapshot | Grew, shrank, appeared, disappeared |
| Check the disappearance | A theme can vanish because its customers churned |
| One page, same shape | The founder should be able to read the second one in ninety seconds |

## Measuring whether the fix worked

| Theme | The measure | The lag |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Activation rate, and the theme's share of new-customer feedback | One cohort |
| Delivery | Days to deliver, and the delivery theme's share | 2-4 weeks |
| Not as described | Return rate by reason, per SKU | One month |
| Support experience | First response time, and the theme's share | 2 weeks |
| Feature gap | Requests for the same feature after the launch | One quarter |
| Price | Win rate at the same segment, and the pricing theme's share | One quarter |

A fix that does not move its measure either did not ship or did not address the cause. Both are worth knowing
before the next fix is scoped the same way.

## Anti-patterns

- **A read with no owner per theme.** The read is filed and nothing happens; the next read is not read.
- **Telling customers "we hear you" with no date.** It is worse than silence, because it is a promise.
- **Re-reading with a new codebook.** The movement becomes an artefact of the coding.
- **Celebrating a shrinking theme that shrank because customers left.** Check the churn list first.
- **Closing the loop only in the deck.** The loop closes with a customer, an article, a fix or a price - never
  with a slide.
