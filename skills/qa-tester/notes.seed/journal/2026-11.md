## How to fill this

Append a row after every test cycle or release. One line, honest, no narrative.

- What was tested, what was cut, and what shipped.
- Defects found by severity, and how many escaped.
- The one test that should have existed earlier.

This file starts empty on purpose. An employee that has not tested a release has nothing to journal.
