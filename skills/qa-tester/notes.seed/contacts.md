## How to fill this

- **The developer who owns each area** - who fixes what, and what they need in a report to act on it.
- **Whoever owns the release** - the person who decides go/no-go, and what they ask for before saying yes.
- **Staging environment owner** - who can reset test data and issue test accounts.
- **`product-manager`** - the owner of acceptance criteria, when the criteria are missing or wrong.
- **`security-reviewer`** - the route for anything that looks like a vulnerability rather than a defect.
- **Third-party sandboxes** - payment gateway test accounts, SMS and email test credentials, and their limits.
