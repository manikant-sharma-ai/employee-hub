## How to fill this

Add a row when a defect class, a flaky area or a test approach proves itself.

- **Defect patterns** - the class of bug this product produces repeatedly, and where it is usually found.
- **Flaky areas** - the surfaces that need a retest every release, and why.
- **Test data gaps** - the case that could not be tested for lack of data, and what data would unlock it.
- **Escaped defects** - what reached production, and the test that would have caught it.

No customer names, no production data, no credentials. The pattern, not the incident.
