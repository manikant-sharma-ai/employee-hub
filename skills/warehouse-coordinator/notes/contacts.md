# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Warehouse supervisor> | The daily plan, shift capacity, who is on the floor | In person, daily | Their view of the floor beats any report |
| <Pickers' lead> | Pick sequence, equipment, training new pickers | Daily huddle | Ask them which bin misleads before you redesign the aisle |
| <WMS or ERP administrator> | Item master, unit of measure, bundle rules, bin records | A ticket | Most "system errors" are a master-data setting |
| <Courier pickup contact> | Collection time, manifest disputes, refusals | Phone | An early truck is a real constraint; get the time in writing |
| <Logistics coordinator> | Exceptions within 24 hours, claims, delivery promises | The exception list | This role never promises a date to a customer |
| <Inventory planner> | Reorder points, stock cover, dead stock to clear | Monthly | They need the count variance, not the stock value |
| <Returns manager> | Inward returns, grading, disposition | The returns register | Unrecorded returns are the biggest hidden variance |
| <Accounts or founder> | Write-off approval above the limit | Email with the cause code | A write-off without a cause is a recurring cost |
| <Quality or QC person> | Damage standards, what may ship | Per SKU where it matters | Never ship a failed unit to clear a backlog |
| <Facilities or landlord> | Space, racking, power, the dock | As needed | Space maths fails on the ceiling, not the floor |
