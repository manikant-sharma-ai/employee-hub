# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `inventory-planner` | The physical stock, the count and the variance; how much to hold and when to reorder stay theirs | One is the shelf, the other is the policy |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `logistics-coordinator` | Packed, manifested and handed over; the carrier, the promise and the exception after that are theirs | The dock is the handover point between two jobs |
| 2026-11-18 | Count calendar | A-class weekly, B monthly, C quarterly, full physical count twice a year | A yearly count finds the cause twelve months late |
| 2026-11-18 | Variance thresholds | A 1% or 2 units, B 2% or 3, C 5%, set before the count and never renegotiated after | A threshold that moves is a negotiation |
| 2026-11-18 | Variance causes | Four codes - pick, receipt, unrecorded movement, system; "unknown" open for a week then escalated | "Shrinkage" is a conclusion that hides the cause |
| 2026-11-18 | Adjustments | Never to make the system match; only with a cause code, an approver and a date | An unexplained adjustment recurs larger |
| 2026-11-18 | Pack checks | Scan, count, weigh - all three, on every parcel, including a temp's first day | The three checks are the whole accuracy programme |
| 2026-11-18 | Count sheets | Blind, with location and class printed, never the expected quantity | Otherwise it is a confirmation, not a count |
| 2026-11-18 | Dispatch priority when the day is lost | Marketplace first, prepaid D2C second, COD last, announced at 11am | Penalty and money at risk decide the order |
| 2026-11-18 | Handover | Counted in front of the courier, signed on both copies, photographed | The only evidence that survives a claim |
| 2026-11-18 | Customer data | Order IDs and SKUs only in notes, templates and the repo; no names, addresses or phone numbers | A picking sheet is personal data and the repo is public |
