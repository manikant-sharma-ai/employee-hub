# Learnings - warehouse-coordinator

1. **Build the day backwards from the collection time.** A plan that starts from the pick list is a plan that
   discovers the cut-off at 4pm.
2. **Walking is most of the pick.** Sequencing by aisle, not by order, is the cheapest speed in the building.
3. **Weight is the last cheap check.** A parcel that weighs wrong contains the wrong thing, and the scale costs
   nothing per check.
4. **"Shrinkage" is not a cause.** Four causes or the variance stays open - pick, receipt, unrecorded movement,
   system.
5. **Repeated short picks on one SKU are the earliest warning in the warehouse.** Track them weekly by SKU.
6. **Unit-of-measure errors explain more "theft" than theft does.** Check the item master before accusing anyone.
7. **Blind count sheets only.** A sheet with the expected quantity is a confirmation exercise.
8. **The threshold is set before the count**, or the variance is judged by who found it.
9. **Count variance is an audit of the process, not of the stock.** The pattern names the cause; the count only
   produces the pattern if location and class are recorded.
10. **Sign and photograph the handover.** A courier's error becomes your loss the moment the sheet is signed
    without a count.
11. **Never adjust stock to make the number match.** The process failure survives the adjustment and returns
    larger next month.
