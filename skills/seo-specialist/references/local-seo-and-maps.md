# Local SEO and the map pack

For a business with a physical location or a service area, the map pack outranks
anything you can do on-page. Three results, above the organic list, and a
different ranking system.

## What actually moves a local ranking

| Factor | Weight | How to influence it |
|---|---|---|
| Proximity | High, not controllable | You cannot move the shop; you can target the areas you serve with content |
| Relevance | High | Correct categories, services, products, and the words people use locally |
| Prominence | High | Reviews, citations, links, and engagement on the listing |
| Completeness | Medium | Hours, photos, attributes, service areas, Q&A |
| Reviews | Medium-high | Volume, recency, rating, and replies - all four |

## The listing checklist

- [ ] Name exactly as the signboard says, no keyword stuffing
- [ ] Primary category correct, secondary categories chosen deliberately
- [ ] Address identical across the site, the listing and every citation
- [ ] Phone number local and answered; a number that rings out costs ranking and trust
- [ ] Hours, including festival closures and special hours
- [ ] 10+ real photos, updated; the interior and the product, not stock images
- [ ] Services and products with prices where the platform allows
- [ ] Q&A seeded with the questions the front desk actually gets
- [ ] Posts: an offer or update monthly, with an expiry
- [ ] Booking or WhatsApp link that works on mobile

## Reviews, honestly

- **Ask at the moment of satisfaction**, in person or by WhatsApp, with a direct link.
- **Never buy reviews or incentivise them.** Platforms detect it and Indian consumer law treats a fake review as a misleading advertisement.
- **Reply to every review**, including the bad ones - the reply is read by the next ten prospects, not the reviewer.
- **A 4.3 with 200 reviews beats a 5.0 with 9.** Volume and recency are signals.

## Multi-location

- One listing per real location, one page per location on the site, each with unique content (staff, parking, landmarks, the services that location actually offers).
- A single "locations" page listing twelve addresses is a duplicate-content problem with no local relevance.

## Service-area businesses

No storefront: hide the address, set a service area, and build location pages only
where you genuinely serve. Ten thin pages for ten localities is a penalty waiting.

## Local content that ranks

"Best [category] in [locality]" pages written by someone who has never been there
do not rank. What works: landmark-based directions, local parking reality, the
nearest metro, delivery times by pin code, and the local language.

## Measuring it

Track in the Business Profile insights: calls, direction requests, website clicks,
messages, and the search queries that surfaced the listing. Add "how did you find
us" to the intake form - the honest attribution for a local business is a question,
not a pixel.

## Anti-patterns

- Keyword-stuffed business name ("Sharma Sweets Best Mithai Shop Pune").
- Ten fake listings for ten localities you do not have.
- Ignoring reviews for six months, then replying to all at once.
- A "near me" strategy with no locality content.
- Chasing map-pack rank in a city you do not serve.
