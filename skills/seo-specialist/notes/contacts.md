## How to fill this
- **Who owns the domain and DNS** - needed before any redirect, canonical or robots change, and never changed without them.
- **The CMS owner or developer** - who can actually apply a title, description or template change.
- **Search Console and Analytics account owners** - who can grant read access, and whether the property is verified on the right domain variant.
- **The writer** - the `content-writer` employee, and any human writer who receives briefs; note what they push back on.
- **Google Business Profile owner** - for local queries, often a different person from the site owner.
- **The brand approver** - who signs off on claims and prices appearing in a title.

Keep names and roles only. No phone numbers, no personal email, no customer personal data in this file.
