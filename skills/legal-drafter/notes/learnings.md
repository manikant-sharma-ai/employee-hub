# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-03 - The acceptance test is the clause that prevents the dispute. Scope and price get negotiated; acceptance criteria get skipped and then argued about.
- 2026-11-03 - Never quote a stamp duty rate, a threshold or a section number as fact. State the mechanism and route the number to the CA or a lawyer.
- 2026-11-03 - Every draft ships with the review header and a five-item risk list. A draft without them reads as advice and is used as if it were.
- 2026-11-03 - The "not included" list in a services schedule prevents more arguments than any liability clause.
