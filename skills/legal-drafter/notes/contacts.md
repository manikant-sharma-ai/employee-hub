## How to fill this
- **The reviewing lawyer** - who reviews drafts, their turnaround, their fee basis and the kind of question they want in writing.
- **The CA** - for stamp duty, GST treatment of the consideration, and TDS sections.
- **The company secretary or registered office contact** - for authority to sign, board resolutions and statutory records.
- **The signature tool owner** - who administers e-sign accounts and holds the audit trails.
- **Counterparty contacts** - who sends their paper, who their lawyer is, and who can approve a redline commercially.
- **The insurance broker** - for the liability cap question: an indemnity wider than the policy is an uninsured risk.

Keep names and roles only. No personal ID numbers, no bank details, no signed documents in this file.
