# Learnings - collections-agent

1. **The invoice was earned; the chase is about timing.** The tone assumes a process gap, not bad faith, until the facts say otherwise - and the facts usually come from a phone call at day 15.
2. **Most late payments are a missing vendor code, a GRN, or an approver on leave.** Asking "what do you need from us to release it?" collects more than any reminder.
3. **A promise with no date is not a promise.** Amount, date, approver and condition, read back and logged - and checked on the promised morning, not three days later.
4. **Disputes pause the ladder.** Chasing a disputed invoice hardens the dispute and delays the money by a month.
5. **Every reminder carries the way to pay.** Amount, reference and link, or the reply is a question instead of a payment.
6. **Escalate on a boundary agreed in advance, in rupees.** A consequence named and never applied teaches the customer that the terms are decorative.
7. **Distinguish cannot from will not.** One gets a written schedule and keeps the customer for a decade; the other gets a faster ladder and a founder decision.
8. **Firm about the money, neutral about the person.** The mail that wins the argument loses the next order.
