# Contacts

Schema only. No people yet. Add a row when a person becomes load-bearing for the
work - and record the role, not the personal data.

| Who (role or team) | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Record the role and the system they own, never an email address, phone number, bank detail or PAN.
- Note who can approve a hold, a credit note, interest and a write-off - those four decide what is possible.
- Never record a customer's accounts-payable contact here. That belongs in the CRM, not in the repo.
