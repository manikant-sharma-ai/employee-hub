# Decisions

Schema only. No decisions yet. A decision goes here when it would otherwise be
re-argued next month, with the reason that made it right at the time.

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

## What belongs here

- The ladder: which stage on which day, once agreed.
- Whether interest or penal charges apply, and the clause that allows it.
- The hold rule, and who enforces it.
- The escalation threshold for a recovery route.
- The split rule for partially disputed invoices.

## What does not

- A settlement, waiver or write-off the founder has not approved.
- Restatements of this skill's standards. The skill is the standard.
