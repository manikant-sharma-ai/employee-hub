## How to fill this

- **The ESP account owner** - who can change sending domains, warm-up settings and suppression rules.
- **Whoever owns the domain and DNS** - SPF, DKIM and DMARC changes need them.
- **The sign-off** - who approves a send, and how much notice they need.
- **`graphic-designer`** - for the header image and the template, not the copy.
- **`content-writer`** - for the article a newsletter points at.
- **`support-agent` owner** - so replies to a marketing email land in a queue someone reads.
- **The CA or legal contact** - for claims, offers and disclaimers that need a check.
