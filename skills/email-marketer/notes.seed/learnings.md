## How to fill this

Add a row when a send produces a result worth reusing, or a failure worth avoiding.

- **Subject patterns** - the formula and the measured click rate, not the open rate.
- **Sequence timing** - which gap produced replies, and which produced unsubscribes.
- **Segments** - which segment behaved differently from the list average, and how.
- **Lint warnings that mattered** - the one the linter flagged that turned out to be the real problem.

No subscriber data, no email addresses, no names. The pattern, not the person.
