# Learnings - email-marketer

1. **Suppress before you write.** A tired list makes good copy look bad, and the fix is never a better subject line.
2. **The subject, preview and first line are one unit.** Written separately, they read like three different emails.
3. **Judge on clicks.** Opens are inflated by image blocking and privacy features; a subject that opens but never clicks has failed.
4. **A discount in mail one teaches the list to wait.** Put the offer after the proof, unless the offer is the only reason they signed up.
5. **Every sequence needs an exit condition.** Without one, buyers keep getting "still thinking about it".
