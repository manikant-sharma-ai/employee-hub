# Role map - the boundary lines

Thirty-two employees, and the near-misses are where routing actually goes wrong.
This file is the tie-breaker. Read the pair, not the description.

## The pairs that get confused

| Pair | The line |
|---|---|
| `content-writer` vs `social-media-manager` | content-writer writes the words. social-media-manager decides what goes out when, in what format, and handles what comes back. If the ask is "write", it is content-writer even if it will be posted. |
| `graphic-designer` vs `ui-designer` | A surface that is looked at (poster, thumbnail, label) is graphic-designer. A surface that is operated (screen, flow, form, app) is ui-designer. A button's colour on a poster is graphic; a button's states in a product is ui. |
| `brand-strategist` vs `graphic-designer` | Strategy decides what the brand means and to whom. Design executes it on a surface. If the answer changes who you are competing with, it is strategy. |
| `research-analyst` vs `data-analyst` | research-analyst works outside the business - market, competitors, vendors - and ends in a decision memo. data-analyst works on your own numbers and ends in a readout. Mixed job: data-analyst first, then research-analyst to benchmark. |
| `finance-ops` vs `bookkeeper` | bookkeeper records what happened, day by day, with a document behind each entry. finance-ops runs the month: reconciliation, categories, close, exceptions. A missing invoice is bookkeeper; a close that does not tie is finance-ops. |
| `legal-drafter` vs `finance-ops` | Contract wording is legal-drafter. What you invoice under it is finance-ops. A payment term dispute starts at legal-drafter and lands back on finance-ops. |
| `support-agent` vs `social-media-manager` | A customer with a problem is support-agent, whatever channel it arrived on. A comment that is not a support case is social-media-manager. The same WhatsApp thread can be both; split it. |
| `sales-outreach` vs `email-marketer` | Outbound to strangers you are trying to win is sales-outreach. A list that opted in and gets a newsletter is email-marketer. Same tool, different consent, different law. |
| `qa-tester` vs `ai-engineer` | Does it work as specified is qa-tester. Make the thing exist is ai-engineer, frontend, backend. QA after a build, not instead of one. |
| `translator-localiser` vs `content-writer` | New words in another language with idiom and tone is translator-localiser. Writing in English is content-writer. Never let a translator "improve" the argument, and never let a writer translate. |
| `product-manager` vs everyone | If the question is "should this exist", product-manager. If the question is "make it exist well", the specialist. |
| `presentation-designer` vs `graphic-designer` | A deck that argues to a decision is presentation-designer. Slides that just need to look right for a client pitch are graphic-designer. |

| `sales-outreach` vs `partnerships` | Both write to strangers and both end in revenue. sales-outreach sells to the buyer who pays us. partnerships recruits the reseller, distributor or integrator who sells for us - and models that partner's margin before the first call. |
| `data-analyst` vs `analytics-instrumenter` | data-analyst answers a question from data that exists. analytics-instrumenter decides what gets captured, under what name, before the code ships. If the ask is "why did this happen", data-analyst. If it is "we will never be able to tell", analytics-instrumenter. |
| `pricing-analyst` vs `finance-ops` | The price is pricing-analyst's: the floor, the plans, the discount ladder. What is invoiced under it, and whether it reconciles, is finance-ops. A discount dispute is a pricing question only if the policy is wrong. |
| `procurement` vs `finance-ops` | procurement chooses the vendor, negotiates and raises the PO. finance-ops pays, reconciles and closes. A three-way match failure starts at procurement and is recorded by bookkeeper. |
| `pr-writer` vs `content-writer` | A story written to be published by us is content-writer. A story written so that someone else publishes it is pr-writer - and the deliverable is a pitch and a named journalist, not an article. |
| `hr-recruiter` vs `hiring-manager` | Everything up to the signed offer is hr-recruiter: the hiring scorecard, the JD, screening, the interview kit, the offer. Everything after it is hiring-manager: onboarding, the 30-60-90, reviews, the PIP, the raise conversation. "We need someone" is hr-recruiter; "they start Monday" is hiring-manager. |
| `hiring-manager` vs `finance-ops` | hiring-manager decides the outcome, the review and the plan. What the person is paid, and whether PF and settlement tie, is finance-ops. A raise approved by hiring-manager becomes a payroll change in finance-ops. |
| `compliance-watch` vs `legal-drafter` | compliance-watch knows what must be filed, held and proved, and whether the evidence exists. legal-drafter writes the document - the policy, the contract, the notice. "Draft the POSH policy" is legal-drafter; "is our POSH committee constituted and is the evidence on file" is compliance-watch. |
| `compliance-watch` vs `finance-ops` | compliance-watch owns the date, the owner and the acknowledgement. finance-ops and bookkeeper prepare and file the return, and the CA signs it. A late GSTR-3B is a finance-ops failure to file and a compliance-watch failure to warn. |
| `employee-hub-router` vs `chief-of-staff` | The router answers "which employee owns this job". The chief of staff answers "what is this week for, who committed to what, and did it happen". One job, one employee, once - versus the operating rhythm across all of them. |
| `chief-of-staff` vs `product-manager` | The chief of staff makes sure a decision is dated, owned and reviewed. product-manager makes the decision about what gets built. Never let the rhythm role choose the product, and never let the product role run the week. |
| `growth-marketer` vs `performance-ads-buyer` | growth-marketer decides where the next rupee goes and what the experiment must prove, at the level of channel and segment. performance-ads-buyer builds the campaign, paces the spend and reads out the ad sets. "Which channel" is growth; "why did this ad set stop spending" is the buyer. |
| `performance-ads-buyer` vs `graphic-designer` | The buyer specifies the variants - hook, angle, proof, format - and reads the result. graphic-designer makes them. A creative that fails is a design note for the designer and a hypothesis for the buyer. |
| `growth-marketer` vs the channel specialists | seo-specialist, social-media-manager and email-marketer own their craft and their channel's execution. growth-marketer owns the allocation between them and the experiment that decides whether the channel deserves more. If the ask is "make this channel work", it is the specialist; if it is "should we keep funding this channel", it is growth. |

## The sequences that actually recur

1. **Launch** - brand-strategist (position) → content-writer (words) → graphic-designer (visuals) → social-media-manager (calendar) → email-marketer (sequence) → data-analyst (readout).
2. **Hire** - hr-recruiter (scorecard, JD, screening, offer) → legal-drafter (offer terms) → hiring-manager (onboarding, 30-60-90, review cycle) → finance-ops (payroll setup).
3. **New product** - product-manager (PRD, slice) → ui-designer (screens) → qa-tester (test plan) → content-writer (launch copy) → seo-specialist (page).
4. **Money trouble** - data-analyst (what the numbers say) → bookkeeper (what is recorded) → finance-ops (close and exceptions) → legal-drafter (only if terms are the cause).
5. **Growth review** - growth-marketer (constraint, allocation) → performance-ads-buyer (build, pace, read out) → content-writer and graphic-designer (message and creative) → analytics-instrumenter (events) → data-analyst (what the numbers say).
6. **Entering a new language market** - research-analyst (is it worth it) → translator-localiser (the words) → ui-designer (layout that survives longer strings) → seo-specialist (local search).

## What the hub cannot do, and who to point at

| Ask | The honest answer |
|---|---|
| Sign a contract, file a GST return, certify accounts | A chartered accountant or lawyer. The hub drafts; a professional signs. |
| Take a photograph, shoot a product video | A photographer or videographer. `graphic-designer` and `video-editor` direct and specify; they do not capture. |
| Make a payment, send a campaign to 40,000 people | A connected payment or ESP account, in your name, with your approval on the first run. |
| Run paid ads autonomously | `performance-ads-buyer` structures, paces and reads out; a person holding the ad account approves every change and every rupee. No employee here spends money on its own. |
| Replace a manager's judgement on people | `hr-recruiter` structures the decision; it does not make it. |
