# Learnings - employee-hub-router

Rules that came out of routing the hub's own work.

1. **"Launch" is not a role, it is a sequence.** Every "we are launching X, who do I give it to" request is four to six employees in order. Answering with one name produces a launch with no calendar and no follow-up.
2. **The surface decides before the topic does.** The same subject - a Diwali offer - is graphic-designer for the creative, content-writer for the caption, email-marketer for the send, and data-analyst for whether it worked.
3. **A request with two surfaces is two employees.** Averaging them into one produces a compromise nobody asked for.
4. **Say the missing connector in the first message.** The most common disappointment is discovering after the work that nothing could be published.
5. **"Can it run on its own?" is a scheduling question, not a routing question.** Point at `docs/scheduling.md`, and say what the schedule needs to reach before it runs unattended.
