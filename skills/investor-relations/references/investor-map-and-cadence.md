# Investor map and cadence

Not every investor wants the same thing, and treating them identically wastes the
founder's time on the ones who want less while under-serving the ones who decide.

## The tiers

| Tier | Who | Gets | Cadence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | The largest cheque, usually on the board | Monthly update, monthly call, board pack | Monthly | Founder |
| Board observer | Contractual right, no vote | Board pack, observer call | Monthly | Founder |
| Institutional | Funds with a position to manage | Monthly update, quarterly call | Monthly + quarterly | Founder |
| Angel, active | Writes cheques and opens doors | Monthly update, ad-hoc calls | Monthly | Founder or IR |
| Angel, passive | Small cheque, no involvement | Quarterly update | Quarterly | IR |
| Strategic | Corporate investor or partner | Monthly update, filtered for their interest | Monthly | Founder |
| Prospective | In conversation, not invested | Nothing from this list | - | `fundraising-analyst` |

Say the cadence out loud when the money lands. An investor who expects quarterly
and receives monthly is delighted; the reverse is a complaint.

## Information rights are contractual

Read the signed documents before choosing a cadence. Typical obligations:

| Obligation | Typical shape | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly/quarterly MIS | Within 30-45 days of month end | A date, not a preference |
| Annual audited accounts | Within 6 months of year end | The CA's calendar |
| Board meetings | Once a quarter, notice period stated | The notice period is the deadline |
| Observer rights | Notice of every board meeting | Missing one is a breach |
| Consent matters | New debt, related-party transactions, ESOP changes | Ask before acting, not after |
| Reserved matters | Listed in the SHA | Counsel reads it, not this role |

Where the documents are unclear, `legal-drafter` and counsel answer. This role
maintains the calendar those answers produce.

## The annual rhythm

| Month | Investor-facing work |
|---|---|
| Every month | Update on the fixed date; question log reviewed |
| Quarterly | Board pack, quarterly call, data room refresh |
| Half-year | Cap table reconciliation, ESOP grant status |
| Annual | Audited accounts, AGM, statutory filings, update archive reviewed |
| On a raise | Data room sweep, FAQ refresh, quiet period rules |

## The intro etiquette

- **Ask before introducing.** One line to both sides: who they are, why they should meet, what each gets.
- **Never introduce an investor to a customer** without both agreeing.
- **Never pass an investor's question to a customer or an employee** with names attached.
- **A warm intro is a favour with a cost.** Track who gave what, and return it.

## The quiet periods

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Active raise | No forward-looking numbers to anyone outside the round; the deck is the document |
| Material event - large customer loss, litigation, key departure | Tell the lead before the monthly update, same week |
| Insider information | Do not share with an investor who is not bound, and do not trade on it |

The specifics of what is material, and who may be told, are legal questions.
`legal-drafter` and counsel decide; this role keeps the list of who was told and when.

## The investor who goes quiet

| Signal | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No reply for three updates | Busy, or writing the position down | Keep sending; do not chase |
| Replies only when something is wrong | Managing risk | A short call; ask what would reassure them |
| Asks for a call after a bad month | Wants to understand the plan | Take it within 48 hours, with the numbers |
| Starts asking about runway repeatedly | Worried about the next round | Bring the plan and the milestone, not reassurance |

## Anti-patterns

- **The same email to everyone.** The lead's questions are different from the angel's.
- **Forgetting the contractual cadence** in favour of a convenient one.
- **A quarterly investor who gets the monthly and never replies.** You are spending attention for nothing.
- **Introducing investors to each other** without permission.
- **Treating a prospective investor as an existing one.** Different rules, different documents.
- **No record of who was told what.** The first question in any dispute.
