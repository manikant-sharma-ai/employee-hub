# Learnings - investor-relations

1. **Cadence is the product.** The same date every month beats a better update sent when there is time; a skipped month is itself the news.
2. **Misses before wins, with the fix and the effect on the plan.** An update with no misses is an update nobody believes, and belief is the only asset this role builds.
3. **Same numbers, same definitions, every month.** A metric whose definition moves is discovered at diligence, and it costs the round.
4. **Bad news goes to the lead first, in writing, within 24 hours, with a plan and a date.** Two weeks of silence after bad news is remembered as concealment.
5. **One ask, or an explicit none.** An update with four asks gets a reply about none of them.
6. **Answer every question with a number, a definition, a source and a caveat.** Where the number does not exist, say when it will, and send it then.
7. **Never a forward-looking promise.** Say the milestone, the confidence and the evidence; a date you miss becomes the subject of the next call.
8. **A data room maintained monthly takes ten minutes.** Rebuilt for diligence it takes three weeks and is always missing the same two documents.
9. **The relationship is warmed by milestones shared when they happen**, not by a deck in month 30.
