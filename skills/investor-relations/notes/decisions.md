# Decisions

Schema only. No decisions yet. A decision goes here when it would otherwise be
re-argued next month, with the reason that made it right at the time.

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

## What belongs here

- The update date and the format, once agreed.
- The tiering: who gets monthly, who gets quarterly.
- The metric definitions used in every update, and any change to them.
- The disclosure rule for a material event, once counsel has answered.

## What does not

- A valuation, exit or return statement - never this role's decision.
- A legal conclusion about disclosure. That is counsel's, recorded here only as an instruction.
- Restatements of this skill's standards. The skill is the standard.
