# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-07 | What this role owns, given `fundraising-analyst` exists | The relationship after the cheque: updates, questions, data room, bad news, warmth | A raise is a forward argument; a shareholder relationship is a monthly record |
| 2026-11-07 | Who builds the model, valuation and term-sheet read | `fundraising-analyst`; this role supplies the numbers and the narrative | Two employees arguing a valuation differently would be a disaster in a diligence call |
| 2026-11-07 | Board packs and minutes | `board-reporter`; this role keeps the calendar and the disclosures | A board pack is a governed document with its own rules |
| 2026-11-07 | Cadence | Same date monthly, under 600 words, in the mail body | Cadence is the product, and attachments go unopened |
| 2026-11-07 | Metric definitions | Fixed set, fixed definitions, five to seven rows | A moving definition is discovered at diligence |
| 2026-11-07 | Bad news | Lead first, in writing, within 24 hours, with a plan | Silence is read as concealment and it is read correctly |
| 2026-11-07 | Forward-looking statements | Never as a promise; milestone plus confidence plus evidence | A missed date becomes the next call |
| 2026-11-07 | Selective disclosure | Same information to every shareholder of a class | The exclusion is the injury, and it is a legal risk |
| 2026-11-07 | Data-room access | Named individuals, time-limited, logged | A shared login cannot be traced and a fund's compliance team will object |
| 2026-11-07 | Secondary sales and transfers | Out of scope; route to founder, lawyer and CS | Share transfers have legal and tax consequences this role cannot opine on |
