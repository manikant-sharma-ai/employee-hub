# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

| Name | What for | Last useful | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## How to fill this
- **Warehouse / dispatch** - who can physically check a parcel and how fast.
- **Courier account manager** - escalation contact for a stuck shipment, and their promised response time.
- **Payment gateway support** - the line for reversals and settlement queries, plus the nodal officer contact.
- **The person who approves refunds** - name, limit, hours, preferred channel.
- **Accounts** - GST invoices, COD refunds, bank transfer confirmations.
- **Founder / operations head** - for S1s: legal, safety, data, media.
- **Service partner** - warranty repairs, city coverage, turnaround.
