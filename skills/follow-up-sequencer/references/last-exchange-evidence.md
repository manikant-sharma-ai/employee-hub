# The last exchange: finding the one line the follow-up hangs on

A follow-up without a reference is a reminder, and reminders get ignored. This file is how the reference is
found, what counts as evidence, and what to do when the evidence is not there.

## Where the evidence lives, in order of quality

| Source | What it gives | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| The call notes, written the same day | The requirement, the objection, the decision-maker, the date | Highest |
| The email thread | The exact words, the attachments sent, what was promised and by whom | High |
| The WhatsApp thread | The informal reality - price sensitivity, urgency, the real gatekeeper | High |
| The quote or proposal PDF | The configuration, the price, the validity date | High, but only the commercial half |
| The meeting summary or calendar note | The attendees and the agenda | Medium |
| The CRM note field | Whatever the owner typed later | Low-medium, often a summary of a summary |
| The owner's memory | Useful, and must be labelled as memory | Lowest, and it is where invented detail enters |

Read in that order. When the CRM note and the thread disagree, the thread is the record.

## What a reference line looks like

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Following up on our discussion" | "You mentioned the 500-unit order needs to land before your Chennai opening on 12 December" |
| "As per our last mail" | "You asked whether the 3-year warranty covers the motor - it does, and here is the clause" |
| "Kindly revert" | "Is the 2% freight included in the ₹1,240 you approved, or should I send it as a separate line?" |
| "Any update?" | "Your partner was reviewing the annual plan on Saturday - did that happen, or should I send a one-page summary he can read instead?" |
| "Just checking in" | "The price I sent holds until 30 November. After that the steel rate changes and I would have to revise it." |

The test: could this message only have been sent to this buyer? If it could go to any buyer, it is a template.

## The four things a reference line should carry

1. **The specific noun.** The model, the quantity, the site, the city, the person, the date. Specificity is the
   proof of attention.
2. **The unresolved thing.** What was left open at the end of the last exchange - a question, an objection, a
   document, a decision by someone else.
3. **The new element.** Something that was not in the last message: an answer, a document, a date, a change in
   availability, a relevant example from another customer.
4. **The single ask.** One question or one decision, answerable in one line.

## Building the deal's conversation card

For each live deal, keep five lines. They fit in the CRM note field or the plan in `assets/follow-up-plan.md`.

```
Deal:        <id> · <stage> · ₹<value> · owner <name>
Said:        <the requirement in the buyer's own words, one line>
Open:        <what is unresolved right now>
Objection:   <the real one, if it was stated - price, trust, timing, a competitor, an internal blocker>
Next:        <the action, the date, the channel>
```

If the "Said" line cannot be filled from evidence, the deal has no reference and the first task is a question to
the owner, not a message to the buyer.

## Reading an objection correctly

| What they said | What it usually means | What the follow-up does |
|---|---|---|
| "The price is high" | They have not seen the value, or they have a cheaper quote | Send the comparison, not a discount |
| "Send me details" | Polite deferral, or a genuine internal forward | Send one page, then ask who else will read it |
| "We will get back" | Nobody owns the decision yet | Ask who signs, and by when |
| "Call me next month" | A real budget or timing constraint | Note the date, do not call before it |
| Silence after a quote | The quote is being compared, or it was wrong | Ask one question about fit, not about the decision |
| "Our current vendor is fine" | No trigger to switch yet | Ask what would have to change |

## When the evidence is missing

Say so, in this order:

1. **Ask the owner one question** - "what did they say they needed?" One question, not five.
2. **Write a shorter message** that references only what is certain - the date of the conversation, the document
   sent, the price validity.
3. **Never fill the gap with plausible detail.** An invented reference is discovered the moment the buyer reads
   it, and it costs the deal and the relationship.

## Anti-patterns

- A CRM note that says "called, positive" - unusable as evidence, and it is the most common note in the world.
- Reading only the last email in a thread when the requirement was in the second.
- Quoting back the buyer's objection in a way that sounds like a scoreboard.
- Referencing a detail the buyer considers confidential in a forwarded email.
- Using the reference line to prove you were right in the last exchange. The follow-up is about their decision,
  not your accuracy.
- Copying the reference line across deals in the same segment: three buyers comparing notes will find it.
