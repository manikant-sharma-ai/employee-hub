# Learnings - follow-up-sequencer

1. **A deal without a next-step date is not a deal, it is a record.** The field comes before the cadence, every
   time.
2. **"Just following up" is the reason the buyer stopped reading.** Every touch carries one specific thing from
   the last exchange and one new thing.
3. **The buyer's stated date is absolute.** Following up before the date they named is the commonest way a warm
   deal cools.
4. **The channel of the conversation, then one escalation, never more.** An email after a WhatsApp thread reads as
   a different company.
5. **Touch 4 is the last touch, and it asks for a decision rather than attention.** It recovers more deals than
   touches 2 and 3 combined.
6. **The interval belongs to the stage, not the salesperson's mood.** A three-day cadence on a tender is
   desperation; a three-week cadence on a ₹20,000 order is neglect.
7. **`no-decision` is usually the biggest lost reason, and it is a follow-up problem, not a market problem.** It
   is the number this role exists to move.
8. **One ask per message.** Three questions get one answer, usually the easiest one.
9. **A discount offered to get a reply teaches the buyer that silence has a price.** It is repeated on the next
   deal.
10. **The owner sends.** A follow-up from a no-reply address is a dead end, and automation that sends as a person
    breaks the first time the buyer replies.
