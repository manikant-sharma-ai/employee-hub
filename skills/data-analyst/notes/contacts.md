## How to fill this
- **Source system owners** - who can explain why the Shopify export counts cancelled orders, or why the ledger has two freight lines.
- **The accountant** - GST treatment, returns provisioning, what a ledger code actually means.
- **The person who maintains the spreadsheet nobody else understands** - and whether they will document it.
- **Each department head** - the metric they are measured on, and the definition they use. This is how definition fights get settled.
- **A second analyst or reviewer** - someone who will recompute one number independently before a board pack.
