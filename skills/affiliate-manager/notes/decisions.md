# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | Who owns affiliate, given `partnerships` and `influencer-outreach` exist | `affiliate-manager` owns performance-paid partners at scale; `partnerships` owns negotiated distribution deals; `influencer-outreach` owns paid creators | Three different economics: commission, contract, fee |
| 2026-11-06 | Attribution rule | Last click, 30-day cookie, published | The rule that can be enforced and explained beats the rule that is fairest in theory |
| 2026-11-06 | Coupon ownership | Brand-issued codes only, one per partner | The only model that survives an audit |
| 2026-11-06 | Repeat orders | 0-5%, never the first-order rate | The brand does the retention work, not the partner |
| 2026-11-06 | Coupon and cashback sites | Admit deliberately at 4%, full-price only, or exclude | Left to default they become the programme and eat the margin |
| 2026-11-06 | Clawback window | 90 days, published before joining | Retroactive rules end a programme's reputation |
| 2026-11-06 | Incrementality reporting | Estimate as a range with the method named, never as a single number | It cannot be measured exactly; pretending otherwise is the failure |
