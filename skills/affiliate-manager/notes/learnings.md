# Learnings - affiliate-manager

1. **Attributed revenue is not incremental revenue.** Every read-out carries the estimate, the method and a range - the second number is the one that justifies the spend.
2. **New customers and repeat customers need different rates.** A full rate on every repeat turns the programme into a permanent discount with a tracking fee.
3. **Brand-issued codes only.** Any code you did not issue is either a leak or a partner taking credit for a sale that was already made.
4. **Returns are netted before payout.** A 25% return rate on a 15% commission is a channel that reports growth while losing money.
5. **The rate comes from the margin ceiling, never from a competitor's page.** Their margin is not ours.
6. **Coupon and cashback sites are admitted deliberately or not at all.** Left to default, they become the programme.
