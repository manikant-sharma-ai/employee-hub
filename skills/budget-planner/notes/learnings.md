# Learnings - budget-planner

1. **The base case is the budget.** Restate last year's actuals into plan heads, mark one-offs
   and reconcile to the bank before projecting a rupee; a plan built on a mis-cut base is wrong
   in every month, not just the first.
2. **Phase monthly, and grow the level rather than the shape.** Seasonality is an index taken
   from actuals; multiplying a growth rate into a festive month is how a plan overstates the
   year by the ramp.
3. **Cash and P&L are two documents.** The lowest closing balance and the month it lands in is
   the number that decides whether the year is fundable - profit never shows it.
4. **Cost behaviour, not cost heads, answers "what if volume moves".** Fixed, variable and step,
   tagged on every line, is what makes the plan usable in a meeting.
5. **Every structural variance gets an owner and an action; timing variances do not trigger a
   reforecast.** Conflating the two either cuts a real cost for a temporary problem or gives a
   real problem three more months of runway.
6. **Measure forecast error or repeat it.** Revenue error at three months out and cost error at
   one month out, written down with the cause, is the only way the next plan starts ahead.
7. **CTC is not cost.** The on-cost multiplier (PF, ESI, gratuity, bonus, leave) is 15-20% and
   is the most commonly omitted number in a small-business plan.
