# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | What this role owns, given `finance-ops`, `mis-reporter` and `pricing-analyst` | The forward plan: driver build, phasing, cash budget, variance method, reforecast triggers | Those three report or record; a budget is the only one that decides before the money moves |
| 2026-11-12 | Budget horizon | One financial year, with a rolling 12-month view from month three | A longer plan is fiction in a small business; a shorter one cannot fund a season |
| 2026-11-12 | Build method | Driver-based by default, zero-based on 10% of lines, committed for contracts | Incremental budgeting inherits last year's waste forever |
| 2026-11-12 | Cash budget | Always produced, never optional, even when the ask is only a P&L | Profitable insolvency is the failure this role exists to prevent |
| 2026-11-12 | Reforecast cadence | On triggers, not monthly by habit; 2-4 a year is healthy | A monthly reforecast makes the plan lose authority |
| 2026-11-12 | Who approves | The founder; this role never approves spend or releases a payment | A planner who also approves has no independent check |
| 2026-11-12 | Prices and tax | Inputs from `pricing-analyst` and the CA, never decided here | Margin consequence is this role's business; the price and the tax position are not |
| 2026-11-12 | Confidentiality | No named salaries, bank details or customer commercial terms in the repo or notes | The repo is public; aggregates and roles only |
