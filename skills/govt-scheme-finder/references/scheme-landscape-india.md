# The scheme landscape in India

Hundreds of schemes exist. Six families cover almost everything a small business can actually use.
Search the family, not the list.

**Every number in this file is a starting point, not a citation.** Rates, ceilings and windows are
revised by notifications, budgets and state orders. Anything quoted here must be confirmed at the
official source named in the row before it goes into a brief. A scheme brief without a verification
date is a rumour.

## Family 1 - Entity and registration linked

| Scheme family | What it gives | Who it fits | Verify at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Udyam registration** | The gateway to almost every MSME benefit; free, self-declared | Any micro, small or medium enterprise | udyamregistration.gov.in |
| **Udyam Assist** | Registration route for informal micro units through a bank or an agency | Unregistered micro units without GST | The bank or the Udyam Assist portal |
| **Startup India / DPIIT recognition** | Tax and compliance benefits, self-certification, fund access, procurement relaxations | Innovative startups within the age limit, with a scalable model | startupindia.gov.in |
| **GeM seller registration** | Access to government procurement, with MSE preferences | Any seller of a listed product or service | gem.gov.in |
| **TReDS** | Discounting of receivables from large buyers | MSMEs selling to corporates and PSUs | The TReDS platform, via the bank |

Rules that decide this family: the **classification is by investment in plant and machinery and by
turnover**, both of which were revised upward in the 2025-26 Union Budget - confirm the current
limits on the Udyam portal before telling a business which band it is in. **A trader is treated
differently from a manufacturer or a service provider** in most benefit schemes, and this is the
single most common disqualifier. And a Udyam registration is **not retroactive for benefits** -
many schemes require it to exist before the investment or the activity.

## Family 2 - Credit and guarantee

| Scheme family | What it gives | Who it fits | Verify at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CGTMSE** | Credit guarantee so a lender can give a collateral-free term or working-capital loan | Micro and small enterprises with a viable proposal and no collateral | cgtmse.in and the lender |
| **Mudra loans** | Small-ticket loans in bands, from working capital to small expansion | Very small and micro units, including service and trade | The bank; mudra.org.in |
| **Stand-Up India** | Loans in the ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore band for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs | First-time entrepreneurs in manufacturing, services, trading, agri-allied | standupmitra.in and the bank |
| **PMEGP** | Margin money subsidy on a new project, with training | New micro enterprises, one per family, in manufacturing or service | kviconline.gov.in |
| **State interest subvention** | A subsidy on the interest for a period, on a specified loan | Units in the state's priority sectors | The state's MSME department |
| **SIDBI and NABARD lines** | Refinance and direct lending, often bundled with a guarantee | Specific sectors and clusters | sidbi.in, nabard.org |

The pattern: **the lender is the channel, and the scheme decides the terms.** A business does not
apply to CGTMSE - it applies for a loan and the lender takes the guarantee. So the useful output of
this family is a sentence the founder can say to the banker, and the two documents that make it
easy to say yes.

## Family 3 - Capital, machinery and technology

| Scheme family | What it gives | Who it fits | Verify at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **State industrial policy incentives** | Capital subsidy on plant and machinery, SGST reimbursement or retention, stamp duty and registration exemption, power tariff concession, employment subsidy | New units, expansions and sometimes diversification, in the state's thrust sectors | The state's industries department and the District Industries Centre |
| **Technology upgradation funds** | A percentage of machinery cost, on a defined list | Manufacturing units in the covered sectors | The sector ministry or the nodal agency |
| **Textile upgradation (ATUFS family)** | Subsidy on eligible machinery, with caps | Textile and garment manufacturing | The textile ministry and the nodal bank |
| **Food processing (PMFME family)** | Credit-linked subsidy on a project, with a cap | Micro food processing units, individual or group | The state nodal agency for PMFME |
| **ZED, Lean and quality certification** | Part of the certification cost, and in some cases a handholding grant | Small manufacturers willing to go through the assessment | The MSME ministry's scheme portal |
| **Design and IPR support** | Part of design project cost, and reimbursement of patent, trademark and GI costs | Units filing for IP or commissioning a design intervention | The MSME ministry's scheme portal |
| **Cluster development (MSE-CDP, SFURTI)** | Common facility, infrastructure and artisan cluster support | Groups of units, industry associations, NGOs | The state directorate and the MSME ministry |

Two rules that decide this family. First, **the investment usually has to happen after the
application is sanctioned, or within a stated window** - a machine bought last year is often
ineligible, and that is the most expensive misunderstanding in the whole landscape. Second,
**capital subsidy is paid after commissioning and utilisation**, so the founder funds the machine
first and is reimbursed later. Any plan that treats the subsidy as the down payment is a cash-flow
plan built on a promise.

## Family 4 - Export

| Scheme family | What it gives | Who it fits | Verify at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IEC (Importer-Exporter Code)** | The licence to export at all | Any exporter | DGFT |
| **RoDTEP** | Remission of embedded taxes and duties on exported goods, by HS code rate | Goods exporters | DGFT and the customs notification |
| **Duty Drawback** | Refund of customs duty on inputs, where RoDTEP does not cover it | Goods exporters | Customs and the drawback schedule |
| **Advance Authorisation** | Duty-free import of inputs against an export obligation | Manufacturers with a known export order | DGFT |
| **EPCG** | Zero-duty import of capital goods against an export obligation | Manufacturers investing in export capacity | DGFT |
| **Market Access Initiative** | Part of the cost of an international fair, a buyer-seller meet or a market study | Exporters going to a new market | The export promotion council and the commerce ministry |
| **RCMC membership** | Registration with the export promotion council, which gates most benefits | Exporters in a council's product group | The relevant EPC |

The disqualifier here is documentation, not eligibility: **an export benefit is claimed on the
shipping bill and the e-BRC**, and a mismatch between the invoice, the shipping bill and the bank
realisation is what loses it. The IEC, the RCMC and a clean document trail matter more than which
scheme is chosen.

## Family 5 - Sector and location

| Family | Examples | Where the money usually is |
|---|---|---|
| Textiles and apparel | Machinery subsidy, skill support, cluster common facility | Machinery and cluster infrastructure |
| Food processing | Project subsidy, cold chain, certification | Plant and machinery, cold chain |
| Pharma and chemicals | Testing lab support, quality certification | Compliance infrastructure |
| Electronics and EV | Capital subsidy, component incentives, state-specific packages | Large capital, usually above micro scale |
| Solar and energy efficiency | Rooftop solar subsidy, energy audit support, efficient equipment | The asset itself, with a metered benefit |
| Artisan and craft | Toolkits, training, market linkage, cluster support | Working capital and market access |
| Aspirational districts, NE, hill states, J&K, islands | Enhanced rates under several central schemes | Location itself is the eligibility |

Location is the most underused lever. A unit in a designated backward or aspirational district, or
in a north-eastern or hill state, frequently gets a higher rate under the same central scheme and a
separate state package on top.

## Family 6 - Procurement and market access

| Family | What it gives | The rule that matters |
|---|---|---|
| **GeM MSE preference** | A share of government procurement reserved for MSEs, with sub-targets for women and SC/ST owned units | Registration and the correct Udyam linkage decide whether the preference applies |
| **Public procurement policy** | Price preference and tender-set-aside for MSEs | The 25% MSE target and the sub-targets are policy, not a guarantee of orders |
| **Export promotion councils** | Buyer meets, catalogues, market intelligence, MAI routing | Membership is the entry ticket |
| **Industry associations and clusters** | Shared infrastructure, common testing, collective bargaining on inputs | Cluster schemes route through an association, not an individual unit |

## How to search, in this order

1. **Purpose** - what is being funded: a machine, a shed, working capital, a certification, a market
   entry, a patent.
2. **Activity** - manufacturer, service provider, trader, exporter, agri-allied.
3. **Scale** - micro, small or medium, by the current Udyam definition.
4. **Location** - state, district, and whether it is a designated category.
5. **Status** - new unit, expansion, diversification, or existing.

Then read the scheme's own guidelines, not a summary of them. A scheme's definition section is where
most applications die: "new unit", "substantial expansion", "eligible fixed capital investment" and
"date of commencement of commercial production" each have a definition that decides the case.

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Cost |
|---|---|
| Starting from a list of schemes | Forty names, none checked against the facts |
| Quoting a rate from a blog or a video | A number the DIC will contradict |
| Assuming a past purchase qualifies | Most capital schemes require prior sanction |
| Treating a subsidy as the down payment | The founder funds the asset; the money comes after commissioning |
| Ignoring the state scheme | Often larger than the central one, and less competitive |
| Missing the Udyam date rule | Registered after the investment, and the benefit is gone |
| Chasing every scheme | Effort spread thin, and the good ones get a rushed file |
