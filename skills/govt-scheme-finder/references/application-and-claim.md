# Application, submission and claim

The application is the visible part. The claim is where the money actually arrives, and
where most businesses lose it.

## The sequence

Several schemes depend on an earlier one, so order matters:

1. **The registration** - Udyam, GST, IEC, DPIIT recognition, factory licence. Most
   incentives cannot be applied for without these, and the date on them is itself an
   eligibility condition in several schemes.
2. **The certificate of eligibility or the loan sanction** - the bank's sanction, the
   guarantee cover, the state's eligibility certificate.
3. **The investment** - the asset has to be bought and paid for on the terms the scheme
   requires, with the invoices in the right name.
4. **The claim** - filed within the window after the investment, with the evidence the
   scheme specifies.
5. **The disbursement** - the release, which has its own follow-up and its own clock.

Filing out of order is the commonest self-inflicted failure: an asset bought before the
eligibility certificate, or an application filed before the Udyam registration the scheme
requires to have existed at the time of investment.

## The application pack

- **Covering note** - one page: who we are, what we are applying for, the amount sought,
  the scheme and its notification reference, and the list of annexures.
- **The application form** - as the portal or the office prescribes, not a reformatted
  version of it.
- **The project report** - the business, the product, the market, the technical process,
  the investment with quotations, the means of finance, the projections, the employment,
  and the implementation schedule. Keep it factual and consistent with the accounts; an
  optimistic projection that contradicts last year's audited number is a rejection.
- **The financials** - audited statements, ITRs, the CA certificate on turnover and
  investment, and the bank sanction.
- **The statutory documents** - registrations, consents, licences, and the land or lease
  papers.
- **The undertaking** - that the asset is new, that no other benefit has been taken on it,
  that the conditions will be met, that records will be kept.

Every annexure is numbered and appears in the covering note. An annexure the assessor
cannot find is an annexure that does not exist.

## The portal

- **Most of these portals have no API.** A human at a browser, with a login and an OTP,
  is the process. Never claim a submission that was not seen through to an
  acknowledgement.
- **Keep the acknowledgement number and the PDF** the moment it is generated.
- **Watch the size and format limits** - the commonest failure at 11pm on the last day.
- **Save a copy of every field as submitted**, because the portal's own view of an
  application is often less complete than the PDF.
- **Screenshot the status page** at every stage, dated. It is the only record of when
  something moved.

## The consultant boundary

- **The CA certifies** - turnover, investment, the accounts, the tax position. Never
  substitute your own computation for a certificate.
- **An empanelled consultant** may be required by the scheme for a project report or a
  technical appraisal. Say so, name the fee question, and let the founder decide.
- **Never route documents through an unofficial agent** without the founder's explicit
  approval of that person and that portal.
- **The nodal officer is the authority** on an ambiguous clause. Ask in writing; keep the
  reply.

## The claim

- **The claim window is not the application window.** It is shorter, it starts from the
  investment or the disbursement, and missing it forfeits the benefit.
- **The evidence is specific** - the invoice, the payment proof, the installation
  certificate, the Chartered Engineer's certificate, the bank's confirmation.
- **The claim is filed per asset or per period**, not once. Most capital subsidies are
  released in instalments against progress.
- **Follow up in writing, on a date, with the reference number.** A claim that is "in
  process" for nine months is a claim nobody is following.

## Anti-patterns

- **Applying before the registration the scheme requires existed.**
- **Buying the asset before the eligibility certificate**, which is unrecoverable in most
  schemes.
- **A project report written by copying another company's.**
- **Filing on the last day** of the window, when the portal is slow and the CA is on
  leave.
- **Treating the sanction letter as the money.** The claim is the money.
- **No diarised claim date**, which is the single most expensive omission in this field.
