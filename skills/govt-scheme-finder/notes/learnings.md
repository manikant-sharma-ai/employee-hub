# Learnings - govt-scheme-finder

1. **Search by purpose and facts, never by a list.** "Manufacturer, Pune, ₹3.4 crore, wants ₹40 lakh
   of machinery" produces six candidates; "what schemes exist for MSMEs" produces forty names nobody
   acts on.
2. **Verify three facts before every brief: the rate, the window, the district.** They change most
   often, and each one alone turns an eligible business into wasted effort.
3. **The definitions disqualify more than the headline numbers attract.** "New unit", "substantial
   expansion", "manufacturing", "eligible fixed capital investment" each have a definition, and the
   file dies there.
4. **The application comes before the purchase.** A machine bought and then claimed is, in most
   capital schemes, simply not eligible - and that is the most expensive misunderstanding in the
   landscape.
5. **A subsidy is a recovery of cost, not a source of finance.** Plan the funding as if it arrives
   six to eighteen months later; a project that only works with the money on time does not work.
6. **The rejected list is half the value.** A scheme ruled out with a reason stops the same question
   being asked next quarter, and it makes the shortlist credible.
7. **Rank by rupees per person-day discounted by odds.** A ₹6 lakh benefit at 70% odds and eight days
   beats a ₹15 lakh benefit at 20% odds and twenty days.
8. **The consultant is paid for work, not access.** The founder can file free on the portal; a
   success fee with a written scope is fair, a guarantee is a fraud signal.
9. **Disbursement is the easy half.** The utilisation certificate and the retention conditions are
   where money is recovered - so the record-keeping starts on day one of the purchase.
