# Learnings - fundraising-analyst

1. **The model foots or it does not ship.** Closing cash in month 12 equals opening cash plus flows; a model that fails the cash check fails diligence in the first meeting.
2. **Every metric carries its definition.** Half of all diligence arguments are definitional, and the side that defines first controls the conversation.
3. **Dilution is shown at the end of the next round.** The pool top-up moves founder ownership as much as the valuation does, and it is the surprise founders remember.
4. **The preference stack matters more than the headline valuation.** 1x participating at a higher price can be worth less than 1x non-participating at a lower one.
5. **Red flags are named, with the answer, before diligence.** A hidden one is found and reprices the deal; a disclosed one is discounted by half.
6. **A raise is a pipeline.** One live conversation is a hope; fifteen parallel ones create price tension.
