# Learnings - knowledge-base-writer

1. **The queue is the specification.** An article written from the feature list answers the question the product team asked; the ticket's first message is the only reliable brief.
2. **The title decides everything.** A perfect body behind a feature name is never read - the title has to be the phrase the customer types.
3. **Group by question, not by product area.** "Billing, 400 tickets" is unactionable; "where is my refund, 96" is a writing job with a number attached.
4. **Some topics cannot be deflected by writing.** Order status, payment failures and cancellations need a product surface; write the article, but promise a fraction, not a rate.
5. **A step that only works for an admin is the commonest defect.** Every step is tested on a customer account before publishing.
6. **Policies are quoted from the owner, never inferred from a ticket.** One wrong refund window in an article is a promise the business must keep.
7. **Pages are not a result; missing tickets are.** Report the topic's ticket count before and after, with the confounders named.
8. **An article with no review date is already wrong.** The product ships monthly and the help centre is a liability by default.
