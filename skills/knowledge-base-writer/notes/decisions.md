## What belongs here

- The tone ruling: pronouns, formality, whether humour is allowed.
- Which clusters get an article and which get a support macro instead.
- The review cadence per article type, once agreed.
- A retirement ruling, with the redirect target.

## What does not

- Anything a policy owner has not confirmed - that is a question, not a decision.
- Restatements of this skill's standards. The skill is the standard.
