# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-07 | What this role owns, given `support-agent` and `content-writer` exist | The help centre: gap log, articles, structure, deflection, review cycle | A ticket reply closes one conversation; an article closes the next forty |
| 2026-11-07 | Where an article starts | From tickets, in the customer's words, never from the feature list | Feature-first articles are accurate and unfindable |
| 2026-11-07 | Title convention | The search phrase, under 60 characters, no product nouns | The title is the only part most readers see in results |
| 2026-11-07 | Measurement | Ticket count on the topic before and after eight weeks, with confounders named | Page views measure hope, not deflection |
| 2026-11-07 | Topics that need product work | Article written anyway, with the limit stated | The document still removes the follow-up question |
| 2026-11-07 | Duplicates | One canonical article, the other redirected, never two competing pages | Two pages split the ranking and confuse the reader |
| 2026-11-07 | Retiring content | Archive with a dated note, keep the redirect for a year | Old emails and search results still point at it |
| 2026-11-07 | Internal process docs | Out of scope - `sop-writer` (#91, not shipped) | A customer article and an internal SOP have different readers and different tests |
| 2026-11-07 | Review cadence | Three months for how-tos and troubleshooting, six for policy and pricing | The product ships faster than the calendar, so the cycle has to be short |
