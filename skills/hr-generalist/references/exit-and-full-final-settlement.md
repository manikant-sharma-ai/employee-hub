# Exit and Full and Final Settlement

A settlement is arithmetic with rules attached. Show every input, and a human can check it in a minute. Show only a total, and the exit becomes an argument.

## The sequence

1. **The resignation or the decision** - in writing, dated, with the last working day stated.
2. **Notice reconciliation** - served, short-served or waived, and the recovery basis if short-served.
3. **Clearance** - assets returned, access revoked, handover signed off by the receiving manager.
4. **Calculation** - the settlement below, item by item.
5. **Documents** - relieving letter, experience letter, and the statutory documents the person is entitled to.
6. **Payment and proof** - the payment reference, and the payslip for the final month.

## The calculation, line by line

| Line | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Salary for days worked | Monthly gross divided by the days in the month, times days worked | State whether the divisor is 26, 30 or calendar days - and use one consistently |
| Leave encashment | Closing balance times the per-day wage | Only for leave types that are encashable |
| Gratuity | 15 days of last drawn basic plus DA, per completed year of service, after five years of continuous service | Formula: (15 x last basic+DA x years) / 26 |
| Notice recovery | Short-served days times the per-day wage, where the contract allows | Must be provided for in the appointment letter |
| Bonus or incentive due | Per the scheme, pro-rated if the scheme says so | State the scheme reference |
| Recoveries | Loans, advances, asset damage, training bond | Each needs a documented basis and, where required, consent |
| Deductions | Statutory, and only statutory, at exit | Do not net off disputed amounts unilaterally |

Show gross payable, deductions, and net payable as three separate lines. Never present a single net figure.

## Notice period and recovery

The recovery right comes from the contract, not from practice. Check three things before quoting a number: the notice period in the appointment letter, whether the employer may waive it, and whether recovery is stated as a right or left silent. Where it is silent, recovery is a negotiation, and saying so is more useful than pretending otherwise.

Garden leave, where offered, needs its own terms: pay in full, availability, and what the person may and may not do.

## Gratuity, precisely

Eligibility turns on five years of continuous service, with the statutory relaxations for death and disablement. "Continuous service" has a defined meaning that counts certain breaks as service. Where the calculation is close to the boundary, present the arithmetic and route the determination to the CA rather than deciding it.

## Termination, when that is the route

A termination needs the contractual or statutory basis stated, the notice or pay in lieu, the final settlement, and the documents. A termination without a stated basis, or one that follows a PIP that was not run as a process, is the pattern that ends up in front of a labour authority.

## What to hand over

The settlement sheet with every input visible, the clearance checklist, the draft relieving and experience letters, the payment reference to be filled by the payer, and a note of any item that requires a human determination - gratuity continuity, a disputed recovery, or a bond.
