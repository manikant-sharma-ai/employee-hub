# Statutory Compliance - India

The point of this file is the calendar and the register, not a legal opinion. Rates, ceilings and state rules change by notification. Give the rule, name the portal, mark the figure as needing confirmation, and route the final number to the CA or the compliance consultant.

## The obligations that follow headcount

| Obligation | Trigger | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| EPF and MP Act | 20 or more employees | Registration, monthly ECR, employee and employer contribution, UAN per employee |
| ESI Act | 10 or more employees (varies by state and wage ceiling) | Registration, monthly contribution, dispensary mapping, IP registration |
| Professional Tax | State law; threshold varies | Registration, monthly or annual payment, enrolment and salary certificates |
| Labour Welfare Fund | State law | Periodic contribution, often half-yearly |
| Shops and Establishments | State law, usually on any commercial establishment | Registration, prescribed registers, working hours and leave records |
| Payment of Gratuity | 10 or more employees | Accrual from year five of continuous service, on exit |
| Maternity Benefit | 10 or more employees | 26 weeks, creche where the threshold is met, and the register |
| POSH | 10 or more employees | Internal Committee constituted, annual report, policy published |
| Bonus, Minimum Wages, Contract Labour | Sector and headcount dependent | Applicability must be checked against the state and the wage band |

A company at 19 employees is planning for the day it hits 20. That planning is the useful output, not a scramble in the month it happens.

## The monthly calendar shape

| When | What | Owner | Evidence file |
|---|---|---|---|
| By the 15th | PF ECR upload and payment | Payroll or CA | ECR acknowledgement, challan |
| By the 15th | ESI contribution | Payroll or CA | Challan, contribution history |
| State date | Professional Tax | Payroll or CA | Receipt |
| Monthly | Attendance and wage register closed | HR | Signed register |
| Quarterly | TDS return (salary), where applicable | CA | Filing acknowledgement |
| Half-yearly or annual | LWF, returns under Shops and Establishments | Compliance consultant | Receipt, return copy |
| Annually | POSH annual report, gratuity valuation, insurance renewal | HR and CA | Report, valuation |

Build this as a real calendar with dates for the establishment's own state, and put a named human against each row. A calendar with no owner is a wish.

## Registers to keep inspection-ready

- Wage register and attendance register
- Leave register with the ledger behind it
- Register of employees with joining, designation and wage
- Overtime register, where overtime applies
- Deduction and fine register
- Contractor and contract labour register, where contract staff are engaged
- POSH register of complaints, held separately and confidentially

Keep them in the format the state prescribes, in the language required, signed at the stated frequency. A register maintained only when an inspection is announced is worse than none, because it can be shown to have been created after the fact.

## How to write this without overstating

Three sentences do most of the work:

- "The rule is X. The current notification may have changed the ceiling - confirm on the EPFO portal before quoting a figure."
- "This is required at 20 employees. You are at 17, so this is a planning item with a date, not a current obligation."
- "The filing itself must be made on the portal by a person with the credentials. Here are the steps and the evidence to keep."

## What to hand over

The obligation map for this establishment, the dated calendar with owners, the register list with formats, and the gap list ranked by penalty exposure.
