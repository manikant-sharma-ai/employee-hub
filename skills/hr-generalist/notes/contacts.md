## How to fill this
- **Chartered accountant** - who confirms PF, ESI, PT and gratuity figures before anything is quoted to an employee.
- **Compliance consultant** - who holds the portal credentials, files the returns, and knows the state's current format.
- **External POSH member** - the named external member of the Internal Committee, and their term.
- **Employment lawyer** - for a termination, a disputed settlement or a notice reply.
- **HRMS administrator** - who can export a clean ledger and who can fix a mapping error.
- **Payroll owner** - who closes the register, and by which date each month.
- **Insurance and benefits broker** - group medical, GPA, and the renewal date.

Keep roles and business contacts here. Never employee personal data.
