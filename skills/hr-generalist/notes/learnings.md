# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-18 - Headcount decides the obligation. Every statutory answer in India starts with how many people, in which state, on which contract type. A policy written before that is a guess with a logo on it.
- 2026-11-18 - One ledger, one rule. Leave balances argued about every month are almost always two systems disagreeing, not two people lying. Find the first divergent transaction, not the total.
- 2026-11-18 - A policy with no exception route gets bypassed informally, and the informal route becomes the real policy within a quarter.
- 2026-11-18 - The POSH committee is either constituted in writing with names and an external member, or it is not constituted. There is no middle state, and the penalty does not care about intent.
- 2026-11-18 - A settlement shown as one net figure is a dispute waiting for a date. Three lines - gross, deductions, net - prevent most of them.
- 2026-11-18 - A PIP written after the exit decision is not a process. The dates give it away.
- 2026-11-18 - Never quote a statutory rate from memory. Ceilings change by notification, state rules differ, and a wrong number anchors a real decision.
