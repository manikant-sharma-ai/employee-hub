# Learnings - growth-marketer

1. **One constraint at a time.** Fixing five things makes every result unattributable and every lesson unusable.
2. **Blended CAC hides the channel that is losing money.** Channel CAC and payback months are the only numbers worth arguing about.
3. **Marginal return decides budgets, not average return.** The fifth Rs 50,000 in a channel does not return what the first did.
4. **A test that cannot fail is not a test.** The hypothesis and its disproof go in writing before the spend.
5. **Small baseline rates need large samples.** A 12% lift on 400 visitors is noise, and someone will rebuild the page because of it.
6. **Segment before channel.** The same channel behaves like two different channels across segments, and the average describes neither.
7. **Never grow into a broken operation.** More demand into a leaking fulfilment or follow-up step produces churn faster.
