# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-05 | Blended or channel CAC? | Channel CAC and payback months, always | One losing channel can hide behind three good ones |
| 2026-11-05 | How much of the budget goes to challengers? | 20-30%, enough to reach a real sample | Below that the test cannot conclude anything |
| 2026-11-05 | Who executes a campaign? | performance-ads-buyer; this role sets the allocation | Deciding and executing in one place makes the read-out self-serving |
| 2026-11-05 | When is a channel killed? | After four weeks or a powered test, whichever is longer | Weekly noise kills channels that work |
| 2026-11-05 | Message test or media test first? | Message, whenever the promise is unproven | Words are cheaper than reach |
| 2026-11-05 | Build a referral programme below 200 active customers? | No - buy demand and instrument "who introduced you" | A loop needs a base to turn |
