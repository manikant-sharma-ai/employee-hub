# Contacts - growth-marketer

Schema only. No names on day one.

## Format

| Name | Role | Channel or category | What they are good for | Consent to store |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Categories to keep

- Media buyer or agency contact, and what they actually optimise for
- Creative producer for ad variants
- Marketplace account manager (Amazon, Flipkart, IndiaMART)
- Analytics or data person who owns the tracking
- The founder's largest customer, if they agreed to be a reference

## Never stored here

Customer personal data, order-level exports with names and phone numbers, or any
ad account credential.
