# Growth loops and referrals

Paid acquisition resets to zero every month. A loop compounds. The difference
between a business that gets cheaper to grow and one that does not is usually one
working loop.

## The four loops that work for small businesses

| Loop | How it compounds | The metric that shows it working |
|---|---|---|
| Referral | Customers bring customers | Referral share of new customers, and its CAC |
| Content | Pages and videos keep earning traffic | Organic sessions per published piece, after 6 months |
| Community | Members answer each other | Ratio of member answers to staff answers |
| Product | Usage creates exposure | Invites, shared links or exports per user |

## Referral design

A referral programme needs four things, and most have only the first:

1. **A moment of delight.** Ask at the peak - first successful order, first month saved, first problem solved. Not at signup.
2. **A reason to refer.** Money, credit, status, or a genuine favour to a peer. For B2B in India, a warm introduction to a named peer usually beats a cash incentive.
3. **A frictionless mechanism.** One WhatsApp forward with a link, not a dashboard with a code.
4. **A reward that pays on value.** Pay after the referred customer's first payment, not after the click. Otherwise the programme funds fraud.

| Model | Best for | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Two-sided credit | D2C, subscriptions | Credit that is never used is a liability, not a reward |
| Cash per converted referral | High-value B2B | Fraud, and the tax treatment of the payout |
| Status and access | Communities, creators | Needs a real perk, not a badge |
| Introduced-by field only | Relationship-led B2B | Measure it, or it disappears into "word of mouth" |

## Content loops

- **One piece, many surfaces.** A single research piece becomes a page, five posts, an email, a video and a sales asset. See `content-writer` for the words and `seo-specialist` for the search shape.
- **Compounding beats volume.** Twenty pieces that answer real questions outperform two hundred that answer none.
- **Measure at six months.** Content that is judged at week three always looks like a failure.
- **The loop closes when the content is linked internally and cited externally.** Otherwise it is a library nobody enters.

## Community loops

- **The rules decide the community.** Write them before inviting anyone.
- **Answer time is the product.** A question answered in an hour builds a habit; in three days it builds a graveyard.
- **Members must be able to help each other.** A community where only staff answer is a support channel with extra steps.
- **Escalate the sensitive things.** Complaints, safety, and anything about a person go to a human, immediately.

## When a loop is not worth building

| Condition | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Fewer than ~200 active customers | Not enough base for a loop to turn |
| Low repeat frequency | Nobody returns to refer |
| High-ticket, low-volume B2B | One introduction is worth more than a programme |
| No product moment worth sharing | A loop amplifies a mediocre experience |

In those cases the honest answer is: buy demand, and use a manual "who introduced
you" field to learn where the good customers come from.

## The loop audit, once a quarter

| Question | If the answer is no |
|---|---|
| Can a happy customer refer in under two minutes? | Fix the mechanism before the incentive |
| Do we know the referral share of new customers? | Instrument it, or it cannot be managed |
| Is referral CAC lower than paid CAC? | The programme is paying for customers who would have come anyway |
| Does content still earn traffic after six months? | The topics are wrong, not the channel |
| Do members answer members? | It is a broadcast list, not a community |

## Anti-patterns

- **Incentives without a moment.** A referral ask at signup converts nobody.
- **Rewarding clicks.** It buys fraud, and the payouts are real money.
- **Launching a community as a marketing channel.** Without a reason to return, it dies in six weeks.
- **Judging content at week three.** The compounding has not started.
- **Ignoring the tax and payout mechanics** of cash incentives. They are income to the recipient in most cases.
