# One-page layout

The page is read in two minutes by someone who is about to walk into a meeting.
Layout is what makes that possible.

## The fixed frame

```
MIS - week 45, 3-9 November 2026          published Monday 10 Nov, 07:00 IST
Reconciles to Zoho Books to 31 Oct (see note 3)

| Metric          | Actual  | Target  | Last wk | vs tgt | vs last | Flag      |
| Revenue (net)   | 41.2 L  | 45.0 L  | 39.8 L  | -8.4%  | +3.5%   | WATCH     |
| Gross margin    | 31.5%   | 34.0%   | 33.2%   | -7.4%  | -1.7pp  | WATCH     |
| Orders          | 1,820   | 1,700   | 1,740   | +7.1%  | +4.6%   | ON TRACK  |

Notes
1. Revenue: two dealer orders slipped to next week, ₹2.8 L.
2. Margin: cotton price up 6% since September, not yet passed through.
3. Weekly view is dispatch-date; accounts are invoice-date. Gap ~₹0.4 L.

This week's decisions
- Refund rate (4.2%, target 2.0%) - owner: support-agent - decision by Wed
- Cash: receivables over 45 days at ₹9.1 L - owner: collections-agent - by Fri

Data: Google Sheets (export 09 Nov), Zoho Books (P&L, pulled 01 Nov)
```

## Rules of the frame

- **Same order, every week.** The reader learns the shape and notices the change. Reordering is how a page gets skimmed and ignored.
- **Units in the header of the column, not repeated in every cell.** Lakh for money, % for rates, count for counts.
- **One page means one page.** If it spills, cut a metric, not the notes.
- **The date and the period are on the page**, with the pull date of every source.
- **Flags use words, not only colour.** Red-green colour blindness affects roughly one in twelve men; the word carries the meaning.
- **Notes are numbered and referenced from the cell.** "See note 2" beside the margin figure.
- **No charts on the one-pager.** A chart takes a third of the page to say what a number and a clause say in one line. Charts belong in the monthly review.
- **The decisions strip is at the bottom**, two items, with an owner and a date.

## The monthly version

Once a month, the page grows by one section: the trend of each metric over the
last six periods, and the reconciliation to the closed accounts. Still one page
plus one chart page. The weekly page never grows.
