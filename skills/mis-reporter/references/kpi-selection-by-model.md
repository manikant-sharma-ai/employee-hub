# KPI selection by business model

Eight numbers, chosen from the model. Copying another company's dashboard is how a
business ends up optimising a metric that does not pay its bills.

## Trading and distribution

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue (net of returns) | The top line, honestly netted |
| 2 | Gross margin % | Trading lives or dies here |
| 3 | Volume by top line | Mix hides inside the total |
| 4 | Stock cover days | Cash is sitting on the floor |
| 5 | DSO and overdue % | The commonest cash leak |
| 6 | Dead stock value | The commonest silent loss |
| 7 | Freight and last-mile cost per order | Eats margin invisibly |
| 8 | Cash and committed outflows (60 days) | Survival |

Trap: reporting GMV or order value instead of net revenue. In distribution, returns and
damage are 2-5% and they belong in the number.

## Manufacturing

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue and order book | Forward visibility |
| 2 | Contribution per unit or per kg | The only margin that matters |
| 3 | Capacity utilisation | Fixed cost absorption |
| 4 | Yield or scrap % | The hidden margin |
| 5 | On-time-in-full | The reason customers leave |
| 6 | Raw material cover days and price | The largest cost line |
| 7 | Power and fuel per unit | Volatile, controllable |
| 8 | Cash, and working capital days | Survival and growth limit |

Trap: reporting production volume as success while finished-goods stock grows. Volume
that is not shipped is inventory.

## Services and agencies

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue (billed and collected separately) | Billed is not collected |
| 2 | Billable utilisation | The capacity you sell |
| 3 | Realisation per billable hour or per project | Price discipline |
| 4 | Pipeline coverage (3x target) | Next quarter's revenue |
| 5 | Client concentration (top 3 share) | The risk that ends agencies |
| 6 | Delivery margin per engagement | Profitability by job |
| 7 | DSO | Services carry the longest payment terms |
| 8 | Attrition of billable staff | Capacity disappearing |

Trap: utilisation measured on total hours rather than available hours. The number looks
bad, everyone stops believing it, and the page loses authority.

## D2C and ecommerce

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Net revenue after returns | Returns are 8-30% and they are real |
| 2 | Contribution after ads, shipping and payment fees | The only true margin |
| 3 | Orders, AOV, repeat rate | Volume, basket, retention |
| 4 | CAC by channel and blended | Paid scale decisions |
| 5 | RTO and return rate by courier and pin code | The silent killer |
| 6 | Stock cover on hero SKUs | A stock-out on the best seller costs the quarter |
| 7 | Ad spend as % of revenue (MER) | Pacing |
| 8 | Cash and settlement receivables | Marketplaces pay late |

Trap: revenue reported gross of returns and RTO, with ad spend below the line. That page
shows growth and hides a loss.

## SaaS and subscriptions

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MRR and net new MRR | Growth, split into new, expansion, churn |
| 2 | Revenue churn and logo churn | Two different problems |
| 3 | Gross margin (hosting, support) | The model's promise |
| 4 | CAC payback months | How fast growth pays for itself |
| 5 | Net revenue retention | Whether the base grows without new sales |
| 6 | Pipeline coverage and win rate | The sales engine |
| 7 | Burn and runway | Survival |
| 8 | Activation rate (first value) | The leading indicator of churn |

Trap: MRR that includes one-time implementation fees. It inflates growth and understates
churn.

## Restaurant and retail store

| # | Metric | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Net sales by day part | Where the traffic is |
| 2 | Food or gross margin % | Menu engineering |
| 3 | Covers or footfall, and average bill | Volume and basket |
| 4 | Staff cost as % of sales | The largest controllable cost |
| 5 | Wastage and shrinkage | The invisible margin |
| 6 | Delivery aggregator share and commission | Margin dilution |
| 7 | Repeat visits | The only cheap growth |
| 8 | Cash and daily reconciliation | Cash businesses leak |

Trap: aggregator orders counted at menu price while the commission sits in overheads.
The channel looks profitable and is not.

## Choosing when the model is mixed

A business that both manufactures and sells D2C needs both sets, capped at twelve: the
four that decide survival (revenue net, contribution, cash, cover days) plus four from
each half. Cap the page, not the truth - the rest goes in the annexure.
