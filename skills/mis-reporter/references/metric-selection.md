# Metric selection

Five to eight metrics is not a style preference. It is the number an owner can
hold in their head and act on in a week.

## The three tests every metric must pass

1. **The owner test.** There is a named person whose week changes when the number moves. No owner, no metric.
2. **The decision test.** You can name the decision the number would change. "We would hire a second packer" is a decision. "It's good to know" is not.
3. **The trust test.** The number can be produced from one authoritative source, the same way, every week, by someone who is not the person being measured.

A metric that fails one test is a dashboard line, not a page metric.

## The default set for a small Indian business

Adapt, do not copy. Two or three from each column is usually right.

| Question the owner asks | Metric | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Are we selling? | Revenue (net of returns and GST) | higher |
| Are we making money? | Gross margin % | higher |
| Is the money arriving? | Cash collected this period / receivables over 45 days | higher / lower |
| Are we growing? | Orders or new customers | higher |
| Are we keeping them? | Repeat rate, or churn for subscriptions | higher / lower |
| Is delivery holding? | On-time dispatch %, or return rate | higher / lower |
| Is the pipeline real? | Weighted pipeline, or quotes outstanding | higher |
| Are we burning? | Net burn and runway months | lower / higher |

## Leading and lagging

Revenue is lagging - it tells you what already happened. Pipeline, quotes issued,
traffic and enquiries are leading. A page made only of lagging metrics tells the
owner about last month on the day they need to act on next month. Keep at least
two leading indicators.

## Adding and retiring a metric

- **Adding:** it must pass the three tests, have a target, and displace something. The page has a fixed size for a reason.
- **Retiring:** a metric that has been green for six months with no decision attached has done its job. Retire it and note why.
- **Never add a metric in the week it is needed.** A number with no baseline is unreadable; add it, run it quietly for a month, then publish it.

## What does not go on the page

Followers, page views, impressions, app downloads, "engagement", headcount
without a plan, and any number whose only reader is the person who produced it.
