# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | What this role owns, given `data-analyst`, `finance-ops` and `chief-of-staff` all touch reporting | The standing page: fixed cadence, fixed definitions, variance to plan, one decision | An analysis answers a question once; the page answers "are we on plan" every week |
| 2026-11-10 | Meeting and action chasing | `chief-of-staff`; this role supplies page two of that meeting | The artefact is separable from the rhythm, and the split stops two employees running the same review |
| 2026-11-10 | Metric count | Eight to twelve on the page, everything else in annexure | A page that explains everything explains nothing |
| 2026-11-10 | Variance base | The approved plan, not a re-forecast; re-forecast shown as a labelled column | Variance against a moving target is always zero and means nothing |
| 2026-11-10 | Definition changes | Announced in the file, applied from the first period of a month, history restated or marked not comparable | Silent restatement destroys the archive |
| 2026-11-10 | One-offs | Named, dated, tested against last year; recurring ones move to the base | The commonest way a run-rate cost hides |
| 2026-11-10 | Outside packs | Variants of the same workbook, never a second file | Two files become two sets of numbers |
| 2026-11-10 | Confidentiality | No customer names, salaries, balances or figures in the repo or in notes | The repo is public; the numbers are the business |
