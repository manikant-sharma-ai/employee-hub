# Learnings - mis-reporter

1. **The variance is the information; the actual is only the news.** A number with no plan
   beside it cannot answer the only question a reader has, which is "are we ahead or
   behind".
2. **Eight numbers, not forty.** Every metric on the page must sit in the KPI tree and be
   movable by someone in the business; everything else belongs in an annexure nobody is
   asked to read on a Monday.
3. **Two numbers for the same thing is the failure that kills the page.** One workbook,
   one definitions sheet, variants as views - never a second spreadsheet for the board or
   the lender.
4. **A definition change is announced, and history is restated or marked not comparable.**
   A silent improvement to a formula is discovered by an auditor and discredits every other
   number in the file.
5. **Rupee impact on every driver.** "Volume fell" is not a driver until it has a number
   beside it and a decision attached.
6. **The day is the product.** The page on the same morning every week is read; the page
   sent when it is ready stops being checked within a month.
7. **An empty cell is honest; a guessed cell is not.** Missing data is written as missing,
   with the reason and the date the final figure arrives.
8. **Every page ends with one decision or an explicit none.** A page that asks for nothing
   trains the reader to do nothing.
