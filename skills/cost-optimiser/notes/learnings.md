# Learnings - cost-optimiser

1. **Build the inventory from the debit, never from the tool list.** The statement finds the two
   subscriptions nobody remembers; the team's memory finds the ten everyone likes and none of the
   leaks.
2. **Annualise before comparing anything.** A ₹4,000 monthly plan and a ₹48,000 annual plan are the
   same number, and only one of them looks like it on the page.
3. **A seat with no login in 90 days is the cheapest saving in the business.** It needs no migration,
   no negotiation and no vendor conversation - only an admin page and an approver.
4. **Remove the waste before committing to a reserved or annual plan.** Committing first locks in
   exactly the spend the review was meant to remove.
5. **The second cloud account is the finding.** A contractor's old account, a personal account that
   became production - ask how many accounts exist before analysing any of them.
6. **A saving that needs a migration is a project, not a saving.** Put the hours in the row or the
   next review reports the same number again.
7. **Identified and realised are different columns.** Reporting identified savings twice is how this
   role loses the founder's trust in month two.
8. **Normalise vendor names before counting duplicates.** `GOOGLE *GSUITE`, `Google Ireland Ltd` and
   `Google Workspace` are one subscription and three rows.
