# Learnings - cost-optimiser

1. **The inventory is the role.** Every recommendation without a reconciled table is a guess; the difference
   between statements and ledger is itself a finding.
2. **Normalise before you total.** Annual prepay ÷ 12, GST excluded, forex markup included. An unnormalised
   total is wrong in a direction that flatters the review.
3. **Seats bought against seats active is the cheapest saving in every business**, and the SSO log is the only
   honest source for the second number.
4. **The notice period, not the charge date, is the deadline.** A 90-day notice on a March renewal is a
   December decision, and December is when it gets missed.
5. **Divide by the business.** Cost per order or per user turns a rising bill into either growth or waste, and
   the founder remembers the ratio, not the rupee total.
6. **Price the switch, not just the saving.** A saving smaller than the migration hours is a loss with a
   spreadsheet attached.
7. **Wave 1 funds the credibility for wave 3.** Unused seats this week beats an architecture review this
   quarter, because one of them produces cash.
8. **Never cut backups, security, monitoring or compliance software** to hit a percentage. Those savings
   become incidents, and the incident is not in the savings column.
9. **A saving is realised only when an invoice proves it.** Everything else is an estimate, labelled.
10. **The lines that failed stay in the tracker.** They are the only thing that makes the next case credible.
