# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Cloud account manager / partner> | Savings plans, credits, enterprise discount | The console's support, then the partner | Ask before the quarter closes, not after |
| <Cloud reseller> | Billing consolidation, INR invoicing, GST credit | Email with the current monthly run rate | Often cheaper than direct billing for an Indian entity |
| <IT admin / workspace admin> | SSO logs, seat counts, licence assignments | The admin console | The SSO log is the only honest utilisation number |
| <Engineering lead> | Right-sizing decisions, migration effort estimates | A ticket with the metric and the proposal | Never downsize a database without their sign-off |
| <Finance / accounts> | The ledger extract, GST treatment, payment terms | The monthly close | The reconciliation needs their export, not a guess |
| <Procurement lead> | Contracts, order forms, the vendor register | The signed copies | The notice period lives here, not on the invoice |
| <Legal drafter> | Clause review for a renewal or an exit | The clause and the ask | Flag, do not draft |
| <The owner of each tool> | Whether it is used, what would break if it went | A direct message with the usage number | One named owner per line, always |
| <Two alternative vendors per category> | A credible competing quote | Their sales page and a trial | Without an alternative there is no negotiation |
| <CA> | Input credit on annual prepay, TDS on software payments | A specific invoice | Foreign SaaS payments have their own treatment |
