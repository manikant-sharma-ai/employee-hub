# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Finance / accounts person> | Card statements, vendor ledger, invoice history | The monthly close pack | Ask for three months, not one |
| <IT / workspace admin> | Google Workspace or M365 seat list, last-login export | Admin console export | The largest single line in most small businesses |
| <Cloud account owner> | Billing export, cost explorer, tagging, account count | Console, read-only | Ask "how many accounts" explicitly |
| <Tool owner, per subscription> | Whether it is used, who uses it, what breaks | Slack or a two-line email | An unowned tool is the one that never gets cancelled |
| <Vendor account manager> | Renewal terms, seat reduction, price increase | Email 60 days before renewal | Ask for the old price with a one-year term first |
| <Procurement / founder> | Approval above the cut threshold | The cut list | Never cancel before approval, even when obvious |
| <Security owner> | Anything touching SSO, audit logs or backups | Before a downgrade | Security is not a cost centre |
| <CA> | Capitalising software, GST input credit on foreign SaaS | Before the annual close | Foreign SaaS often carries reverse-charge GST |
| <The person who left> | Subscriptions billed to their card or inbox | Their manager, not them | The most common leak in a 20-50 person business |
