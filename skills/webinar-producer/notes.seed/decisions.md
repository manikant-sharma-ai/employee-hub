# Decisions

Schema only. No decisions yet. A decision goes here when it would otherwise be
re-argued next month, with the reason that made it right at the time.

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

## What belongs here

- The promise and audience for a recurring session, once chosen.
- The offer structure and the price, once approved.
- The follow-up cadence, once tested.
- A format ruling: webinar versus workshop for a given audience size.

## What does not

- Anything the business has not approved yet - that is a proposal, not a decision.
- Restatements of this skill's standards. The skill is the standard.
