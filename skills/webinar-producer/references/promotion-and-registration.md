# Promotion and registration

Promotion is arithmetic before it is copy. The plan is only credible if it says
how many invitations produce how many registrations produce how many people who
stay, and which of those three numbers is measured.

## The ladder, worked backwards

```
target attended-and-stayed
  ÷ attended-and-stayed rate   = registrations needed
  ÷ registration rate          = invitations needed
  ÷ reach per channel          = channels and touches needed
```

Typical ranges for a small business, to be replaced by the business's own history
as soon as it has one:

| Rate | Weak | Normal | Strong | What moves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invitation → registration | 1-2% | 3-6% | 8-15% | A named audience and a specific promise |
| Registration → attendance | 20-25% | 30-40% | 45-55% | Reminders at 24h, 1h and 10 min; calendar hold |
| Attendance → stayed to the offer | 40% | 55-65% | 70%+ | Content that answers the promise early |
| Attended-and-stayed → meeting | 3-5% | 8-12% | 15-25% | A clear, cheap next step |

Worked example: 30 people in the room at the offer, at 55% staying and 35%
attending, needs 156 registrations; at a 5% registration rate that is 3,120
invitations. A 3,000-person list, one email, will not do it - which is the useful
answer, delivered before the campaign is built rather than after.

## The message ladder

| When | Channel | Job of the message | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-14 days | email | Announce: the promise, the date, the reason now | 120 words |
| T-7 days | email + LinkedIn/social | The teaching angle - one insight from the session | 150 words |
| T-2 days | email | Agenda and the one thing they will leave with | 100 words |
| T-24 hours | email | Logistics, calendar file, "reply if you cannot make it" | 80 words |
| T-1 hour | WhatsApp or SMS | "We start in an hour" + join link | 25 words |
| T-10 minutes | WhatsApp or SMS | Join link only | 10 words |

The 1-hour reminder is the highest-yield message in the sequence and the one most
often skipped. In India it is a WhatsApp template message, which needs opt-in and
Meta approval - so the opt-in has to be collected at registration, not on the day.

## The landing page

Seven blocks, in this order:

1. **The promise** - one sentence, the outcome and the audience.
2. **The date, time and duration** - with timezone. "7:00 pm IST, 60 minutes."
3. **Three things you will leave with** - concrete, not "insights".
4. **Who it is for and who it is not for** - the second half raises attendance quality.
5. **The speaker** - one line of credibility with a number in it.
6. **The form** - name, email, and one qualifying question. Every extra field costs registrations; the qualifying question is worth it.
7. **The consent line** - what they will receive, and the unsubscribe.

Anti-patterns on the page: a stock photo of a handshake; "register now" as the
only call to action; a countdown timer that resets on reload; a form with a phone
field marked optional that is then required by the reminder tool.

## Channels, ranked by yield per hour of work

| Channel | Best for | Cost of a registration | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your own email list | Everything | Near zero | List fatigue - one webinar a month, not four |
| WhatsApp broadcast list | Indian SMB and consumer audiences | Near zero | Template approval; opt-in required |
| A partner's list | Audiences you do not have | A revenue share or a reciprocal session | Their list is not yours - expect 30-50% of the rate |
| LinkedIn posts and DMs | B2B, founder-led | Time | DMs to strangers is outbound; see `sales-outreach` |
| Communities (Slack, WhatsApp groups, forums) | Niche B2B | Time, and the group's rules | Ask the admin first; one post, not five |
| Paid ads | Scale, last 72 hours | ₹150-800 per registration | Cheap registrations are often cheap attendees - see `performance-ads-buyer` |
| Your own customers | Expansion sessions | Near zero | Do not invite them to a session that sells what they own |

## Registration mechanics that change attendance

- **Calendar file in the confirmation email**, not just in the reminder.
- **One-click join link in every message** - a link that requires a login loses a third of the room.
- **A reply-to question at T-24h** ("what do you want covered?") raises attendance and gives the host real material.
- **A waitlist when the room is capped** - scarcity that is true works.
- **Ask for the phone number only if WhatsApp reminders will be used.** Otherwise it is data collection with no purpose.

## Anti-patterns

- **Promoting for four days.** The registration curve needs two weeks to bend.
- **Three emails on the final day.** It reads as desperation and lifts unsubscribes more than attendance.
- **A promise that needs the whole hour to explain.** The promise is the invitation.
- **Inviting everyone you know.** Friends in the room flatter the attendance number and destroy the conversion rate you are trying to measure.
- **No qualifying question.** Without one, the follow-up cannot be segmented and every conversation starts from zero.
