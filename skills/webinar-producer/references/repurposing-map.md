# Repurposing map

A recording is a raw material. This file turns one session into a month of assets
with a map that other employees can execute without a meeting.

## The map, one row per asset

| Timecode | Segment | Asset | Platform | Hook line | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 04:10-06:30 | The mistake everyone makes | 45s vertical clip | Instagram, LinkedIn | "We lost ₹4 lakh doing this" | video-editor |
| 12:00-19:00 | The method, step by step | 8-minute clip | YouTube, replay page | "The full method, no pitch" | video-editor |
| 22:00-26:00 | The demo | 60s screen clip | LinkedIn, X | "Watch it happen in four minutes" | video-editor |
| 31:00-33:00 | The pricing answer | Text post, 150 words | LinkedIn | "Three people asked this" | content-writer |
| 36:00-40:00 | The offer | 30s clip | Retargeting ads | "The thing we sell, explained once" | performance-ads-buyer |
| Whole session | Transcript | Long article, 1,200 words | Blog, help centre | The promise, as a title | content-writer |
| Q&A log | Ten questions | Help-centre article or FAQ | Site | The question as the headline | knowledge-base-writer |
| Poll results | The room's answers | One graphic | LinkedIn, Instagram | "62% of manufacturers still do this on paper" | graphic-designer |
| Best quote | A sentence | Quote card | Instagram, LinkedIn | Verbatim, attributed | graphic-designer |

Nothing leaves this file without a timecode and a hook. "Make some clips from the
webinar" produces four mediocre clips and a week of waiting.

## Choosing the segments

- **The strongest 90 seconds is usually the mistake, not the method.** People watch a warning before they watch a lesson.
- **Pick the moment the speaker stopped reading and started answering.** Q&A is more alive than the scripted section.
- **Cut at a sentence boundary, never mid-thought.** A clip that starts mid-sentence reads as an ad.
- **One idea per clip.** If the segment has two lessons, it is two clips.
- **Skip the offer in the organic clips.** Keep the offer for the retargeting cut and the replay page.

## What each asset is for

| Asset | Job | Shelf life | Where it goes next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45s clip | Stop the scroll, build familiarity | 2-3 weeks | Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn feed |
| 8-minute method clip | Prove depth, rank in search | 12+ months | YouTube, replay page, sales follow-up |
| Text post from a Q&A answer | Cheap authority, invites comments | 1 week | LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp broadcast |
| Long article from the transcript | Search traffic, sales enablement | 12+ months | Blog, help centre, proposal appendix |
| FAQ from the Q&A log | Deflection and pre-sale answers | Until the product changes | Help centre, sales objection doc |
| Poll graphic | Social proof and segmentation | 1 month | Feed, deck, proposal |
| Quote card | Brand recall | 1 month | Feed, stories |

## The brief you hand over

For `video-editor`, per clip: the timecode in and out, the hook line to put on
screen in the first 2 seconds, the caption style, the platform and its aspect
ratio, and whether the speaker's face or the screen leads. For `content-writer`,
the transcript segment, the target reader, the promise, and the three headings you
want. For `knowledge-base-writer`, the ten questions verbatim, with the answers the
speaker gave.

A brief that says "make it good" is not a brief. A timecode is.

## The publishing order

| Day | Asset | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Follow-up mail with the recording | The room still remembers |
| Day 2 | One 45s clip on the founder's channel | Momentum, cheap |
| Day 4 | Text post from the best Q&A answer | Starts a conversation that feeds the next session |
| Day 7 | Long article | Search traffic compounds slowly, so start it early |
| Day 10 | Second and third clips | Spaced, not dumped |
| Day 14 | Poll graphic and the next session's registration | Turns an audience into the next room |
| Day 21 | FAQ articles | Deflection value starts here |
| Day 30 | Read the numbers, decide the next topic | What got watched tells you what to run |

## Anti-patterns

- **Publishing all eight clips on the same day.** The algorithm and the audience both treat it as one post.
- **A clip with no hook in the first two seconds.** The recording's first words are rarely the hook; write one.
- **Reusing the session title as the article title.** Titles are promises; write them per asset.
- **Skipping the transcript.** It is the cheapest source of search traffic and the only accurate record of what was claimed.
- **Never deleting a clip that underperforms.** Keep a note of what did not work in `notes/learnings.md`; that is how the next map gets better.
