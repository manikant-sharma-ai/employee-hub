# Follow-up and pipeline

The session ends; the work starts. Follow-up is where a webinar becomes revenue or
becomes a folder of recordings.

## The 24-hour rule

Send inside 24 hours, ideally within 12. After 48 hours the open rate drops by
half and the session is a memory. The three segments are decided by behaviour,
not by preference:

| Segment | Definition | What they get | What you ask for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attended and stayed | Present at the offer minute | Recording, the template, the offer with the date | A 20-minute call, two specific slots |
| Attended and left early | Joined, left before the offer | Recording, the section they missed, a one-line summary of the offer | The recording, and "reply if you want the template" |
| Registered, did not attend | No join record | "Sorry we missed you" + recording + the one insight | The recording, no offer in email one |
| Watched the replay | Platform replay report | The template, the offer | A call, once |

Sending the same email to all four is the failure this file exists to prevent. It
is also the fastest way to teach your list that your follow-up is automated and
ignorable.

## The follow-up sequence

| When | Segment | Subject line shape | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| +2 hours | Attended | "The template, and the two dates" | 90 words, link, two call slots |
| +2 hours | No-show | "Sorry we missed you - the recording" | 70 words, recording, no pitch |
| +24 hours | Attended, no reply | "One question about your <problem>" | 50 words, one question |
| +48 hours | No-show | "The part everyone asked about" | 80 words, one clip or section, then the offer |
| +5 days | Attended, no reply | "Closing the loop on <session>" | 40 words, last call, then stop |
| +7 days | Everyone | Nothing | Stop. A fourth email is a list you are burning |

Three emails per person, maximum, and the third is a close, not a reminder.

## What the follow-up must contain

1. **The recording link**, in the first line, for everyone who registered.
2. **The one thing they asked for** - template, checklist, calculator. If it was
   promised on the landing page, it goes out whether or not they attended.
3. **One specific next step with a price or a date.** "Book a call" is not a next
   step; "pick Thursday 4 pm or Friday 11 am" is.
4. **A reply invitation.** A one-line reply is the cheapest qualification signal
   you will get all month.

## Handoff to the pipeline

| Signal | Owner | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Attended, asked a buying question in Q&A | Closer, same day | Call, referencing the question |
| Attended, clicked the offer link, no booking | Closer, +24h | One email, one call attempt |
| Attended, no engagement | `email-marketer` list | Nurture, not sales |
| No-show, opened three times | Closer, +48h | One email with the recording and the offer |
| Asked for a proposal | `sales-outreach` or the founder | Proposal within 48 hours, referencing the session |
| Existing customer attended | Account owner | Check for expansion signal, do not re-sell what they own |

Write the handoff as a rule, not a hope: who gets which segment, by when, in which
system. If the CRM is not connected, the handoff is a sheet with four columns -
name, segment, next action, due date - and a named person who owns it.

## Measuring the follow-up

| Metric | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-up send rate | Sent ÷ registered | Whether the machinery ran at all |
| Open rate by segment | Opens ÷ sent | Whether segmentation was real |
| Reply rate | Replies ÷ sent | The cheapest quality signal |
| Meeting rate | Meetings ÷ attended-and-stayed | Whether the offer matched the room |
| Cost per meeting | Total session cost ÷ meetings | The number to compare with other channels |

## Anti-patterns

- **"Thanks for attending!" with no recording and no ask.** A courtesy email is
  not follow-up.
- **The same email to attendees and no-shows.** It is visible, and it is rude.
- **Five emails over three weeks.** That is a nurture campaign wearing a webinar
  hat; hand it to `email-marketer`.
- **A discount in the first follow-up.** It teaches the list to wait for the next
  webinar.
- **No owner for the call list.** The most common reason a good session produces
  nothing.
- **Follow-up that promises a proposal nobody writes.** Only offer what the
  business will deliver inside 48 hours.
