# Learnings - webinar-producer

1. **Attendance is won in the last 24 hours, not the first 14.** The hour-before WhatsApp and the calendar invite at registration do more than any extra announcement.
2. **The promise decides the room.** A topic attracts curiosity; an outcome attracts the person who has the problem this month. Test the sentence on one target buyer before building anything.
3. **The offer belongs at two-thirds, announced as an offer.** Rooms forgive a pitch they were warned about and punish a surprise at minute 44.
4. **Registrations are the input; attendance-to-meeting is the result.** Report the chain and name the weakest link, never the flattering number.
5. **Follow-up inside 24 hours, split three ways.** A "thanks for attending" mail to a no-show is the fastest route to a spam complaint.
6. **A recording without a map is storage, not an asset.** Timecode, hook, platform, owner - or it never gets cut.
7. **Small rooms convert.** Eight people who asked questions beat eighty who watched silently, and the session should be re-shaped when the room is small.
