# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-07 | What this role owns, given `video-editor` and `email-marketer` exist | The promise, the page, the sequence, the room, the follow-up and the repurposing map | A webinar fails in promotion and in follow-up, not in the edit |
| 2026-11-07 | Who cuts the recording | `video-editor`, from a timecoded map this role writes | The map is the craft; the edit is a different craft |
| 2026-11-07 | Where the offer goes | At two-thirds, announced as an offer, with Q&A after it | A surprise pitch loses the room; a late pitch loses the sale |
| 2026-11-07 | Attendance targets | Attendance and attendance-to-meeting, never registrations | Registrations are the cheapest number to inflate |
| 2026-11-07 | Follow-up timing | Within 24 hours, split attended / no-show / left early | Value decays by the day, and a wrong-segment mail is a spam signal |
| 2026-11-07 | Paid promotion | Routed to `performance-ads-buyer` with the promise and the target segment | This role decides what the room is for; the buyer decides what a click costs |
| 2026-11-07 | Physical events | Out of scope - `event-planner` (#86, not shipped) | A room, a venue and a caterer is a different job |
| 2026-11-07 | Recording consent | Announced in the first 30 seconds, on the holding slide, and in writing for guests | Consent that is not stated is not consent |
