# Packaging: titles and thumbnails

Packaging is the title and the thumbnail together, and it has one job: make the right person click. It is not
marketing copy and it is not a summary. It is a promise specific enough to be believed.

## Titles

- **Under 60 characters**, so it survives the mobile feed and the suggested sidebar without truncation.
- **The promise in the first three words.** "GST invoice in 4 clicks" beats "How we finally solved our GST
  invoicing problem after months of trying".
- **Concrete over clever.** A number, a name, a stake. "₹40,000 of dead stock" is clickable; "our inventory
  journey" is not.
- **No clickbait the video does not pay.** The gap between title and content is measured in retention and in
  returning viewers.
- **One idea per title.** Two promises halve the click.
- **The search variant**: for search-led videos, put the query in the title in the words people type, not in the
  words the industry uses.

## Thumbnails

A brief, not a design. Three rules and one test:

1. **One subject.** A face, an object or a number - never a collage.
2. **Three words at most**, at a size readable at 120 pixels wide.
3. **One emotion or one contrast** - before/after, red/green, big/small.
4. **The test**: shrink it to thumbnail size on a phone, look for one second, and ask what the video is about. If
   the answer needs the title, the thumbnail is decoration.

Send the brief to `graphic-designer` with the subject, the words, the emotion and the reference - never "make it
pop".

## The packaging test

You cannot A/B a title on YouTube without changing it, so test in order:

1. **Change one thing at a time**, and give it seven days and at least 2,000 impressions before judging.
2. **Compare click-through against the channel's own baseline** for the same traffic source, never against a
   famous channel.
3. **Hold retention constant** in the comparison. A title that lifts click-through and drops retention has bought
   views that cost watch time - which the algorithm reads as a worse video.
4. **Record the test** in the video log: old title, new title, impressions before and after, verdict. Without the
   record, the same test is run twice a year and learned from neither time.

## Common packaging failures

| Symptom | Usual cause |
|---|---|
| High impressions, low click-through | The thumbnail is busy, or the title is a summary rather than a promise |
| High click-through, low retention | The packaging promised something the video did not deliver |
| Low impressions | The topic has no search demand and no browseable hook - a topic problem, not a packaging one |
| Good on desktop, bad on mobile | Text too small, title truncated at 40 characters |
