# Search and suggested traffic

Three doors into a video, and a plan that ignores two of them is a plan for one third of the audience.

## Search

- **Find the query, not the topic.** "how to make gst invoice in excel" is a query; "GST compliance" is a topic
  nobody types. Use YouTube's own search suggestions, the "people also search" row, and the titles of the videos
  already ranking.
- **Judge demand and competition together.** A query with 200 monthly searches and six weak videos is worth more
  than one with 20,000 and a wall of well-made answers from large channels.
- **Answer in the first minute.** Search viewers are impatient and they arrive with the question; a 90-second
  introduction loses them and the ranking follows the retention.
- **One query per video**, in the title and in the first line of the description. A video answering four queries
  ranks for none.

## Suggested

- **Suggested is the compounding one.** It is won by videos that keep people watching after the one they clicked,
  which means series, playlists and a consistent promise.
- **Build the series deliberately**: a first video that ends by naming the next one, a playlist in order, and
  end screens that point at the nearest neighbour rather than the newest upload.
- **The same audience, not the same topic.** Suggested works on the viewer's next interest, which is often a
  neighbouring problem rather than a repeat of the same one.

## Browse

- **Browse is packaging plus recency.** A channel that posts once a quarter cannot win browse; one that posts
  weekly with a coherent promise can, even when each video is small.
- **The first 24 hours decide the ceiling.** Notify the audience, publish when they are awake, and give the video
  a thumbnail that survives the feed.

## The monthly traffic split

Record, per video and per month, the share from search, browse and suggested. Then read it:

| Split | What it means | What to do next |
|---|---|---|
| Mostly search | A library, not a channel | Add a series and a browseable idea |
| Mostly browse | Volatile - one bad thumbnail and the month halves | Build playlists and returning viewers |
| Mostly suggested | A compounding channel | Protect the promise and keep the series going |
| Mostly external or direct | The channel is a shop window for traffic you already had | Check whether that traffic converts |

## Keyword work is not web SEO

The query research here is for YouTube's own search and suggestions. Web rankings, page titles and backlinks
belong to `seo-specialist`; the same keyword in the two systems behaves differently, because the intent and the
competition are different.
