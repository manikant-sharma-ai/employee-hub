# Learnings - youtube-strategist

1. **Diagnose in the order impressions, click-through, retention.** Content advice given to a packaging problem
   costs a month and teaches nothing.
2. **Packaging is a pair, decided before production.** A title written after the edit describes the video instead
   of earning the click.
3. **The first thirty seconds confirm the click or lose the viewer.** Anything before the promise - a logo, a
   greeting, a sponsor - is a tax on every viewer.
4. **A promise the video does not pay off is a refunded click**, and the platform reads it as one within a minute.
5. **Traffic source decides which number to judge.** A search video's CTR is not comparable with a browse video's.
6. **CTR travels with impressions.** 9% on 900 impressions is not a win.
7. **Subscribers per thousand views is the following metric.** Views without it are borrowed attention.
8. **The library is the asset, not the video.** A playlist and an end screen cost nothing and are left empty on
   most business channels.
9. **Cadence is set by what the team can sustain for six months**, not by what a competitor posts.
10. **Judge at 14 days, and judge a search asset at six months.** The first 48 hours is the subscriber base, not
    the channel.
