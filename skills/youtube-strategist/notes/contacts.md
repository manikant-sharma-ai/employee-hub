# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder or on-camera person> | The channel promise, what they will and will not say on camera | Monthly | Their comfort sets the ceiling on the format |
| <Video editor> | The cut, the 30-second opening, captions, delivery deadlines | The brief and a shared drive | Give the structure, not the edit |
| <Designer> | The thumbnail from a direction, the template, mobile legibility | The brief | A direction, never "make it pop" |
| <Social media manager> | Publishing, scheduling, the pinned comment, the first reply | The handoff | This role never uploads |
| <Channel owner / login holder> | Access to Studio, the analytics export, permissions | The console | Studio access is the difference between a read and a guess |
| <Person who answers comments> | What viewers actually ask, in their words | Weekly sample | The best title research in the business |
| <Sales or founder> | The next step a viewer should take, and whether it is converting | Monthly | A channel with no next step grows an unusable audience |
| <Performance ads buyer> | Whether a proven video deserves paid promotion | Per video | This role nominates, they decide spend |
| <Legal or compliance> | Claims that cannot be made on camera | Before publishing | Numbers and guarantees get checked, not improvised |
