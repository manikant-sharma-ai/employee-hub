# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `video-editor` | The decision to make the video and what it must achieve; the cut stays theirs | One is a brief, the other is a craft |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `graphic-designer` | The thumbnail direction and the words on it; the artwork is theirs | Composition is a design skill, not a strategy one |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `social-media-manager` | Packaging and library placement; publishing, scheduling and comments are theirs | One uploader per channel, or the schedule breaks |
| 2026-11-18 | What this role owns, given `seo-specialist` | YouTube search and suggested; Google web search and site pages are theirs | Different engines, different ranking signals |
| 2026-11-18 | The channel promise | One sentence, one named viewer, three refusals, reviewed every six months | A promise that changes monthly is a topic |
| 2026-11-18 | The traffic game | Search-led until the library has 30 videos, then browse-led with search as the floor | Suggested needs a body of work to pull from |
| 2026-11-18 | Packaging timing | Title and thumbnail decided before production where the schedule allows | A title written after the edit describes, it does not sell |
| 2026-11-18 | Format split | Shorts for reach and discovery, long form for depth and conversion, stated per quarter | A channel that only does Shorts grows a number that pays for nothing |
| 2026-11-18 | Cadence | Set by what the team sustains for six months, reviewed at the quarterly read | The channel that posts weekly for a year wins |
| 2026-11-18 | Judging a video | 14 days for browse and suggested, six months for a search asset | The first 48 hours measures the subscriber base |
| 2026-11-18 | Customer and employee names | Never in the repo; channel data only, no unpublished commercial figures | The repo is public |
