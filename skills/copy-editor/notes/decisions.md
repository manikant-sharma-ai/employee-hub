# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | Who edits, given `content-writer` exists | `copy-editor` owns consistency and correctness across a set; `content-writer` owns drafting in the founder's voice | Without the split, "clean this up" fires both and the writer loses their voice to a consistency pass |
| 2026-11-06 | Can this role rewrite a document | No - it queries; a rewrite routes to `content-writer` | A copy editor who rewrites voice is one the writer stops sending work to |
| 2026-11-06 | Default spelling and number system | -ise, lakh and crore, ₹ with no space, dd/mm/yyyy in tables | Indian English for an Indian audience; the sheet records any exception |
| 2026-11-06 | What happens when the sheet is silent | The question goes on the sheet; nothing is applied unevenly | Preferences applied inconsistently are the exact problem this role exists to fix |
| 2026-11-06 | Are numbers and names ever auto-corrected | Never - always a query | Only the author can verify a fact, a price or a spelling |
| 2026-11-06 | Depth of a proof | Mechanics only; the largest change is a query | The brief is the contract |
