# Learnings - copy-editor

1. **The style sheet decides, not the editor.** Every correction traces to a written decision; anything the sheet is silent on becomes a new line on the sheet, not a preference applied to half a document.
2. **One pass, one class of error.** Four merged passes find the easy errors twice and the expensive ones never.
3. **Consistency is not uniformity, and voice is not an error.** Fragments, repetitions and jokes stay; the writer must recognise their own text.
4. **Numbers, names, dates and legal terms are always a query.** They are the changes that cause real damage and the ones the author alone can verify.
5. **The terminology ledger outlives the job.** It is the only artefact that makes the next document cheaper than this one.
6. **A proof is not a rewrite.** Delivering the briefed depth is the contract; the biggest change in a proof should be a query.
