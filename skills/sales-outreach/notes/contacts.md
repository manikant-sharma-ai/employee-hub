# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

| Name | What for | Last useful | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## How to fill this
- **Account owners** - who owns which customer, so outbound never touches an open deal.
- **The person who approves copy** - and what they refuse to say about the product.
- **Referenceable customers** - name, the exact number they agreed to be quoted on, and the date approved.
- **Data sources** - the IndiaMART or LinkedIn seat holder, the enrichment tool, who pays for it and its monthly cap.
- **Domain and deliverability owner** - who controls DNS, warm-up and the sending tool.
- **Suppression list owner** - who maintains the do-not-contact list, and where it lives.

Keep names and roles only. No phone numbers, no personal email, no customer personal data in this file.
