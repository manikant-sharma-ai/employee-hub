# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-02 - The list is the strategy. Before writing a word, prove the ICP can produce the number of contacts the target needs; a weak list makes every other craft irrelevant.
- 2026-11-02 - Each touch must add information. A cadence of reminders trains the buyer to ignore the thread, and the sixth touch is the one that usually earns the reply.
- 2026-11-02 - Ask for the meeting at touch three, never touch one. The first touch's job is to earn the second, and a 15-minute request in touch one is the commonest cause of silence.
- 2026-11-02 - "Not now" without a date is a no. Ask for the date once, then close the row rather than re-litigating it every month.
