# Creative testing

In most accounts, creative is the biggest lever left. Budget changes move the
result by tens of percent; a new hook moves it by multiples.

## The four variables

| Variable | What changes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | The first three seconds or first line | "Your GST filing takes 11 hours" vs "Filing season, without the panic" |
| Proof | The evidence | Customer number, demo, before-and-after, certification |
| Offer | The deal framing | Free trial vs free audit vs first month free |
| Format | The container | UGC video, static, carousel, founder talking head |

Test one at a time. Two variables in one test and the winner tells you nothing
about why.

## Test design at a small budget

| Budget | What you can test | Minimum per variant |
|---|---|---|
| Rs 20,000 a month | 3 hooks, same everything else | ~1,500 impressions or 100 clicks each |
| Rs 60,000 a month | 3 hooks, then 2 formats | ~3,000 impressions each |
| Rs 2,00,000+ | Hook family, then proof, then offer | Platform-level significance |

Declare a winner on cost per verified action, not on CTR. A hook that gets double
the clicks into a page that does not convert is a worse ad.

## The variant brief

For each variant, write these five lines before the designer starts:

```
Hook:      <the first line or first frame>
Angle:     <time saved / money saved / risk removed / status / replacement>
Proof:     <the specific evidence>
CTA:       <the one action>
Format:    <9:16 video 15s / 1:1 static / carousel 3 cards>
```

Five variants with distinct angles beat fifteen variations of one idea.

## Reading a creative result

| Pattern | Read |
|---|---|
| High CTR, low conversion | The ad promises something the page does not deliver |
| Low CTR, high conversion | Wrong audience or weak hook, good page |
| High CTR and conversion, rising CPM | The winner is saturating; produce siblings |
| Falling CTR at stable frequency | Fatigue; refresh the hook |
| Frequency above 3-4 on a cold audience | Burnout; widen the audience or stop |
| One variant far ahead of all others | Confirm with a second week before scaling into it |

## Fatigue management

- **Keep a creative bank** of at least three ready variants per angle.
- **Watch frequency and CTR together.** Frequency rising with CTR falling is the signal.
- **Refresh the hook first.** Most fatigue is in the first three seconds, not the offer.
- **Siblings, not replacements.** Produce variations of the winner rather than starting over.
- **Never let one creative carry the account** for more than four to six weeks without a sibling ready.

## India-specific craft

- **Vernacular first, English second** for tier 2 and 3 audiences; the same angle often doubles in Hindi or Tamil.
- **WhatsApp-style UGC** outperforms polished studio work for D2C under Rs 2,000 AOV.
- **Festive creative is planned in August**, not in October, because production queues fill.
- **Trust markers** - GST number, address, COD availability, returns policy - belong in the creative for first-time buyers.
- **Data costs and device mix** mean heavy video is a tax on the audience; keep the first frame meaningful on a slow connection.

## Anti-patterns

- **Testing copy and creative together.** Neither result is usable.
- **Calling a winner on day one.** The first day is the algorithm exploring.
- **Fifteen variants, one idea.** Volume without angle diversity tests nothing.
- **Judging on CTR.** It measures curiosity, not purchase.
- **No creative bank.** The account stalls the week the winner fatigues.
