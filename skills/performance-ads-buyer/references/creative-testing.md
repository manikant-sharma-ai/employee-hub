# Creative testing

In a modern auction, the creative is the targeting. Audience work moves CPA by a few
per cent; a new hook moves it by tens.

## The cadence

| Cadence | What ships | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | 3 new concepts | Keeps a fresh supply without flooding the test |
| Per concept | 2 hooks x 2 formats (the 3-2-2 shape) | Isolates hook from format |
| Monthly | 1-2 bigger swings: a new angle, a new proof, a new offer framing | Small iterations converge; only a new angle breaks a plateau |
| Retire | Anything above the CPA ceiling for 7 days with 50+ clicks | A rule, not a mood |

A concept is an idea, not a file: "unboxing in a real kitchen", "founder explains
the price", "customer before-and-after". Variants are executions of one concept.

## What to measure, in order

| Metric | What it tells you | Rough reading |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) | Whether the first frame stops the scroll | Below 20% on cold video is usually a weak hook |
| Hold rate (thruplays ÷ 3-second views) | Whether the middle earns the watch | Falling hold with a good hook means the promise was not kept |
| CTR (link clicks ÷ impressions) | Whether the offer is legible | Compare within the account, never to an industry number |
| CVR on the landing page | Whether the page matches the ad | A high CTR with a low CVR is a promise the page breaks |
| CPA and reconciled orders | Whether it made money | The only metric that decides |

Diagnose in that order. A high CPA with a strong hook and a strong CTR is a landing
page or offer problem, not a creative problem - and no amount of new creative will
fix it.

## Format notes for the Indian market

- **Vertical video, sound-on, subtitles burned in.** Most viewing is on a phone,
  often without sound.
- **The first frame must show the product or the problem.** Lifestyle openers
  without a product in frame lose the scroll.
- **Price on screen early** for considered purchases; hiding it increases clicks and
  kills conversion.
- **Language matters more than polish.** A Hindi or Marathi version of a rough UGC
  clip routinely beats a polished English studio ad for the same product.
- **Regional specificity works**: the local festival, the local weather, the local
  payment habit.
- **Trust elements**: a real address, a return promise, a phone number, a GST invoice
  line. In categories with fraud anxiety, these move CPA more than editing.

## Fatigue, and how it shows

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| Frequency above 2.5-3 on cold audiences | The same people are seeing it repeatedly |
| CTR falling week over week at flat spend | The hook has been absorbed |
| CPM rising with stable delivery | Auction pressure or audience exhaustion |
| Hook rate falling first, then CTR | The opening frame has worn out; test a new hook before a new concept |

Refreshing the first three seconds is the cheapest intervention in paid media.
Rebuilding the concept is the second cheapest. Changing the audience is usually the
most expensive way to learn nothing.

## Briefing the creative

Give the producer the constraint, not the script:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| The buyer | One sentence, specific |
| The one thing they must believe | The single claim |
| The proof | What makes it credible: a demo, a number, a customer |
| The format | Duration, aspect, subtitles, platform |
| The variants | How many hooks, how many formats |
| The kill criteria | What would make this a failure |

Production belongs to `graphic-designer` and `video-editor`. This role briefs,
tests and retires; it does not design.

## Anti-patterns

- **One creative, one audience, six weeks.** Nothing is learned and CPA drifts.
- **Judging a creative on 12 clicks.** The rule needs a sample.
- **Copying a competitor's ad.** It worked for their offer and their price.
- **Testing five things at once.** Hook, format, audience and offer in one test
  produces a result nobody can use.
- **Killing on day two.** The learning phase is not the creative's fault.
- **Making claims the product cannot support.** The cheapest CPA in the account is
  usually the ad that gets the account restricted.
