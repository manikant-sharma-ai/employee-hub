# Learnings - data-privacy-officer

1. **The map comes before the notice.** A policy written before the map describes an aspiration, and the gap
   between the two is the liability.
2. **The cheapest privacy fix is deleting a field nobody uses**, not adding a policy. The third bucket - data held
   with no purpose - is where most of the exposure sits.
3. **Free text and attachments are where deletions fail.** A CRM note with a phone number survives a field-level
   erase, and a support attachment is the most sensitive data in the business.
4. **The withdrawal route is the test of the consent.** If withdrawing takes more steps than giving, the consent was
   not free.
5. **A request is answered from every system in the map, not from the CRM.** The inbox, the helpdesk, the WhatsApp
   thread and the vendor all hold copies.
6. **Statutory retention outranks an erasure request.** Deleting a tax record to satisfy a customer is a second
   violation, not a fix.
7. **Contain before you notify.** Notifying on a scope you do not have produces three corrections and a worse
   incident.
8. **The rule position moves.** The DPDP obligations are being phased in and the Rules and commencement dates decide
   what applies when, so every answer carries the date it was checked.
9. **A vendor's certification is not the business's compliance.** The fiduciary's obligation stays with the
   fiduciary.
10. **Never say compliant.** Say what is in place, what is not, and what needs a lawyer. The first is an employee's
    answer; the second is a position taken on advice.
