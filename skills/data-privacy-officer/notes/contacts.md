# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Lawyer, data and technology> | The opinion: lawful basis, notification decisions, DPAs, regulator contact | Email, retained | The one signature this role cannot provide |
| <CA or tax consultant> | Statutory retention periods for tax, payroll and company records | Email | Needed before any erasure of a financial record |
| <Founder or director> | The notification decision, the retention approval, the risk acceptance | Direct | The decision owner in the breach playbook |
| <IT or app vendor> | Access controls, logs, backups, the backend nobody can see | A ticket | The gap in every data map |
| <CRM and helpdesk admin> | Fields, free-text notes, deletion tooling, exports | Admin console | Deletion reaches here first, and usually stops here |
| <Website or marketing owner> | Consent banner, tags, analytics, form fields, session replay | Admin console | Tags load before consent more often than anyone admits |
| <Payroll provider> | Employee data, statutory records, exit deletion | Portal and email | The most sensitive data the business holds |
| <Payment gateway support> | Card data scope, tokenisation, dispute data | Dashboard | Card data should never be in the business's systems |
| <Delivery partner's compliance contact> | Address and phone handling, their retention, their breach notice | Email | They are a processor for the address, and rarely say so |
| <Grievance officer, named publicly> | The first stop for a data principal's complaint | The published channel | Must be a working route, not a placeholder |
