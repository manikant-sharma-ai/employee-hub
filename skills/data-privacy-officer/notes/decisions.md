# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `compliance-watch` | The data map, consent, requests, retention and breach; the obligation register and change watch stay theirs | One knows what the business holds, the other knows what is due |
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `legal-drafter` | The facts and the operational answer; the policy document is drafted there | A policy drafted without the map is the problem, not the fix |
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `security-reviewer` | Process, rights and records; technical controls and vulnerabilities stay there | A privacy role cannot test an app and should not imply it did |
| 2026-11-16 | Retention | One period per category, with the statute written next to it, approved by the founder | "As long as needed" is not a period |
| 2026-11-16 | Consent | Specific per purpose, with the wording recorded | A database flag is not proof of consent |
| 2026-11-16 | Verification | Reply only to the registered channel; never to an address supplied in the request | The convenience route is the theft route |
| 2026-11-16 | Erasure | Delete in every system and at every processor, after the statutory check | A hidden record is not a deleted record |
| 2026-11-16 | Breach | Contain, log, scope, then decide on notification with advice | Notifying before containment makes the incident worse |
| 2026-11-16 | Compliance statements | Never state that the business is compliant | Compliance is a position taken on advice, at a date |
| 2026-11-16 | Personal data in the repo | Field names, system names and counts only | The repo is public; a map with records in it is a breach |
