# The anatomy of a quotation

A quote is read in ninety seconds by someone deciding whether to reply. Every element below earns its
place on the page.

## The page, top to bottom

| Block | What goes in it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Your legal name, GSTIN, address, the document title and number, the date | It becomes the reference in every later email |
| Customer block | Their legal name, contact, GSTIN, delivery address | Wrong GSTIN means a wrong tax split |
| Reference | Their enquiry number, your quote number, the salesperson | The thread that survives a change of hands |
| Subject | The item in their words, the quantity | Confirms you understood the request |
| Line items | Description, specification, HSN/SAC, quantity, unit, rate, amount | The audit trail and the argument's subject |
| Charges | Freight, installation, packaging, training, each as its own line | Never a lump "other charges" |
| Tax | Rate, taxable value, CGST/SGST or IGST, total | The most common source of disputes |
| Total | Amount in figures and words | Indian commercial convention, and it stops alteration |
| Terms | Payment, delivery, warranty, validity, exclusions | The part the purchase order will contradict |
| Signature block | Name, designation, phone, email | A document a person can accept |

## The terms that prevent the argument

| Term | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Validity | "Prices subject to change" | "Valid till 30 November 2026" |
| Payment | "As per agreement" | "50% advance with PO, balance before dispatch" |
| Delivery | "Ex-works, 4-6 weeks" | "Ex-works Pune, 30 days from PO and advance receipt" |
| Freight | "Extra as applicable" | "₹8,400 to Guwahati by surface; insurance at 0.3% if required" |
| Installation | "Included" | "Included at site within city limits; outstation travel at actuals" |
| Warranty | "Standard warranty" | "12 months from commissioning against manufacturing defects; consumables excluded" |
| Taxes | "Plus GST" | "GST at 18% (HSN 9403) extra; IGST applicable, inter-state supply" |
| Exclusions | None | "Civil work, power point, and unloading at site excluded" |

## Quote, estimate, proforma - three different documents

| Document | What it commits | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Quotation | A price for a defined scope, for a stated period | The normal commercial offer |
| Estimate | An indicative price, scope not final | Early conversations; label it clearly |
| Proforma invoice | The amount payable, against which the customer pays or opens an LC | After acceptance, before the tax invoice |

Calling an estimate a quotation is how a business ends up held to a number it never meant.

## The legal position, in one paragraph

Under the Indian Contract Act, a quotation is ordinarily an invitation to offer. The customer's
purchase order is the offer, and the business accepts it by acknowledgement or performance. That is
why the purchase order must be checked against the quote before it is accepted: acceptance on their
terms converts their terms into the contract.

## Presenting a choice

| Presentation | When it works | The risk |
|---|---|---|
| Single price | A commodity with a known market rate | Invites a pure discount negotiation |
| Good / better / best | A considered purchase, or a first-time buyer | The middle option is chosen most, so price it to be chosen |
| Base plus options | Equipment with add-ons | The customer strips the options and compares the base |
| Volume break table | Repeat or bulk buyers | Commits the price to the break, so set it carefully |

## Anti-patterns

- **"As applicable" anywhere.** It is an unfinished document.
- **Taxes hidden in the price.** The customer discovers the real number at the invoice.
- **A validity of "30 days" with no date.** Two people count from different days.
- **Line items with brand names only.** The specification is what is being bought.
- **A quote with no exclusions.** Everything not written is assumed included.
- **Reusing a quote without changing the reference and date.** Two customers, one number, an audit
  finding.
