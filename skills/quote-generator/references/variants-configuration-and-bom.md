# Variants, configuration and the BOM

Most quoting errors are not pricing errors. They are configuration errors - the right price for the
wrong thing.

## The configuration block

Before pricing, restate what is being sold in a block the customer confirms:

| Field | Example | Why it is asked |
|---|---|---|
| Model / SKU | Chair EX-200 | The base everything attaches to |
| Variant | Mesh back, black, gas lift | The option that changes cost |
| Size or capacity | 500ml, 2HP, 6-seater | Where the price steps |
| Material or grade | SS 304, 18mm ply | The substitution customers ask for |
| Finish or colour | Powder-coated RAL 9005 | Non-standard finishes carry a minimum order |
| Quantity and unit | 200 nos | The unit is the quantity's denominator |
| Delivery point | Site at Chakan, Pune | Freight and installation depend on it |
| Installation required | Yes / no | A cost line, not a courtesy |
| Site readiness | Power, civil, access | The condition that voids an installation date |

## Options and extras, priced separately

| Extra | Price as | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Extended warranty | % of item price, or a flat per year | The service cost over the period |
| Installation and commissioning | Flat per site, or per unit above a threshold | Outstation travel and stay |
| Training | Per session, per batch | Number of people and location |
| Spares kit | Per unit or per site | Consumables the customer will need |
| Annual maintenance | Per year, with a scope | What is excluded from the AMC |
| Custom branding | Per unit, with a minimum | Setup cost spread over the quantity |

## The BOM, when the product is made to order

A build-to-order quote needs a bill of materials before a price:

1. **Structure** - the assemblies and sub-assemblies.
2. **Components** - item, specification, quantity per unit, unit cost, wastage.
3. **Buy-versus-make** - what is outsourced, at what rate and lead time.
4. **Labour and machine hours** - per unit, at the shop rate.
5. **Consumables and finishing** - often 5-12% of material cost.
6. **Yield and rejection** - the honest number, not the brochure number.
7. **Scrap value** - what the offcut recovers.

Where the BOM is more than a page, the quote carries a summary and the BOM stays internal.

## Substitutions and the conversation they cause

A customer asking for a cheaper grade is asking for a different product. Quote the substitution as a
separate option, with what changes in performance, warranty and lead time. A silent substitution is a
rejection at delivery and a return.

## Anti-patterns

- **Quoting a model without the variant.** The price is right and the product is wrong.
- **Installation assumed included.** It is a cost, and it is usually the one that kills the margin.
- **A site visit skipped on a project quote.** Access, power and civil work decide the cost.
- **A BOM priced from memory.** The supplier's last rate is not this month's rate.
- **Options priced after the customer chooses.** The delta becomes a negotiation.
- **Quoting a non-standard finish without a minimum order.** The setup cost lands on one unit.
