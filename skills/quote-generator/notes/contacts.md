# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Pricing owner / founder> | A price outside the approved structure, a floor breach | One-line recommendation with the margin | Never a number decided alone |
| <Key customer - purchase manager> | Specification, budget, decision process | Phone, then email | Know who else signs |
| <Dealer / distributor> | Tier rates, territory, stock commitment | Email with the price list version | Their resale margin is the whole relationship |
| <Transporter - surface and air> | Freight rates by pin code, transit time | Rate card, then a quote per consignment | Rates move with diesel; refresh quarterly |
| <Installation partner> | Site work, outstation travel, commissioning | A site-specific quote | The cost that kills project margins |
| <CA / tax consultant> | HSN classification, rate changes, export treatment | A written question per product | Get the classification in writing once |
| <Credit controller> | Payment terms approval, credit limit for a new customer | Before the terms are quoted | Terms quoted are terms granted |
| <Production / planning> | Lead time and capacity for a delivery date | The order book | Never quote a date without this |
| <Bank - forex desk> | Export quotes, currency exposure | With the invoice value and date | For export quotations |
| <Legal> | Non-standard terms a customer insists on | Before the quote is sent | Penalty clauses and indemnities |
