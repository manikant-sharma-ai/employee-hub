# Learnings - quote-generator

1. **Nothing "as applicable" survives the relationship.** Freight, taxes, installation and lead time are
   numbers or stated exclusions, and every one left vague returns as a discount demand or a dispute.
2. **A quote is an invitation to offer; the purchase order is the contract.** Checking the PO against
   the quote before acknowledging it is the step that stops their terms becoming ours.
3. **The margin is checked before the quote leaves, not after the order is won.** A discount that
   becomes the new list price compounds across every future order.
4. **Offer a choice, not a number.** Good, better, best moves the conversation from "how much off" to
   "which one".
5. **Taxes are decided by HSN, rate and place of supply, and all three are looked up.** CGST plus SGST on
   an inter-state delivery is the most common and most visible error.
6. **The variant is confirmed before the price is built.** A confident quote for the wrong specification
   is the fastest way to lose a customer's trust and a week.
7. **Every revision gets a version and a date.** Silent edits leave two prices with one reference number.
8. **A follow-up must carry new information.** "Just checking in" trains the customer to ignore the next
   one.
9. **Record the win/loss reason on every quote.** Without it, every loss is "they were cheaper" and the
   real cause never gets fixed.
