# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | What this role owns, given `pricing-analyst` | Application of an approved price to one customer, one quantity, one date | Setting the price and packaging is a policy decision; applying it is a commercial one |
| 2026-11-13 | Below the floor | Never quote; a one-line recommendation to the founder instead | A quote is a commitment, and a salesperson deciding alone makes the price list fiction |
| 2026-11-13 | Terms | Every charge a number or an exclusion; validity a date, not a duration | Ambiguity returns as a discount demand at the invoice |
| 2026-11-13 | Taxes | HSN, rate and place of supply looked up and stated once, inclusive or exclusive | The tax line is what gets audited and credit-noted |
| 2026-11-13 | Revisions | A new version, a date and the reason, old version retained | Two prices with one reference is a dispute with evidence |
| 2026-11-13 | Purchase orders | Checked against the quote before acknowledgement | The PO is where their terms replace ours |
| 2026-11-13 | Invoicing | Never - `finance-ops` raises the tax invoice | Quoting and invoicing are different documents with different liabilities |
| 2026-11-13 | Notes | No customer names, GSTINs or prices in the repo | Public repo, commercially sensitive data |
