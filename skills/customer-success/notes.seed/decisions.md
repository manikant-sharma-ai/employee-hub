## What belongs here

- The six signal weights, once chosen for this business.
- The band thresholds, if they are tuned away from the default.
- The renewal checkpoint that applies to this sales cycle.
- A save-play ruling: which play for which cause, once tested.

## What does not

- Anything the founder has not approved - that is a proposal, not a decision.
- Restatements of this skill's standards. The skill is the standard.
