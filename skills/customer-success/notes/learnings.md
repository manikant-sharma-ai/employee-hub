# Learnings - customer-success

1. **Usage predicts renewal; sentiment does not.** The account that says "all good" and stopped logging in is the one that cancels, and it was telling the truth.
2. **Ninety days out, not thirty.** A renewal conversation that starts inside the notice period has already lost the negotiation, and the notice period itself is discovered late more often than it is honoured.
3. **Establish the reason before the concession.** A discount offered before the customer has said why teaches the account that threatening to leave is the way to get a better price.
4. **One champion is a single point of failure.** The economic buyer has to have heard the value case in their own numbers, or the champion is defending the renewal alone.
5. **The QBR is the customer's meeting.** Their goal quoted back, their outcome number, one ask, decisions with owners - a feature review answers a question nobody asked.
6. **Every health score shows its inputs.** The score ranks the work; the raw values are what the conversation is actually about.
7. **Expansion follows proof, and the ladder starts with information.** Showing a limit approaching is not a sales move, and it is the one that gets the upgrade.
8. **A clean exit produces a referral.** A messy one produces a review, and the reason code is the most valuable thing a lost account leaves behind.
