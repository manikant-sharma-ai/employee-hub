# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

| Name | What for | Last useful | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## How to fill this
- **Printers** - name, city, what stock they hold, whether they will send a soft proof, typical turnaround, and the one thing they always get wrong.
- **Photographers** - product vs lifestyle, studio or on-site, day rate, how many retouched images per shot.
- **Illustrators and retouchers** - style, rate, whether they will sign a licence.
- **The approver** - who actually says yes on brand work, and what they care about.
