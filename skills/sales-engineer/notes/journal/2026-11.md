# 2026-11

- **2026-11-12** - Skill written. Baseline before the first deal: seven discovery facts established
  before any design (systems and versions, integration surface, data volume and frequency,
  compliance rule, IT owner, deadline and its driver, what would make it a no) with the environment
  map drawn live and corrected by the buyer, a written verdict of fit, fit with conditions, not a
  fit today, or unknown, a one-page solution design with scope written as deliverable plus volume
  plus frequency and an out-of-scope list as long as the scope, effort as a range with the
  assumption behind each end, a demo in three acts on their data with the failure path rehearsed, a
  POC with numbered success criteria, pre-agreed exit criteria, a decision date in the diary and a
  named owner on each side, a security review started in week one, and a comparison that concedes
  at least one row. Nothing is called demonstrated unless it was shown.
