# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-05 | Who owns onboarding and reviews? | hiring-manager, from the signed offer onwards | hr-recruiter ends at the offer; a gap there is how good hires drift for six weeks |
| 2026-11-05 | 30-day milestones: output or context? | Context and learning, with output from day 60 | Output targets at day 30 reward noise and punish honest ramp-up |
| 2026-11-05 | Review cadence | Quarterly, same dates for everyone, evidence collected two weeks ahead | Predictable dates remove the ambush and the recency bias |
| 2026-11-05 | PIP length | 30-60-90 days by gap size, weekly check-ins, written end date | An open-ended PIP is a slow exit with legal exposure |
| 2026-11-05 | Who sets pay bands? | Founder, written down, benchmarked by research-analyst | A band invented during a negotiation has no anchor |
| 2026-11-05 | Does this role handle POSH complaints? | No - internal committee, statutory process | Not a drafting or judgement call for an AI employee |
