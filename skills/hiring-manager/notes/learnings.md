# Learnings - hiring-manager

1. **The outcome sentence comes before the milestone.** A 30-60-90 for a role nobody defined produces activities that cannot be failed, and a review that becomes an argument about expectations.
2. **Four weeks is the limit between noticing a pattern and naming it.** Beyond that the person has a fair complaint, and the business has a documentation problem instead of a performance one.
3. **Feedback same-week, in writing after every difficult conversation.** The email is the process; the meeting is only where it is said out loud.
4. **30-day output targets are a mistake.** At day 30 the person is still learning names; targets that early produce theatre or an early exit.
5. **A no without a date is a resignation with a delay.** Every declined raise needs a reason, a condition and a review date.
6. **Ratings follow evidence collected before the meeting.** A rating decided in the room is a mood, and the team can tell within one cycle.
