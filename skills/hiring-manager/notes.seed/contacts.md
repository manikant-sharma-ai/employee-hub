# Contacts - hiring-manager

Schema only. No names on day one. Add a person the second time they are useful,
not the first.

## Format

| Name | Role / relationship | What they are good for | Last useful | Consent to store |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Categories to keep

- Reporting managers and their management experience
- Labour lawyer or consultant, and the state they advise on
- Payroll and HRMS administrator
- Background verification vendor
- Recruitment partner, if one is used
- Internal committee contact for POSH matters (name and channel only, never case detail)

## Never stored here

Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, medical notes, salary figures, or anything from a POSH
or harassment matter.
