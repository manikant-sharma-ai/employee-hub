## How to fill this
- **The presenter** - who will stand up, their speaking register, and the words they refuse to say.
- **The decision-maker's office** - who sets the agenda and the slot length, and whether the deck is read before or during the meeting.
- **The number owner** - finance or the founder, for the figures that must reconcile; never take a number second-hand.
- **The brand owner** - the template, fonts and colours, and whether a custom deck is allowed at all.
- **Legal or compliance** - for comparative claims, regulatory language and customer names.
- **The `graphic-designer` employee** - for the visual system; note which template was used last time.

Keep names and roles only. No phone numbers, no personal email, no customer personal data in this file.
