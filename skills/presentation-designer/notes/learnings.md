# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-03 - The title run is the deck. If the slide titles do not argue on their own, no amount of design rescues it.
- 2026-11-03 - Fix the time budget before the slide count. A 15-minute slot holds nine slides, and thirty slides means the ask never lands.
- 2026-11-03 - Every number on a slide needs period, unit and actual-or-forecast on the slide itself. The footnote in the appendix is never read in the room.
- 2026-11-03 - The ask is its own slide, before the appendix. Decks that bury it get a polite follow-up instead of a decision.
