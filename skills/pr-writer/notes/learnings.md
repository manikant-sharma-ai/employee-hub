# Learnings - pr-writer

1. **The hook is decided before the release is written.** A weak hook cannot be rescued by good prose; it can only be replaced by a stronger fact or a different angle.
2. **One angle per publication, from one fact set.** Six identical pitches read as a blast and get treated as one.
3. **The list is the strategy.** Fifteen named reporters who cover the beat beat five hundred addresses from a database, and a bought list burns the sending domain.
4. **Every number carries a period and a basis.** "₹4.2 crore in FY26, audited" survives a fact-check; "crores of revenue" becomes a correction.
5. **Never claim a send, a reply or a clipping that is not in a system you can see.** A pitched list is a plan until the sent folder says otherwise.
6. **No coverage is a finding.** Twenty pitches and no reply means the story is not news yet - say that, and propose what would make it news.
