# Media list and outreach

The list is the strategy. A perfect release sent to a wrong list is a wasted week;
a plain one sent to fifteen right people gets covered.

## The row

| Field | Why it exists | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Reporter | The only thing that matters - not the publication | Nandita Raghavan |
| Publication | Section matters as much as the title | Mint, money page |
| Beat | What they actually write about, in five words | small business compliance |
| Last three pieces | Proof you read them, and the line to reference | Aug: compliance costs; Sep: GST portal; Oct: CA shortage |
| Why them | The specific line of this story that is theirs | The ₹299 price point against CA-firm pricing |
| Email | Verified this month, not last year | (from byline or the outlet's contact page) |
| Channel | Email, X/LinkedIn DM, or the outlet's tip form | Email |
| Status | Not sent / sent / opened / replied / published | replied - wants data |

## Building it, in this order

1. **Start from the story, not the database.** Which section would run this? Money,
   startups, retail, agriculture, technology, the city page. That names five to
   eight publications.
2. **Read the last month of each.** Search the publication's site for the topic and
   note the bylines. Reporters who covered it once will cover it again.
3. **Verify the byline is current.** People move every few months. A dead address
   is worse than no address: it teaches the spam filter your domain.
4. **Find the address from the byline page or the outlet's contact page**, not from
   a scraped list. Where only a tip-form exists, use the form and say so.
5. **Rank by fit, not by prestige.** A trade magazine that covers your category
   monthly beats a national daily that covers it never.

## India's media map, roughly

| Layer | Examples | Covers | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business dailies | Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine | Money, markets, policy, large scale | 3-7 days |
| Startup media | YourStory, Inc42, Entrackr, TechCircle | Funding, founders, product | 2-4 days |
| General news | Times of India, Indian Express, Hindustan Times, NDTV, India Today | Consumer impact, city, human interest | 1-5 days |
| Regional language | Lokmat, Loksatta, Maharashtra Times, Eenadu, Dinamani, Anandabazar | Local jobs, local pride, local price | 1-3 days |
| Trade and B2B | The Hindu BusinessLine agri pages, Express Pharma, AutoCar Pro, Fibre2Fashion | Specification, capacity, compliance | 1-2 weeks |
| Wires | PTI, ANI, IANS | Anything with a national number | same day |
| Newsletters and podcasts | Category-specific Substacks, creator shows | Depth, founder interviews | 1-2 weeks |

Regional-language coverage is usually the highest-return, lowest-competition
channel for a business that sells locally, and the most often skipped. A note in
Marathi to Lokmat's district edition can produce more inbound than a national
mention.

## Embargo and exclusivity

- **An embargo is a written promise**: "not before 9 a.m. IST on 12 November". Put
  it at the top of the note, in the mail body, in words.
- **Give embargoes only to people who have honoured them.** Record it in the tracker.
- **One exclusive, one outlet.** An exclusive means they get it first and nobody
  else gets it before publication. Five "exclusives" is a reputation problem.
- **If the embargo breaks**, publish immediately, tell the others, and do not
  pretend it did not happen.

## Outreach sequence

| Day | Action | Note |
|---|---|---|
| D-5 | Exclusive offer to the first-choice reporter | One only, with a 24-hour decision window |
| D-3 | Embargoed note to the shortlist of 8-12 | Personal line per mail, no BCC |
| D-1 | Answer questions, supply photos and data | The press kit link, not six attachments |
| D-0, 9 a.m. | Wider list, 10-20 more | Paste the note in the body |
| D+3 | One follow-up, adding something | Never "just checking in" |
| D+7 | Publish a first-person version if there is no coverage | Route to content-writer |

## Anti-patterns

- A list bought or scraped, sent as one BCC.
- Pitching a funding story to a reporter who covers product reviews.
- Sending a 2 MB PDF and no text in the body.
- Following up four times.
- "Circling back" as the entire content of a mail.
- Pitching on Friday at 6 p.m.
- Calling a reporter's personal number that was never given to you.
