# Contacts - chief-of-staff

Schema only. No names on day one.

## Format

| Name | Role | Owns which number | Best channel | Consent to store |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Categories to keep

- Each metric owner, and the number they are accountable for
- The founder's executive assistant or admin, if one exists
- The accountant or finance lead who produces the monthly numbers
- The person who holds the calendar and the person who holds the sheet

## Never stored here

Salary figures, personal performance notes, or anything from a personnel or legal
matter.
