# The operating cadence

A cadence is the smallest set of repeating conversations that keeps the business
steerable. More meetings is not more control; it is less attention.

## The four layers

| Layer | Frequency | Length | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | 10-15 minutes, standup | 15 min | Blockers only, no reporting |
| Weekly | Operating review | 45 min | Two or three decisions, the register sweep |
| Monthly | Numbers and review | 90 min | The month's read, one course correction |
| Quarterly | Bets and plan | Half day | Three bets, the numbers that define them, what stops |

A business of five people needs the weekly and the monthly. The daily earns its
place only when work is handed between people every day.

## What each layer must not do

- **The daily must not become reporting.** "I am working on X" is not a blocker.
- **The weekly must not become planning.** If it plans the quarter, the quarter has
  no plan.
- **The monthly must not re-run the weekly.** It reads the month, not the week.
- **The quarterly must not be a strategy offsite.** Three bets with numbers, and a
  list of what stops.

## The quarterly bets

Three, not five. Each one is written as:

| Field | Example shape |
|---|---|
| The bet | "Move 60% of new revenue to annual contracts" |
| Why now | The fact that makes this quarter different |
| The number | The metric and the target that says it worked |
| The owner | One name |
| What stops | The work that is dropped to make room |
| The reversal test | What would tell us by mid-quarter that it is not working |

"What stops" is the field that decides whether the bet is real. A quarter with three
bets and nothing stopped is a quarter with three wishes.

## The monthly numbers review

Same page as the weekly, at month grain, plus:

- The variance against plan, with the cause named.
- The two things that are structurally wrong, not the ten that are urgent.
- One change for next month, with an owner and a date.

## Defending the cadence

- **Fixed slots.** Same day, same time, booked for the quarter. A moveable review
  is a cancelled review.
- **Cancel the meeting with no decision in it.** Two consecutive reviews with no
  decision is a signal to change the format, not to add attendees.
- **The note goes out the same day.** A review whose actions arrive on Thursday was
  a discussion.
- **One owner of the cadence.** The chief of staff role exists so the rhythm does
  not depend on the founder's energy that week.
- **A lighter version for travel weeks.** The page still gets filled and read; the
  meeting is 20 minutes on a call. The rhythm bends, it does not break.

## Anti-patterns

- **The meeting audit that adds meetings.** The usual outcome of "we need better
  communication" is three more recurring invites.
- **The all-hands weekly.** Useful for announcements, useless for decisions.
- **The review that everyone attends and nobody prepares.** Preparation is the
  price of a 45-minute meeting.
- **The quarter with no plan until week six.** By then the quarter is being
  executed by whoever is loudest.
- **The cadence that only the founder can run.** It will run for three weeks.
