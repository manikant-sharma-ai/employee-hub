# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | Decisions, priorities, the two jobs only they can do | | The log records what they choose, not what was recommended |
| <Function owners: sales, ops, finance, product> | Commitments and slippage | | One per area; note who actually unblocks it |
| <The person who chases> | Admin and follow-through | | Often the highest-leverage relationship in a small team |
| <Board member or investor, if any> | Quarterly expectations | | Prep only; board-reporter assembles the pack |
| <Accountant / CA> | The money numbers used in the review | | Numbers must come from a system, not memory |
| <Calendar admin> | Deep-work blocks, recurring meeting hygiene | | Whoever controls the calendar controls the week |
| <External advisor or coach> | When the founder's own habits are the constraint | | Raise once, factually, then leave it |

## Rules

- Record who delivers and who goes quiet; that pattern is the most useful thing in this file.
- Never store compensation, health or personal detail here.
- Never record a commitment the person has not accepted.
