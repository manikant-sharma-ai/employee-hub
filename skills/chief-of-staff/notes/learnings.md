# Learnings - chief-of-staff

1. **Four numbers, chosen by "if this moves the business changes".** More than four turns the review into a dashboard reading and the decisions stop.
2. **A decision without a reversal condition cannot be revisited.** Without one, the only time a decision is reopened is when it already hurts.
3. **Three slips is a decision nobody has made.** Stop chasing it and put it to the founder as a yes or a no.
4. **Anything owned by the founder and not on the calendar is not going to happen.** The calendar is the real plan.
5. **The review is for decisions, the tracker is for status.** Mixing them produces a meeting people skip.
6. **Write the note during the meeting.** A note written the next day is a reconstruction, and reconstructions favour whoever writes them.
