# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-05 | Weekly review length | 45 minutes, fixed slot, one page | Length is where reviews turn into status meetings |
| 2026-11-05 | Number of metrics | Four, chosen by whether they change the business | A dashboard of fourteen changes nothing |
| 2026-11-05 | Does every decision need a reversal condition? | Yes, observable and dated | Otherwise it is a preference, not a decision |
| 2026-11-05 | Who owns a commitment assigned in a meeting? | Only the person who accepted the date | Assigned but unaccepted is how trackers fill with ghosts |
| 2026-11-05 | Does this role decide for the founder? | No - options, trade-off, recommendation, then record the choice | The log is only useful if it reflects real decisions |
| 2026-11-05 | Where do slipped commitments get escalated? | In the review, by name, visibly | Private chasing is why the same items slip for months |
