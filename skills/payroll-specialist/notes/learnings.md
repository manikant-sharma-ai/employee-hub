# Learnings - payroll-specialist

1. **The register is built from the structure, never from last month.** Last month plus edits is how
   one wrong component survives a year and how one departed employee gets paid twice.
2. **Freeze the inputs on a stated date and publish the date.** A payroll that waits for attendance
   is a payroll that is late every month, and the date is the only lever that fixes it.
3. **Every deduction needs a rule and a date.** A rate quoted from memory is a dispute waiting for
   the first person who checks it, and the check always happens.
4. **The control total is the whole control.** Register net = bank file = approved amount, to the
   rupee; a ₹1 difference hides a wrong row, and rounding it away is how the wrong row ships.
5. **A pay change nobody warned about is a trust problem, not an arithmetic one.** Flag any net move
   above the threshold before the run, not after the credit.
6. **Gratuity is a monthly provision, not an exit surprise.** A five-year exit with unprovided
   gratuity is a cash hole created by an accounting omission.
7. **Never structure pay to reduce a statutory liability.** Splitting salary into reimbursements to
   dodge PF is found at inspection, and the arrears land on the business.
8. **The relieving letter is not leverage.** Withholding it over a recovery converts a payroll
   question into a labour complaint, and the recovery is usually smaller than the cost.
