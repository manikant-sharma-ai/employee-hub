# Full and final settlement

An exit is a computation with a deadline and a feelings problem attached. Do both properly.

## The computation, line by line

| Line | Rule | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Salary for days worked | Monthly gross x days paid / days in month | State the basis: calendar or working days |
| Leave encashment | Earned leave balance x gross or basic per day, per policy | Encashment of sick or casual leave is usually not allowed |
| Notice pay - employee side | Shortfall in notice recovered, per the contract | Recovery needs a contract clause; without it, it is a dispute |
| Notice pay - employer side | Pay in lieu where the business waives notice | Taxable as salary |
| Gratuity | 15/26 x last basic + DA x completed years, after the qualifying service | Ceiling applies; part-year above six months is often rounded, per the Act |
| Bonus | Pro-rata for the months worked, where the Act applies | Calculation ceiling is not the eligibility ceiling |
| Recoveries | Advances, loans, asset not returned, training bond | Only with a written basis and, ideally, the employee's acknowledgement |
| Full and final amount | Sum, with TDS on the taxable parts | Gratuity within the exemption is not taxed; encashment has its own limit |
| Settlement date | Per policy, typically 30-45 days | State the date and hold to it |

## The exit checklist

1. Resignation or termination letter on file, with the last working day stated.
2. Notice period served, waived or recovered - decided in writing before the last day.
3. Handover signed off by the manager, including files, credentials and assets.
4. Leave balance confirmed by HR, not by memory.
5. Loan and advance balance confirmed by finance.
6. Statutory exit steps: PF member record, ESI, and the UAN left intact so the member can transfer.
7. Full-and-final statement issued, with the computation visible, not just a net figure.
8. Relieving letter and experience certificate issued on the settlement date.
9. Register updated: exit date, reason, and the final settlement reference.

## Gratuity, the four things that decide it

- **Qualifying service**: five years of continuous service, with the statutory relaxation for death
  and disablement.
- **Rate**: 15 days' wages per completed year, on the last drawn basic plus DA.
- **Ceiling**: the statutory exempt ceiling; amounts above it are taxable.
- **Payment**: within the statutory period after it becomes payable, with interest for delay.

## The conversation

An exit settlement is the last impression and the first reference check. Three rules: give the
computation in writing before the last day, explain every deduction with its basis, and never let a
recovery be a surprise on the final day. A disputed ₹8,000 costs more in time and reputation than the
recovery is worth.

## Anti-patterns

- **Withholding the relieving letter** to force a recovery. It is leverage that becomes a legal
  complaint.
- **Recovering notice pay without a clause.** The clause is the whole case.
- **Settling without the handover.** Credentials and assets leave with the person.
- **Forgetting the statutory exit.** A PF record left un-transferred is the employee's problem for
  years and their memory of the employer is set by it.
- **No settlement date.** "We will process it" is how a settlement becomes a labour-office visit.
- **Merging the settlement into a normal payroll run** without a separate line, so the variance is
  unexplainable a quarter later.

## The letter

The settlement statement is a document the employee keeps and may show a lawyer. It carries:

1. **The dates** - joining, last working day, settlement date.
2. **The components**, each on its own line, with the rule beside it: salary for days worked, leave
   encashed (and the balance it came from), gratuity (and the years counted), bonus, notice.
3. **The recoveries**, each with its basis and, where it exists, the employee's written acknowledgement.
4. **The statutory deductions** on the taxable parts, named.
5. **The net amount and the payment reference**, once paid.
6. **The documents issued** - relieving letter, experience certificate, Form 16 status, PF and ESI
   position.
7. **A contact for queries**, with a name, for ninety days.

Anything not on the letter does not exist, and anything on it without a rule becomes the argument.
