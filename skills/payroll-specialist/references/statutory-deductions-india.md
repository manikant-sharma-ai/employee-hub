# Statutory deductions in India

Every rate below is a starting point, not a fact. Confirm the current rate, ceiling and due date at
the official source before you compute, and record the date you confirmed it.

## The four deductions, in one table

| Head | Employee share | Employer share | Coverage test | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) | 12% of basic + DA | 12%, of which 8.33% to EPS capped, balance to EPF, plus EDLI and admin charges | Basic + DA within the statutory wage ceiling; optional above it by agreement | 15th of the following month |
| ESI (Employees' State Insurance) | 0.75% of gross | 3.25% of gross | Gross wages within the statutory ceiling, and the establishment covered | 15th of the following month |
| Professional tax | State schedule, salary-based | Nil | State of the employee's posting; registration per state | State-specific, often monthly or half-yearly |
| TDS on salary (Section 192) | Computed on projected annual income | Nil | Every employee, on the regime elected | 7th of the following month |

The employer's PF share is not 12% of what the employee contributed in every case: the EPS portion
is capped, so the split inside the employer's 12% matters for the member's pension and for the
challan.

## EPF, the parts that cause disputes

| Question | Answer | Where it goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Which wages? | Basic + DA, plus retaining allowance and most regular allowances | "Special allowance" that is really salary excluded to reduce PF - an inspection finding |
| Which ceiling? | The statutory monthly wage ceiling for mandatory coverage | Applying the ceiling to the contribution instead of to the eligibility |
| Employee above the ceiling | May be kept out if never a member; once a member, generally continues | Removing an existing member to save 12% |
| International worker | Different rules, and a certificate of coverage may apply | Assuming the Indian rule applies to a seconded employee |
| VPF | Voluntary, employee's choice, over and above 12% | Deducting VPF without a written request |
| Withdrawal vs transfer | Transfer on job change, withdrawal only on defined conditions | Advising a withdrawal that closes the member's pension service |
| Nomination | EPS and PF nominations filed and updated | A nomination missing at a death claim is a family's worst year |

## ESI, the parts that cause disputes

- Coverage is on gross wages, not basic, and it includes most allowances.
- The test is the wage at the start of the contribution period; crossing the ceiling mid-period does
  not immediately end coverage - the rules on continuing contribution until the period ends matter.
- Once covered, the employer's 3.25% is not optional and not a "benefit"; it buys the employee
  medical cover the business would otherwise face as a liability.
- New establishments have a registration threshold; confirm it before assuming exemption.

## Professional tax

State-driven and small in amount, and the most common source of small errors:

| State pattern | What to check |
|---|---|
| Monthly slab | The slab for the month's gross, not the annual CTC |
| Half-yearly or annual payment | Some states collect in one or two instalments; the deduction is still monthly |
| Exemption for certain months | A few states exempt a month or a salary band |
| Registration and enrolment | The establishment registers; each employee may need enrolment |
| Cap | Several states cap the annual deduction |

## TDS on salary, the practical version

1. **Regime first, per person, per year.** Record the election; do not assume.
2. **Project the annual figure** from the structure plus confirmed variable pay, not from the month.
3. **Apply the standard deduction and the exemptions that survive the regime.**
4. **Add previous-employer income** for a joiner, and collect the prior Form 12B details.
5. **Recalculate in January** once declarations and proofs are in; the last quarter absorbs the
   correction, which is why a March net pay often moves.
6. **Never promise a refund or a final tax liability.** Projection is a projection; the return decides.

## Anti-patterns

- **Quoting a rate from memory.** These change with a Finance Act and a notification.
- **One state's professional tax applied to everyone.** Location is a payroll field, not an afterthought.
- **PF on gross.** Over-deduction is also a dispute, and the excess is refunded slowly.
- **Ignoring the employer share in the budget.** It is 13-16% of payroll that nobody plans for.
- **Contractors on the payroll register.** Different section, different rate, different return.
- **Filing without a reconciliation** between the twelve monthly challans and the annual return.
