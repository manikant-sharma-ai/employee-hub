# Learnings - voice-and-audio

1. **The room decides the episode.** Every recurring "hollow" or "echoey" note traces to a hard surface and a mic 40 cm from a mouth. Fix the room in the brief, not in the chain.
2. **Never describe a sound you have not heard.** The transcript gives words, the person gives tone. Everything else is a number: peak, LUFS, duration, file count.
3. **Loudness is measured, not felt.** -16 LUFS integrated and -1 dBTP on every podcast master; -14 LUFS for anything vertical. Stated on the master, every time.
4. **The phone speaker is the real test.** A mix that only works on headphones is unfinished for an Indian audience on cheap earbuds and a car stereo.
5. **Every name gets a pronunciation guide.** It is the cheapest quality signal in the show and the one listeners forgive least.
6. **Repair time per finished minute is the health metric.** If it climbs, the recording setup is the problem and the edit is where it shows up.
