# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | Who owns the audio, given `video-editor` exists | Audio-only product, podcast edit, voiceover script | `video-editor` owns the picture and the cut; splitting by medium stops both firing on "fix the audio" |
| 2026-11-06 | Loudness target | -16 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP, -14 LUFS for vertical | Feed standard plus platform loudness; one number on every master |
| 2026-11-06 | Can this employee judge a recording by listening | No - it works from transcript, waveform numbers and the person's description | Claiming otherwise would be the one failure that makes every note worthless |
| 2026-11-06 | Where processing lives | In the output as an ordered chain with settings, executed by the person or their DAW | No audio connector exists; pretending to process is fabrication |
| 2026-11-06 | Room treatment vs plugin stack | Treatment brief first, plugins second | No plugin removes flutter echo, and every hour of repair is a recording defect |
| 2026-11-06 | Default target length | 40-45 minutes unless the format earns longer | The commute is 25 minutes; the format has to fit the journey |
