# Show notes and clips

One episode should work for a month: the feed, the search results, five clips and
a newsletter paragraph. That is a writing job, not a publishing job.

## Show notes structure

```
<One line: the promise of the episode, in the listener's words.>

<Guest name> is <role> at <company>. In this episode:
- <payoff 1, with the number or the story>
- <payoff 2>
- <payoff 3>

Chapters
00:00 - <name of the payoff>
06:12 - <name of the payoff>
...

Links
<Guest's company, the book, the report mentioned, the tool>

<One paragraph for the newsletter, 40-60 words, quotable.>
```

The first 150 characters are what appears in the player. Write them last.

## Chapters, done properly

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Name the payoff, not the topic | "The ₹4 lakh warehouse mistake" beats "Operations" |
| One idea per chapter | Two ideas make a chapter nobody clicks |
| Minimum 90 seconds apart | Platforms reject or hide very short chapters |
| First chapter at 00:00 | Always, even if it is the cold open |
| Same names in ID3, YouTube and notes | Three names for one chapter is three weak search results |

## Choosing the clips

Read the transcript and mark every place where the guest says something a
stranger would repeat. Then apply three tests:

1. **Standalone** - does it make sense with no setup?
2. **Specific** - is there a number, a name or a concrete action in it?
3. **Emotional or contrarian** - would someone disagree or feel something?

A clip that fails two of the three is a clip for the archive.

## Clip brief format

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| In / out | 00:14:22 - 00:15:04 |
| Hook line (first 3 s) | "We lost ₹4 lakh on a warehouse we never used." |
| Why it works | Specific loss, self-implicating, ends on a lesson |
| Caption text | Burned, 2 lines max, 42 characters per line |
| Platform | Reels 9:16, LinkedIn 1:1, YouTube Shorts 9:16 |
| CTA | "Full episode, link in bio" |

Three to five per episode. Fewer, better clips beat eight that are cut from the
same two minutes.

## Titles that get clicked without lying

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Episode 42 with Rohan" | "He sold 40,000 kurtas and kept 3% margin" |
| "Discussing pricing" | "Why he raised prices 20% and lost no customers" |
| "Startup journey" | "The month his co-founder quit and the orders doubled" |

The test: could the guest read the title and agree it is what they said?

## The repurposing chain

| Asset | Time cost | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 clips | 45 min | Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn |
| Newsletter paragraph | 10 min | The weekly email |
| 3 quote cards | 15 min | LinkedIn carousel, Instagram |
| Blog post | 60 min | Site, search traffic |

## Anti-patterns

- **A transcript pasted as show notes.** It is not searchable, not skimmable and nobody reads it.
- **Chapters named after the question asked.** Name the answer.
- **Clips with no caption.** 70% of feed viewing is silent.
- **A clip that starts with "so, as I was saying".** The hook is the first three seconds or it is nothing.
- **Publishing the notes a week after the episode.** The window is the first 48 hours.
