## How to fill this
- **The person who holds the footage** - and how fast they upload originals rather than WhatsApp-compressed copies.
- **The brand kit owner** - who sends fonts, logo lockups and caption style, and in what format.
- **The music licence holder** - which subscription covers which channel, and whether ads are included.
- **The voice-over artist** - language, register, turnaround, rate.
- **The reviewer** - who signs off the cut, and whether they review on a phone or a laptop (it changes what they notice).
- **The publisher** - who uploads and schedules, since this employee does not publish.

Keep names and roles only. No personal phone numbers or client customer data in this file.
