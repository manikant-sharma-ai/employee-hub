# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-02 - Read the transcript before the footage. The hook is almost never the first sentence the speaker said, and finding it in text takes minutes instead of hours.
- 2026-11-02 - Cut the lead-in and the trailing silence from every clip first. It is the cheapest pace gain in editing and it makes every later decision easier.
- 2026-11-02 - Audio decides whether a cut feels professional. Level every clip before the mix, and state the loudness target rather than trusting the ear.
- 2026-11-02 - Say plainly which frames were seen and which motion was not judged. A confident edit note written from a transcript alone is the fastest way to lose the client's trust on delivery.
