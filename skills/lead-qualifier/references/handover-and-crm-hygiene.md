# Handover and CRM hygiene

A qualified lead is a handover, not a status. This file is the note, the fields and the stage discipline that make
the next person's hour productive.

## The handover note, six lines

| Line | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What they are trying to do, in their words | The next person opens with their language, not ours |
| 2 | Why now - the trigger | It decides the urgency and the follow-up |
| 3 | What they use today, and what is wrong with it | The displacement angle, and the objections to expect |
| 4 | The number and the date, or the honest absence | The two facts that make a forecast |
| 5 | Who else is involved, and who signs | The deal shape |
| 6 | The next step and the date | The only line that stops the lead going cold |

Plus one line the salesperson will thank you for: **the objection to expect**, based on the segment. A
distributor will ask about credit terms; a founder will ask about implementation time; a procurement team will
ask about GST and delivery.

## The fields that must be filled, or the lead is not qualified

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Source | Required. The channel, not "website" for everything |
| Owner | Required. A named person |
| Score - fit | 1-5 with the reason in the note |
| Score - intent | 1-5 with the evidence |
| Disposition | Call today / nurture / partner / self-serve / closed |
| Next step date | Required for anything that is not closed |
| Language | The language the reply should be in |
| Requirement | One line, in their words |

If the CRM does not have a field, put it in the note. Never in a spreadsheet outside the CRM, which is where
leads go to be forgotten.

## Stage definitions, written once

| Stage | Means exactly | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| New | Arrived, not yet read | Anything read but unscored |
| Scored | Fit and intent assessed, disposition set | A score with no disposition |
| Routed | Owner assigned, deadline set, acknowledgement sent | Owner assigned, nothing sent |
| Contacted | A human spoke or wrote, specifically | A template was sent |
| Qualified | Need, timeline and the decision route established | They replied |
| Meeting | A dated conversation with the right people | "Interested in a demo" |
| Proposal | A number went out | A price was discussed |
| Won / Lost | A decision, with a reason | Silence |

The distinction between Routed and Contacted is the one that fixes most pipelines, because it stops a template
being counted as a conversation.

## Deduplication, before it becomes a project

| Match on | Action |
|---|---|
| Same phone number | Merge; keep the earliest source, the latest note |
| Same email, different name | Suspect a shared inbox or a reseller; ask, do not merge blind |
| Same company, three contacts | One account, three contacts, one owner |
| Same person from two channels | Merge, and credit the first source |
| An existing customer enquiring | Route to `customer-success`, do not create a lead |

Deduplicating is not this role's job at scale - that is `crm-hygienist` (backlog #142). But never create a
duplicate knowingly, and never merge two real people because the names are similar.

## What the salesperson actually wants in the handover

1. The one-line reason to call.
2. The opening question.
3. The objection to expect.
4. The number and the date.
5. The name of who decides.

Anything else is research they did not ask for. Put it in the note, not in the first message.

## The weekly source read, in five lines

1. Leads and qualified leads by source, this week and last.
2. Median first-response time by source.
3. Qualified-to-meeting rate.
4. The one source to fund, with the number behind it.
5. The one source to stop or fix, with the reason.

Five lines, Monday morning, and the founder can make a spend decision without opening an ad account.

## Anti-patterns

- **A handover that is a data dump.** Twelve fields and no sentence is not a handover.
- **"Interested" as a stage.** It means nothing and forecasts worse.
- **A lead with no next-step date.** It is the single best predictor of a lead that dies.
- **Fixing the CRM by exporting to a spreadsheet.** Two sources of truth is one too many.
- **Merging duplicates by name.** In India, two people with the same name in the same city are common, and one
  of them is a real customer.
