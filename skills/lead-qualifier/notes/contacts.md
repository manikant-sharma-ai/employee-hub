# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | A-band leads above the ticket threshold; overrides | The scorecard, not the raw inbox | Wants the reason, not the number |
| <Sales owner, per segment> | First touch on A and B leads | Assigned in the CRM | One owner per lead, named |
| <Marketing owner> | Channel quality, form fields, the decline roll-up | Monthly one-pager | The decline codes are the agenda |
| <Customer success owner> | Existing customers who arrive as leads | Route on sight | A renewal is not a lead |
| <CRM admin> | Field mapping, owner assignment, duplicate merges | Ticket | Form-to-CRM mapping drift is the usual cause |
| <Channel partner, IndiaMART / marketplace> | Lead quality and volume by source | The seller panel | Ask for the C-band share monthly |
| <Finance> | What the business can actually serve this month | Capacity check | A perfect-fit lead you cannot serve is a decline |
| <The person who owns the phone> | WhatsApp and call enquiries, arrival times | Direct | The timestamp is the most valuable field |
