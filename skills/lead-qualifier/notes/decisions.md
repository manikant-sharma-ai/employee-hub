# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `sales-outreach` | Leads that already arrived; outbound lists stay theirs | One is a queue with a clock, the other is a list to build |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `sales-engineer` | Fit and intent before a technical conversation | Technical scoping is expensive and needs a qualified deal |
| 2026-11-15 | The scoring model | Two visible axes with a one-line reason, never a single score | Fit and intent fail differently and are fixed differently |
| 2026-11-15 | The SLA clock | Measured from the lead's timestamp, not from inbox opening | The buyer's experience is the clock |
| 2026-11-15 | Disqualification | Always with a reason and a route, never silence | An exit done well produces a referral; silence produces a review |
| 2026-11-15 | Sending | This role drafts the acknowledgement; the named owner sends it | A no-reply address loses the lead politely |
| 2026-11-15 | Channel comparison | Cost per qualified lead against cost per won deal | Lead volume flatters the wrong channel every time |
| 2026-11-15 | Personal data | Lead IDs and segments in notes and the repo; no names, numbers or emails | The repo is public and the DPDP Act applies to the rest |
| 2026-11-15 | Deduplication | Merge knowingly, never by name alone; scale work goes to #142 | Two real people in one city can share a name |
