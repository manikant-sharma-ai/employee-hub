# Learnings - lead-qualifier

1. **A score without a written ICP is a preference with a number attached.** The rubric has to exist
   before the batch, not after the first twenty leads have been looked at.
2. **Unknown is a value, and it scores zero.** The instinct to fill in a plausible employee count is
   the single most damaging habit in qualification, because it is invisible in the output.
3. **A lead with more than 40% of its weight unknown is a question, not a decision.** That is what
   band D exists for, and using it is what keeps good leads from being declined early.
4. **Speed decides more inbound than the pitch does.** A same-day first touch on an A lead outperforms
   a better pitch three days later, which is why the SLA sits inside the score rather than beside it.
5. **Channel is a tiebreaker, never a ranking.** A WhatsApp enquiry from a good-fit company outranks a
   beautifully formatted form from a bad one.
6. **Every decline needs a code.** The roll-up of codes is the only feedback loop the marketing spend
   has, and free-text declines cannot be rolled up.
7. **One question can be worth 20 points.** Ask the two that matter - headcount and what they use
   today - in the first reply, not in a form with ten fields.
8. **A B band without a date becomes a C with nobody deciding.** Nurture is a dated reason, not a
   holding pen.
