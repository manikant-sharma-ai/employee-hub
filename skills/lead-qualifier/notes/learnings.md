# Learnings - lead-qualifier

1. **The first hour decides the deal, and in India the enquiry arrives at 9:40pm.** The SLA is measured from the
   lead's timestamp, and the promise has to survive a Sunday.
2. **Fit and intent are two axes, never one score.** A perfect fit with no intent is a nurture; a poor fit with
   high intent is a referral, and the referral is often worth more than the call.
3. **Every lead gets a decision, including the ones that get nothing.** A queue with no exits grows until it is
   ignored, and the good leads die in it.
4. **"Our team will contact you shortly" is a receipt, not a response.** Four specific lines beat any template.
5. **A score without a reason cannot be argued with, and cannot teach the model anything.** One line per lead,
   every time.
6. **Response time is blamed on lead quality.** Before any channel is cut, measure the clock; the read is almost
   always the delay.
7. **Volume is what the channel sells.** Qualified leads are what the business buys, and cost per qualified lead
   is the only channel comparison that survives contact with a close rate.
8. **Answer the channel the lead arrived on.** A WhatsApp lead answered by email is a lead lost politely.
9. **Disqualify the business need, never the person.** No scoring on name, region as a proxy for community, or
   any protected characteristic.
10. **A lead with no next-step date is a lead that has already died.** The date is the discipline.
