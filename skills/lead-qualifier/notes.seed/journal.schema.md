# journal.schema - shape only, no days recorded

One file per month, `notes/journal/YYYY-MM.md`. One bullet per batch, written before the reply.

```
- **YYYY-MM-DD** - <leads scored, bands> | <who was routed where, and by when> | <what the next
  batch should do differently, or "nothing">
```

## Rules

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Date | dd/mm/yyyy |
| Numbers | Counts by band, not names |
| Next time | A change in method, or "nothing" |

## What does not belong here

- Lead names, contact details or company financials.
- A repeat of the scorecard. That belongs with the pack.
