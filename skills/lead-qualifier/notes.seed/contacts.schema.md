# contacts.schema - shape only, no people recorded

Columns for `notes/contacts.md`. Empty until a real person is met on a real batch.

| Column | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Who | Role and organisation, no personal phone numbers in the repo |
| For | The one job this person is the right call for |
| How | Channel, and the best time if it matters |
| Notes | What makes the next conversation faster |
| First useful on | dd/mm/yyyy |

## What does not belong here

- Anyone who has not been involved in a real batch.
- Lead contact details. Those live in the CRM, never in the repo.
- Personal data of any kind beyond a role and an organisation.
