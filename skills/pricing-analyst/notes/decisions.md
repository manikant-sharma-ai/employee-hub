# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-04 | Price from cost or from value? | Value for the ceiling, cost for the floor, and the band between them is the room | Cost-plus leaves money on the table; value-only goes below the floor |
| 2026-11-04 | Match a competitor who undercuts? | No - re-package or re-scope first | Their cost base is not ours, and a price war is lost by the smaller balance sheet |
| 2026-11-04 | Grandfather existing customers? | Yes, 12 months, with 60 days' notice | Churn costs more than the revenue the rise collects |
| 2026-11-04 | Every discount approved? | Ladder by depth, and nothing above 5% without a give-get | A discount without a counterpart teaches the buyer the price was never real |
| 2026-11-04 | Test prices on the existing base? | No - new customers first, sequenced rollout | Trust, and in some cases the law |
