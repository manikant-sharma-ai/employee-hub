# Learnings - pricing-analyst

1. **The realised price is the price.** A list price with an 18% median discount is a different product with a paperwork habit.
2. **Cutting price demands volume that is almost never there.** On a 40% contribution margin, a 10% cut needs 33% more volume to hold profit; a 10% rise survives a 20% volume loss.
3. **Discounts buy nothing when the win rate at full price matches the win rate at 20% off.** Measure that before designing any policy.
4. **The floor is arithmetic, and it belongs in the quoting tool.** A floor in a founder's head is discovered by the tenth salesperson.
5. **Grandfather existing customers, and give notice.** A surprise rise is a churn event with a date on it.
6. **Every change gets a reversal trigger written before it ships.** Otherwise a bad decision becomes the baseline within a quarter.
