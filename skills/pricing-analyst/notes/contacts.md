# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder / approver> | Sign-off on the price and the discount ladder | | Signs the one-page memo |
| <Sales head> | Discount behaviour, win-loss reasons | | The person who knows what the objection really is |
| <Top discounter> | The deals that leak | | Audit their last 20 deals, not their intentions |
| <CA / accountant> | True variable cost, GST treatment of the price | | Cost lines, not tax advice from this role |
| <Billing / gateway owner> | Changing the price in the system | | Executes; this role recommends |
| <5-10 customers, per segment> | Willingness-to-pay evidence | | Ask permission before quoting them |

## Rules

- Record the segment next to every customer name; a finding from a 5-person firm does not transfer.
- Never store payment details or personal identifiers in these notes.
