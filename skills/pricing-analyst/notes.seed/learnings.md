## How to fill this

Add a row when a pricing change teaches something reusable.

- **Elasticity observed** - what happened to volume when the price moved, by segment.
- **Cost surprises** - the fee, freight or return cost that was under-modelled.
- **Objections** - what the price is compared against, in the customer's words.
- **Discount behaviour** - which segment asks, how often, and what they accept instead.

No customer names, no individual deal terms. The pattern, not the account.
