## How to fill this

- **The CA** - GST treatment, discount versus credit note, and any statutory rate.
- **`bookkeeper`** - actual COGS, fee and returns figures, rather than estimates.
- **`finance-ops`** - what the close shows against the model.
- **`research-analyst`** - competitor and market evidence, with sources.
- **`sales-outreach` owner** - who negotiates, and what they are allowed to concede.
- **`quote-generator`** - where an approved price becomes a customer document.
- **The payment and marketplace account owners** - settlement reports and fee schedules.
