# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Inventory planner> | Reorder policy, safety stock, the quantity that becomes an order | Shared sheet, monthly | Consumes the forecast; owns the policy this role does not set |
| <Purchase or procurement owner> | Lead times, MOQ, supplier capacity, advance booking for the peak | Email, before the season | The peak order is placed weeks before the peak |
| <ERP or data owner> | Sales export, stockout record, SKU master, unit conversions | A scheduled export | The stockout record decides whether the forecast is usable |
| <Sales head> | Promotion plan, known large orders, distributor commitments | Monthly, week 4 | Known orders are added separately, never smoothed in |
| <Finance owner> | Cash timing of the purchase, working capital | Monthly | The forecast's value output goes here, not to the buyer |
| <Marketplace account manager> | Sale event dates, inbound cut-offs, fee changes | Seller panel and phone | The inbound cut-off is the real deadline, not the event date |
| <Distributor or channel partner> | Secondary sales, stock in the channel, order intentions | Email or WhatsApp | Without secondary, the forecast is of purchases, not consumption |
| <Production or plant lead> | Capacity, changeover, batch minimums | Monthly | A forecast the plant cannot make is not a plan |
| <Founder> | The three assumptions that would change the number | Monthly, one page | Wants the range and the reason, not the model |
