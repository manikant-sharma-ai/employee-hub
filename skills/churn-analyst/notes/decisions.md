# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `customer-success` | The pattern across accounts; the single account's save plan stays theirs | One is a cohort question, the other is a relationship question |
| 2026-11-16 | What this role owns, given `feedback-synthesiser` | Counted reason codes from exits; the cross-channel theme list stays theirs | A theme list does not carry tenure, price or revenue lost |
| 2026-11-16 | The churn definition | Voluntary cancellation and non-renewal are reported separately; the headline names both | Hiding failed payments in either bucket flatters one of them |
| 2026-11-16 | Churn date | End-of-service date, not request date | It is the month the revenue actually stopped |
| 2026-11-16 | Reason taxonomy | Nine codes plus `unknown`, added never renamed, reviewed every six months | A renamed code breaks the trend line |
| 2026-11-16 | `unknown` handling | Reported as a measured gap, never redistributed across other codes | Redistribution invents evidence |
| 2026-11-16 | Save evaluation | Control group required, otherwise the result is labelled unattributed | Saves claimed by the person who offered them are not data |
| 2026-11-16 | Discounts | Not a first play; capped, time-boxed, tied to a commitment | An open discount becomes the price list |
| 2026-11-16 | Customer contact | None by this role; the list goes to the owner | Two employees contacting one customer is how a save becomes an exit |
| 2026-11-16 | Personal data | Account IDs and segments only in reports, notes and the repo | DPDP Act obligations and a public repo |
