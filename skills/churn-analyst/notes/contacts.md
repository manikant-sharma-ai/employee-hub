# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Founder> | The read, the save-play ceiling, any offer above the authorised discount | Direct, monthly | Wants one page and the rupees |
| <Finance owner> | MRR base, failed payments, refunds, revenue churn reconciliation | The books, monthly | Revenue churn must match the ledger, not the CRM |
| <Subscription / billing vendor support> | Dunning settings, cancellation reasons in the portal, retry logic | Vendor console and phone | Failed payments are churn in a different costume |
| <Helpdesk admin> | Ticket export, tags, the ninety days before each exit | The helpdesk export | Tag quality decides how much of the cause is visible |
| <Account owner 1> | The save call on red accounts, segment A | The CRM | Supplies the real reason the form missed |
| <Account owner 2> | The save call on red accounts, segment B | The CRM | Knows which customers respond to which play |
| <Product or engineering lead> | Feature dates that can be promised in a `missing-feature` save | A ticket with a date | Never promise a date this role has not been given |
| <Data or BI owner> | Usage export, event definitions, the customer table | A scheduled export | Event definitions drift; ask for the definition, not the chart |
| <CA or auditor> | Whether a retention credit is a revenue adjustment | Email | Affects how a save is booked, not whether it works |
| <Analytics vendor> | Cohort tooling when the spreadsheet is outgrown | Console | Only after the definitions are settled |
