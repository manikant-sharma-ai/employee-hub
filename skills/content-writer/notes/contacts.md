# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

## How to fill this
- **Subject-matter expert** - who actually knows the mechanism being written about, and how long they take to answer three questions.
- **Customers who agreed to be quoted** - name, role, the exact quote approved, and the date approved. Never re-use a quote for a new claim.
- **Reviewers** - legal, compliance, or the founder, and what each insists on.
- **Editor** - who does the final line pass, and their style quirks.
- **Designer** - who turns a piece into a carousel or a one-pager, and how much lead time they need.
