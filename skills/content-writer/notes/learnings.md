# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-01 - Get the proof before the prose. A piece with no number, no scene and no admission is opinion with better formatting, however cleanly it is written.
- 2026-11-01 - Voice is an input, not a polish. Ask for two samples before drafting; writing first and matching afterwards always reads as a costume.
- 2026-11-01 - Count the tells rather than feeling them. More than one of the top five humanizer tells in 300 words is the point where a reader decides a machine wrote it.
