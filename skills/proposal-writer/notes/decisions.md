# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | What this role owns, given `sales-outreach`, `tender-bidder` and `legal-drafter` | The written response to a named requirements document: matrix, themes, solution, commercials, submission | Outbound creates interest; a bid answers a document that will be scored |
| 2026-11-10 | Tender discovery and eligibility | `tender-bidder` (backlog #121) when it ships; a manual eligibility check until then | Discovery and eligibility are a different job from writing the response |
| 2026-11-10 | Who sets the price | The founder, `pricing-analyst` or finance; this role builds the cost-up and presents options | A writer who also sets the price argues for the win instead of the margin |
| 2026-11-10 | Deviations | Always declared, with a reason and an alternative | An undeclared deviation surfaces at contract and costs the deal |
| 2026-11-10 | Page limits | Treated as hard limits, with a written page budget before drafting | Exceeding the limit is penalised or disqualified |
| 2026-11-10 | Freeze | T-48 hours, then fixes only; checklist run by a second person | Editing to the deadline ships an unchecked page |
| 2026-11-10 | Base option | Must be fully compliant; options add value, never remove compliance | An option that fails a mandatory requirement is a disqualification |
| 2026-11-10 | Confidentiality | No client documents, price build-ups or personal contact details in the repo or in notes | The repo is public and bid documents are confidential |
