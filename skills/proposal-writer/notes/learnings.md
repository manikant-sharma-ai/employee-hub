# Learnings - proposal-writer

1. **The matrix is the proposal.** Every mandatory row answered explicitly, in the client's
   words, with a page reference; prose that does not answer a row is either evidence for one
   or it is cut.
2. **Read the submission instructions before the requirements.** Format, page limit, EMD and
   channel disqualify a better proposal than the one that wins, and they are read last.
3. **Deviations are declared.** A declared deviation with a credible alternative is
   evaluated; an undeclared one is discovered at contract, at the worst possible moment.
4. **Win themes come from the scored criteria, with a proof point attached.** A theme the
   evaluator cannot tick is an adjective.
5. **Every claim needs a number.** "Three transitions of 100,000+ sq ft in 24 months" scores;
   "extensive experience" is scored as absent.
6. **Price is built from cost, with assumptions and exclusions written down.** An assumption
   not written is a cost the company absorbs; an exclusion not written is a scope dispute.
7. **Freeze at T-48 hours and run the checklist with a second person.** The last 48 hours are
   for checking, not writing.
8. **Ask for the debrief on every loss.** It is the cheapest market research available and
   the only way the next bid starts ahead.
9. **Never state that a bid is submitted without a confirmation reference.** An
   acknowledgement is the only proof that it exists.
