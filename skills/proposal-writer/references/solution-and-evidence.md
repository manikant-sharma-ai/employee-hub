# Solution and evidence

Evaluators score against criteria, and most criteria reward one thing: evidence
that the claim is true.

## The paragraph shape that scores

For each requirement:

```
[RESTATE] The buyer asked for <requirement, in their words>.
[ANSWER]   We will <what we do>, delivered by <who>, from <when>.
[EVIDENCE] This is the same model we run for <reference>, where <number, date>.
[POINTER]  See section <n>, page <p>.
```

Four sentences. The restate step feels repetitive and it is the single highest
value habit in proposal writing: the evaluator reads the requirement, then reads
your answer, and never has to bridge the two.

## Evidence that survives a check

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Extensive experience in healthcare" | "Three hospital deployments: <a> (2023), <b> (2024), <c> (2025); reference contact for <a> agreed" |
| "ISO certified" | "ISO 27001:2022, certificate <number>, valid to <date>, issued by <body>" |
| "High customer satisfaction" | "CSAT 4.6/5 across 1,240 tickets, Jan-Sep 2026, measured in <tool>" |
| "Strong team" | "Delivery lead: <name>, 11 years, led <project>; 6 engineers, 4 based in <city>" |
| "We are flexible" | "Change requests priced at <rate>, approved within 5 working days" |

Every strong claim has a number, a date and a source. If it has none of the three,
it is a claim, not evidence.

## References

- **Ask before naming.** One message: what the buyer will be asked, by whom, and roughly when.
- **Brief the referee** with the three questions the buyer will ask.
- **One referee per claim**, not one referee for the whole document.
- **Never name a client who has not agreed**, even a logo. It ends relationships and, in some contracts, breaches confidentiality.

## Differentiators that actually differentiate

A differentiator is something the buyer cannot get from the other two bidders. "We
care about quality" is not one. "We hold stock in Pune, so a replacement reaches
you in 24 hours" is. Ask: which of the competing bids could say this sentence
truthfully? If all of them, it is not a differentiator.

## The red-team pass

Read the finished document twice:

1. **As the evaluator** - can I score every criterion from this document alone, without calling anyone?
2. **As the delivery lead** - can I deliver every sentence in this document with the team I have, at this price, in this timeline?

The second pass is the one that saves the company money.
