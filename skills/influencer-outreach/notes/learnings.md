# Learnings - influencer-outreach

1. **A rate is a number with a denominator.** Every recommendation carries cost per thousand impressions and cost per expected order; a fee quoted without a ceiling calculation is a guess.
2. **Audience before follower count.** Geography, language and comment quality decide conversion; tier only decides the price band.
3. **Negotiate the deal, not the fee.** Rights, deliverables and exclusivity move the value more than the headline number, and rights are where the expensive mistakes hide.
4. **Disclosure is the brand's exposure too.** It goes in the brief, in the supplied caption text and in the approval checklist - never left to the creator's memory.
5. **Payment follows proof.** Live link plus screenshot is the trigger, written into the brief before the fee is agreed.
6. **Micro creators win on cost per order; macro wins on credibility.** Budget accordingly, and keep the bench of repeatable micro names.
