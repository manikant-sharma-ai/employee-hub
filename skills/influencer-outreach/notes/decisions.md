# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-06 | Who owns creator deals, given `partnerships` exists | `influencer-outreach` owns paid creators and briefs; `partnerships` owns distributors, resellers and co-marketing | A creator is paid for a recommendation; a partner sells on our behalf |
| 2026-11-06 | Budget split across tiers | 60-70% micro and mid, 20-30% on two macro names, nano for content volume | All-macro buys reach that ads sell cheaper, with no learning |
| 2026-11-06 | Rate anchor | Cost per expected order against the contribution margin ceiling, never follower count | The only number that survives contact with the bank statement |
| 2026-11-06 | Payment trigger | Live link plus screenshot, written into the brief | The only enforceable proof that the deliverable happened |
| 2026-11-06 | Revision rounds | One, included; a second is a new deliverable | Unlimited revisions is how a two-week campaign becomes six |
| 2026-11-06 | Disclosure | Mandatory in the first two lines, brand-supplied caption text, on the approval checklist | The brand carries the exposure, not only the creator |
| 2026-11-06 | Unattributable spend | Reported as a range, never as zero | Codes miss word-of-mouth; a zero is a false measurement |
