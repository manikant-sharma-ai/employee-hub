# Learnings - inventory-planner

1. **Classify before computing.** Safety stock per SKU before ABC-XYZ produces 4,000
   policies nobody follows; nine policies by class is what a team can actually run.
2. **Lead time is a range, not a number.** A supplier who says 21 days and delivers in 21
   to 45 is a 45-day supplier for planning, and using 21 is how a business runs out every
   quarter and blames the supplier.
3. **Service level is a business decision with a price.** 98% instead of 95% is 25-40% more
   stock on that SKU; state the rupees and let the founder choose.
4. **Units for cover, rupees for cash.** Cover in days is a units question and working
   capital is a rupee question; mixing them in one column hides both.
5. **Dead stock needs a value, an action and a date.** "Slow-moving" is a word, not a
   decision, and a liquidation without a price floor damages the hero SKU permanently.
6. **The order date is the only date that matters in a seasonal business.** Work backwards
   from the selling window at P90 lead time, with a buffer for one slipped shipment.
7. **The physical count is the truth.** When the system says 400 and the count says 180,
   the plan uses 180 and the gap goes to whoever owns the count the same day.
8. **Every recommendation states the cash effect.** "₹8.2 lakh released this quarter" is a
   decision; "reduce inventory" is a slogan.
