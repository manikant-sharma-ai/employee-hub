# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Trademark attorney / agent> | Filing, examination replies, oppositions | Email with the search log attached | Ask for a fixed fee per class, not hourly, for a filing |
| <Patent agent> | Prior art reads, claim drafting, FTO charts | Email with the element table | Give them the chart, not the product story |
| <IP India portal login holder> | Status checks behind the login | They export, you read | The public search is enough for a knockout, not for status |
| <Designer / agency> | Copyright assignment for the logo and artwork | A signed assignment, one page | Without it the designer owns the logo |
| <Printer / packaging vendor> | What is already printed, and the cost of a rename | Phone | Ask before the brief, not after |
| <Marketplace brand registry owner> | Amazon / Flipkart / IndiaMART brand enrolment | The seller panel | Enrolment is the cheapest enforcement available |
| <Company secretary> | MCA name availability, board approvals | Before the company is named | A company name is not a trademark |
| <CA / finance> | Capitalising filing costs, TDS on professional fees | Before the fee is paid | Small, and it appears in the audit |
| <The founder> | The decision: file, rename, or proceed at risk | The one-page brief | Never the search log |
