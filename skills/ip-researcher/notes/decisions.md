# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `legal-drafter` | The search, the landscape, the route, the cost and the ownership record | Drafting a deed and reading a register are different skills; the drafter needs the facts this role produces |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `govt-scheme-finder` | The filing itself; the scheme money is theirs | A reimbursement is an incentive; a clearance is a legal position |
| 2026-11-15 | Verdict vocabulary | Three verdicts plus "cannot answer" - never "safe" or "available" | A register search is a dated observation, not a warranty, and the wording is the liability |
| 2026-11-15 | Search scope | Four query types on at least two registers, every time | The objection arrives from the phonetic or transliterated variant |
| 2026-11-15 | Patent work | Landscape and claim read only; no opinion on validity or infringement | Validity is an advocate's opinion, and a wrong one here is expensive |
| 2026-11-15 | Filing execution | This role never files or pays | Portal credentials and fees belong to a human or the registered agent |
| 2026-11-15 | Provisional patents | Only where a disclosure is imminent and the description can support the claim | A thin provisional buys a date it cannot defend |
| 2026-11-15 | Notes | Application numbers and asset names only; no applicant personal data, no unpublished product detail | The repo is public |
