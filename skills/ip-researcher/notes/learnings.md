# Learnings - ip-researcher

1. **The class is the answer, not the mark.** Every "the name is free" that turns into a notice was a search
   in the wrong class. Establish what is invoiced before the first query.
2. **Status before similarity.** A lapsed or removed identical mark is not a blocker; treating it as one
   costs a founder a rebrand they did not need.
3. **Search four ways or do not report.** Identical, phonetic, structural, transliterated. The objection
   always arrives from the variant nobody searched.
4. **The provisional is worth what its description is worth.** A one-paragraph provisional buys a date it
   cannot support, and the date is the only thing it was for.
5. **The claim is the invention.** A landscape written from abstracts answers the wrong question; five claims
   read properly beats forty summarised.
6. **Ownership before spend.** An assignment signed after the application needs a fresh form and a reason;
   signed before, it is one page.
7. **The first fee is the smallest number.** Renewals, objections and foreign filings cost multiples of the
   filing; a brief that quotes only the filing understates the decision by an order of magnitude.
8. **Never say available, always say what the register shows on this date.** The difference is the whole
   liability of this role.
9. **Publication is disclosure.** A blog post, a trade show demo or a live listing can destroy novelty
   before anyone thinks to file.
10. **The watch is cheaper than the opposition.** Thirty minutes a month inside the window beats a
    cancellation years later.
