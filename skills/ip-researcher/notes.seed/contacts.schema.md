# contacts.schema - shape only, no people recorded

Columns for `notes/contacts.md`. Empty until a real person is met on a real task.

| Column | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Who | Role and organisation, no personal phone numbers in the repo |
| For | The one job this person is the right call for |
| How | Channel, and the best time if it matters |
| Notes | What makes the next conversation faster - a preference, a constraint, a deadline pattern |
| First useful on | dd/mm/yyyy |

## What does not belong here

- Contact details for anyone who has not been involved in a real task.
- A name with no context. A phone book of strangers is not a phone book.
- Anything confidential: fee schedules under NDA, unpublished filings, personal data.
