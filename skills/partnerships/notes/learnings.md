# Learnings - partnerships

1. **A partner with no reason to sell on Monday will not sell on any day.** The fit test comes before the first call, not after the signature.
2. **Model the partner's net per deal, not their headline percentage.** A 30% margin that leaves them ₹950 after cost to sell is a line they will never push.
3. **Exclusivity is bought with a committed volume and a review date.** Never granted verbally, never renewed automatically.
4. **The signer and the person who carries the line are two different people.** Write to the second one; the first one only signs.
5. **Zero at day 30 is information, zero at day 60 with no pipeline is a decision.** The kill date is set at signature, not discovered at renewal.
6. **Channel revenue is recognised when the end customer pays,** not when the partner places the order.
