# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-04 | Default exclusivity for new partners? | None; exclusivity only against a committed volume and a 6-month review | Verbal exclusivity is the most-quoted promise in a channel dispute |
| 2026-11-04 | Credit on first orders? | Advance for the first three orders, then 30 days | A bad debt costs more than the margin on ten good deals |
| 2026-11-04 | Referral fee basis? | Percentage of first-year value, paid on collection | Paying on signature rewards introductions that never close |
| 2026-11-04 | One partner type or a portfolio? | One primary model per segment, named in writing | Two models in one segment produces channel conflict and undercutting |
