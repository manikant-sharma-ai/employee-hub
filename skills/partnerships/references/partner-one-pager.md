# The partner one-pager

One page. It is forwarded internally by someone who is not you, to a manager who
has never heard of the product and has thirty seconds. Write for that reader.

## The rule

Every sentence answers one of their questions: **what do I sell, to whom, at what
price, at what margin, with what help, and what do I do on Monday?**

Nothing about our vision, our founding year, our investors, or our awards.

## The six blocks, in order

| Block | Content | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The line | "Sell <product> to <buyer> at <price>. You earn <margin> per deal." | 2 sentences |
| 2. The buyer | Who they are, what they buy today, why they switch | 3 lines |
| 3. The money | A worked deal: their buy price, their sell price, their gross margin, their cost to sell, their net | A small table |
| 4. The support | Demo, training, collateral, second-line support, lead sharing - what we do, dated | 4 bullets |
| 5. The ask | What they must do: one named action, one target, one date | 3 lines |
| 6. The next step | A specific 30-minute call with a named person, two time options | 2 lines |

## The worked deal table

The single most persuasive element. Use their currency, their tax reality, their
discount habits.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Your buy price from us (ex-GST) | ₹8,000 |
| Your sell price to the customer | ₹12,500 |
| Your gross margin per unit | ₹4,500 (36%) |
| Your cost to sell (estimate: 4 hours at ₹700) | ₹2,800 |
| Your net per deal | ₹1,700 |
| Deals to cover a ₹50,000 monthly target | 30 |
| Our support | Demo, install training, second-line support |

If the net per deal is under about ₹1,500 or the volume needed is implausible, the
model does not work and the honest one-pager says so - do not hide it in a footnote.

## What to include about us - and what to leave out

| Include | Leave out |
|---|---|
| Price list, ex-GST, with the partner tier | "Revolutionary", "world-class", "cutting-edge" |
| What we do for their customer in one line | Our funding, our awards, our office |
| Two reference customers, named with permission, or anonymised with numbers | A logo wall of companies who once took a meeting |
| Support commitments with response times | "Dedicated partner success manager" with no name |
| Territory and exclusivity rules, plainly | A footnote that changes the meaning of the margin |

## Format

- One A4 page, or a two-slide PDF. If it does not fit, the offer is not clear yet.
- Their language if it is a regional channel - a Marathi one-pager to a Nagpur
  distributor is read; an English one is forwarded and forgotten.
- A single contact with a phone number that is answered.
- A price table that survives being forwarded without you in the room: no "call for
  pricing" unless the price genuinely varies by volume, and then give the bands.

## The internal-forward test

Before sending, ask: if this lands in a WhatsApp group of the partner's sales team
with no explanation, can a salesperson work out (a) who to call, (b) what to say,
(c) what they earn? If not, rewrite block 1 and block 3.

## Anti-patterns

- A deck of eighteen slides where a one-pager was asked for.
- Margin expressed as a percentage of our price without the rupee figure.
- A worked example that assumes a close rate the partner will never hit.
- Support promises with no owner - "we will help with marketing" is not a commitment.
- The same one-pager for a distributor, a CA firm and a system integrator.
- No next step, so the document ends instead of starting something.
