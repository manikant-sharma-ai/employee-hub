# Learnings - field-sales-manager

1. **Days available come before calls planned.** The commonest design failure is a beat that needs nine days in a
   six-day week, and it fails silently, as "market is slow".
2. **Frequency follows outlet class.** Uniform frequency over-serves the C outlets and starves the A outlets at
   the same cost.
3. **Travel time is a cost line.** At 40% travel, a rep costs roughly half a salary in time that sells nothing.
   Put the number in the plan.
4. **Primary is not secondary.** A rising primary with a flat secondary is stuffing, and it becomes a return and a
   stock-out three months later.
5. **A visit without a log did not happen.** Six fields on WhatsApp beats twenty-five fields in an app nobody
   opens.
6. **"Market is slow" is not a decomposition.** Sales = coverage × strike rate × drop size, and one of the three
   moved.
7. **The distributor's ROI decides whether your brand gets pushed.** Under 10% and they stop quietly, with no
   report saying so.
8. **A target with no potential beside it is an instruction to argue.** Territory potential, current share and a
   realistic gain, in that order.
9. **Pay on primary with a stock-cover gate, or the incentive buys a godown.** The gate is the cheapest control in
   field sales.
10. **A rep resigns after two months of signal in the coverage data.** Read it before the resignation, not after.
