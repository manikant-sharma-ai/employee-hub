# Learnings - field-sales-manager

1. **The frequency policy has to be written before the plan.** A beat built on a rep's preference is a
   tour, and the A outlet that gets visited twice a quarter is the one that starts buying elsewhere.
2. **Calls per day is a travel number, not a motivation number.** A plan with fifteen calls across four
   zones is abandoned by Wednesday, and the abandonment gets blamed on the rep.
3. **A target becomes actionable only as a daily call number.** Target divided by working days divided
   by average order value, divided by strike rate - and the arithmetic usually names the real lever.
4. **Primary is a shipment; secondary is a sale.** A rep can hit primary by loading a godown while the
   market dies, and the business discovers it as a return.
5. **The stock reconciliation identity settles most dealer disputes**: opening + primary - secondary =
   closing. Whichever of the four is wrong is the conversation.
6. **A visit log with no dated follow-up did not happen.** Six fields, one follow-up, one owner, one
   date - that is the whole standard.
7. **Never cross a zone twice in a day.** The second crossing is where the selling hours disappear.
8. **Coverage is measured against the policy, not against last month.** Last month may have been the
   month the territory quietly shrank.
