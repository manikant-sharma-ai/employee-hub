# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `sales-outreach` | The physical territory and the beat; digital outbound stays theirs | A rep's day and an inbox are different cost structures |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `lead-qualifier` | The field cycle after a lead is qualified | One owns the queue and the clock, the other the territory |
| 2026-11-15 | What this role owns, given `customer-success` | Coverage and offtake at outlet level, not account health or renewals | Key accounts have a relationship owner; the beat has a manager |
| 2026-11-15 | The log | Six fields on WhatsApp when there is no app | Adoption decides whether data exists at all |
| 2026-11-15 | Frequency by class | A weekly, B fortnightly, C monthly as the starting point | A design rule that can be argued with, unlike a uniform frequency |
| 2026-11-15 | Target basis | Territory potential, current share, realistic gain, seasonality, then the beat check | A target that the beat cannot deliver is arithmetic nobody executes |
| 2026-11-15 | Incentive gate | No variable above 45 days of distributor stock cover | The cheapest defence against stuffing |
| 2026-11-15 | Reporting primary and secondary | Both, always, with the gap explained | Primary alone flatters and hides the stock position |
| 2026-11-15 | Individual performance | Coaching in private; no default leaderboard in a group channel | Publishing numbers produces politics, not performance |
| 2026-11-15 | Notes | Outlet IDs, classes and pin codes only; no names, numbers or personal data | The repo is public |
