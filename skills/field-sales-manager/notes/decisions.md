# Decisions

Contested choices, dated, with the reason.

| Date | Decision | Why | What would reverse it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | The frequency policy is written per category before any plan | Without it, frequency becomes a rep's preference and the biggest outlets lose | A category whose economics genuinely differ - then add a category, not an exception |
| 2026-11-13 | The plan is filled zone-first, most-visited zone first | Travel is the largest controllable cost in a selling day | A territory where two zones are physically interleaved |
| 2026-11-13 | Six visit-log fields, no more | A log that takes four minutes happens; a log with twelve does not | If the business starts tracking shelf share systematically |
| 2026-11-13 | Objection codes are fixed and counted monthly | Free-text objections cannot be rolled up, so the territory never learns | — |
| 2026-11-13 | Coverage is reported against policy, not against last month | Last month may itself have been the failure | Never |
| 2026-11-13 | This role never marks a visit as done | A plan is not evidence of a visit; only the rep's log is | Never - the log is the control |
