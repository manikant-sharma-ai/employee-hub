# Contacts

| Who | For | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Area manager / founder> | Discounts above the ladder, credit beyond policy, distributor replacement | The escalation ladder, in writing | Only these three, nothing else |
| <Rep, per zone> | The beat, the visit log, the follow-up | WhatsApp, then the log | Ask for the objection code, not a paragraph |
| <Distributor, per territory> | Secondary sales, stock cover, claims | The monthly review sheet | Ask for the stock count before the claim |
| <Dealer credit controller> | Outstanding ageing, order holds | The ledger export | The field knows before the order, not after |
| <Collections agent> | Anything beyond terms | The ageing report | Field stops loading; collections does the chasing |
| <Inventory planner> | Stock cover at the depot, scheme stock | The monthly review | A scheme needs stock, or it becomes a backorder |
| <Market association contact> | Closed days, market holidays, local events | Phone | Cheapest way to stop wasting hops |
| <Transport / courier contact> | Delivery windows, damaged stock claims | Phone | Damage claims need photographs and batch numbers |
| <Pricing owner> | A price objection repeating across the territory | The objection code count | Forty `PRICE` codes is a pricing finding |
