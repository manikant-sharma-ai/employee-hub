# decisions.schema - shape only, no decisions recorded

Columns for `notes/decisions.md`. Recorded when the call was contested.

| Column | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Date | dd/mm/yyyy |
| Decision | The choice, in one line, stated as a rule |
| Why | The reason that will still be true in six months |
| What would reverse it | The specific change that reopens it |

## What does not belong here

- A decision nobody argued about.
- A decision with no reversal condition - that is a belief.
- A credit, pricing or recovery decision. Record who took it, not what it was.
