# India market sources

Where the data actually lives for an Indian business question, and what each source is good for. Everything here must be checked live in the session - a register read last year is not a check.

## Company and entity records

| Source | What it gives | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MCA21** (mca.gov.in) | Company/LLP incorporation, directors, charges, filings, status | The primary record. Free search; documents cost a small fee |
| **Udyam registration** portal | MSME status, type, activity | Self-declared, useful for category not for scale |
| **GST portal** - GSTIN validation | Whether a GSTIN is active, state, registration date | A suspended GSTIN means no compliant invoice |
| **GeM** (gem.gov.in) | Government tender history, past bidders, awarded prices | The best public price data for many categories |
| **CPPP / state e-procurement portals** | Tenders and corrigenda | State portals differ; check the state, not just the centre |
| **IP India** | Trademarks, patents, designs | Trademark status and who owns what |
| **e-courts, NCLT, IBBI** | Litigation, insolvency | Search by entity and by director name |
| **SEBI, RBI, IRDAI** | Orders, penalties, registered intermediaries | Check before trusting anyone "regulated" |
| **Tofler, Zauba Corp, IndiaFilings** (aggregators) | Convenient mirrors of MCA data | Faster to search; verify anything material against MCA21 itself |
| **BSE / NSE filings** | Listed company annual reports, results, related-party detail | Free and detailed. The most under-used source in India |

## Market and macro

| Source | Good for |
|---|---|
| **MoSPI** (mospi.gov.in) | National accounts, CPI/WPI, IIP, consumption expenditure surveys |
| **RBI** (rbi.org.in) | Monetary policy, credit growth, sectoral deployment of credit, household savings, FX |
| **CEA / Ministry of Power** | Generation, demand, tariffs |
| **TRAI** | Telecom subscribers, data usage, porting |
| **DGFT / DGCIS** | Export-import volumes by commodity and country (the best trade data available) |
| **DPIIT** | FDI inflows, industrial policy, startup recognition |
| **NITI Aayog** | Sector reports, indices, district-level data in places |
| **Census and NFHS** | Demographics, district-level population, household characteristics |
| **State industrial development corporations** | Land, incentives, state-specific industrial policy |
| **Udyog Aadhaar / MSME dashboards** | Counts of enterprises by district and activity |

## Trade and category

| Source | Good for |
|---|---|
| **Industry associations** - CII, FICCI, ASSOCHAM, and the sector body (e.g. apparel, chemicals, auto components) | Category reports, member directories, expo lists |
| **Expo exhibitor lists** | A real competitor universe, by category and city |
| **IndiaMART, TradeIndia** | Enquiry volumes, price bands, supplier count - a leading indicator of wholesale demand |
| **Amazon, Flipkart, quick-commerce** | Actual retail prices, review counts and volumes, review text as qualitative research |
| **Credit rating agencies** - CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings | Sector outlooks and company ratings, free summaries |
| **Annual reports of listed peers** | The most honest industry description available, including risks |
| **GST rate schedules and customs tariff** | What the category actually pays, which changes the unit economics |

## Regulation and compliance

| Topic | Where |
|---|---|
| Company law, LLP, filings | MCA21 |
| GST rates, returns, e-invoicing thresholds | CBIC, GST portal |
| Food | FSSAI - licence status is publicly searchable |
| Drugs and cosmetics | CDSCO, state drug controllers |
| Legal metrology (packaged goods, MRP) | Department of Consumer Affairs |
| Advertising claims | ASCI code, and the ASCI complaint log |
| Data protection | DPDP Act 2023 and the MeitY rules as notified |
| Labour, PF, ESI | EPFO, ESIC |
| Environment | CPCB / SPCB consents |
| Imports and exports | DGFT, ICEGATE |

## Working with Indian data, honestly

- **District-level data is sparse.** State-level is often the finest granularity available. Say so rather than implying precision.
- **The unorganised sector is most of many categories.** Any total that counts only registered players understates the market and overstates the addressable one. Both errors matter.
- **Currency and scale:** use ₹ and the Indian grouping (lakh, crore) with the international figure in brackets where the reader may be foreign. State the year and whether it is nominal or real.
- **Fiscal year is April-March.** A "2025" figure may mean FY25 (Apr 2024-Mar 2025). Get this wrong and every comparison is off by a year.
- **Aggregator sites lag the registry.** For anything material, go to the registry.
- **Anything about a private company's financials** that is not filed is a claim, not a fact - including what the company told you, unless they put it in writing.

## Anti-patterns

- Citing a consultancy's India market size without opening the report and reading its definition.
- Using a global growth rate for an Indian category.
- Treating a self-declared MSME registration as evidence of turnover.
- Trusting a "verified supplier" badge as verification.
- Using an aggregator's data as the primary record when the registry is one search away.
- Mixing FY and calendar year in one table.
- Quoting a state-level average for a district-level decision.
