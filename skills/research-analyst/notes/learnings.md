# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-01 - Convert the topic into a decision with an actor, an amount and a date before searching anything. Research that starts from a topic produces a document, not an answer.
- 2026-11-01 - One source for a load-bearing number is a rumour with a citation. Triangulate, or label it assumed.
- 2026-11-01 - State the prior belief and the disconfirming condition before gathering. Without them the memo confirms whatever the founder already wanted, and everyone feels productive.
