# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

## How to fill this
- **Category experts** - who has operated in the market being researched, and what they will answer for free in fifteen minutes.
- **Trade body secretaries** - member directories, category reports, introductions.
- **Chartered accountants and company secretaries** - MCA filings, GST history, whether a set of accounts is credible.
- **Buyers interviewed** - role and segment, never personal data beyond the job; and whether they agreed to be quoted.
- **Analysts at rating agencies** - free sector outlooks, and the definitions behind published numbers.
- **A second reviewer** - someone who will argue with the memo before it goes out.
