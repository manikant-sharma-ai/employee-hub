# Decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | What this role owns, given `bookkeeper`, `finance-ops` and `budget-planner` | Testing spend already made against policy, contract and approval - the exceptions register and the recovery | The others record, reconcile or plan; nobody was checking whether the money was spent correctly |
| 2026-11-12 | Materiality | A threshold agreed and stated before every sweep | An untargeted audit finds ₹40 of noise and buries the ₹4 lakh |
| 2026-11-12 | Evidence standard | Amount, document and clause, or the finding moves to "to confirm" | One published false finding ends the mandate |
| 2026-11-12 | Who acts on a finding | The approver, accounts or the vendor, with this role tracking to closure | An auditor who also reverses payments has no independent check |
| 2026-11-12 | Findings about people | Transactions and control failures only, never intent or a disciplinary conclusion | Not auditable from data, and it destroys the cooperation needed to get documents |
| 2026-11-12 | Tax questions | Flagged to the CA with the invoice, never decided | A wrong GST or TDS call is a disallowance and interest |
| 2026-11-12 | Vendor contact | Only with the founder's go-ahead | A rate query sent to the wrong contact can cost more than the finding |
| 2026-11-12 | Confidentiality | No vendor bank details, employee personal data or invoice copies in the repo or notes | The repo is public; amounts, patterns and categories only |
