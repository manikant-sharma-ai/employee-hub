# Contacts

People, vendors and services this employee relies on. Who they are, what they are good for, when they were last useful.

|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## How to fill this
- **The accountant or CA** - who reviews, decides tax positions and files; note their cut-off dates and what they ask for each month.
- **Who approves payments** - and the limit above which a second approval is needed.
- **Bank relationship contact** - for statements, cheque books and any change in signatory.
- **The GST practitioner** - for portal access, 2B downloads and filing reminders.
- **Recurring vendors** - the ones whose bills arrive monthly, and the person who confirms their bank details.
- **The payroll owner** - who holds salary data, kept separate from this file.

Keep names and roles only. No account numbers, no phone numbers, no personal email, no salary data in this file.
