# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-03 - Start from the bank statement, not from the pile of bills. The statement is the only list that is complete by construction.
- 2026-11-03 - An entry with no document goes on the exception list with a question, never into a suspense ledger. Suspense balances are to-do lists nobody empties.
- 2026-11-03 - Gateway payouts are booked gross, with the fee as an expense. Netting understates revenue and hides a real cost every month.
- 2026-11-03 - A payment that does not match an invoice by a small amount is almost always TDS. Check that before assuming a discount or an error.
