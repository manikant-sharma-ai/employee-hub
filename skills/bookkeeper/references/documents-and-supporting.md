# Documents and supporting

An entry without a document is a claim. The document is what turns it into a record - and it is the first thing an auditor, a bank or a GST officer asks for.

## What counts as support

| Document | Proves | Acceptable form |
|---|---|---|
| Tax invoice from a registered vendor | Purchase, GST, input credit | PDF or clear photo with GSTIN, invoice number, HSN/SAC |
| Bill of supply | Purchase from a composition or exempt vendor | Same, without tax |
| Receipt | A cash payment | Prefer a numbered receipt; a handwritten note with a signature is weak but better than nothing |
| Bank statement line | That money moved | The statement itself, not a screenshot of a message |
| Payment-gateway report | Gross sales, fee, net | The settlement report, downloadable |
| Contract or PO | Why the payment was due | Signed copy, or the email accepting the quote |
| Salary register | Payroll | Signed by the employee or the authorised person |
| Self-made voucher | Cash expense with no bill, e.g. a taxi | Voucher with date, purpose, amount, signature |

## Naming and filing

`YYYY-MM-DD_vendor_shortname_amount.pdf` - 2026-10-28_sharma-printers_18400.pdf

- One folder per month, one subfolder per document type.
- Scan at 200-300 DPI, greyscale is fine, PDF rather than JPG where possible.
- A WhatsApp photo of a bill is acceptable if the number, date, GSTIN and total are legible - check before filing, not after.
- Never overwrite a document. A corrected version gets `-v2` and the original stays.

## The missing-document ladder

1. **Ask the vendor for a copy.** Cheapest and correct. Most vendors resend in a day.
2. **Ask the person who paid.** They may have the email or the message.
3. **Reconstruct from the bank line** - date, amount, payee name. This proves the payment, not the purpose.
4. **Raise a self-made voucher** with the purpose as stated by the person who authorised it, marked "supporting document not available".
5. **Escalate** if the amount is material and nobody can say what it was for. That is a control failure, not a filing problem.

Never skip to step 4 when step 1 is available.

## Documents that are not what they look like

- **A proforma invoice** is not a purchase. It is a quotation. Do not enter it as a liability.
- **A delivery challan** is not a bill. It proves goods moved.
- **A handwritten bill without a GSTIN** is not a tax invoice; input credit cannot be claimed on it.
- **A bill in a different entity's name** cannot be booked here, whatever the payment says.
- **A screenshot of a UPI confirmation** is not a bank statement. Get the statement.

## The document index

For each period, deliver an index with the daybook:

| Date | Vendor | Amount | Document type | Reference | File | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-28 | Sharma Printers | 18,400 | Tax invoice | SP/4471 | 2026-10-28_sharma-printers_18400.pdf | Filed |

Status is one of: filed, requested, reconstructed, missing. A period with twenty "missing" is not closed, whatever the totals say.

## Retention

- GST records: retain for the statutory period, and in practice keep everything for at least eight years - the exact requirement is a question for the CA, not a guess.
- Bank statements: keep the full set, every month, including the months with no activity.
- Payroll records: keep separately and with restricted access.
- Never store scanned documents in a public folder or a personal Drive. Access follows the role, not the convenience.

## Anti-patterns

- **Filing a photograph nobody can read.** It is not support; it is a placeholder.
- **Entering a bill and attaching the wrong image**, which is worse than attaching none because it looks complete.
- **A folder called "bills misc"** at the end of the year.
- **Deleting a document because the entry was reversed.** The reversal needs its support more, not less.
- **Storing personal ID documents (Aadhaar, PAN) in the general bills folder.** Separate, restricted, and only where the law requires it.
