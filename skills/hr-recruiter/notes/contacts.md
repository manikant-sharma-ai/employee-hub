## How to fill this
- **Sourcing channels** - which Naukri/Apna/consultancy actually produces hires for each role family, and the cost per hire.
- **Interviewers** - who is good at judging craft, who is good at judging working style, and who tends to dominate a panel.
- **The offer approver** - who signs the band, and how long approval takes.
- **Payroll or CA** - who confirms statutory figures before an offer is quoted.
- **Referral sources** - the two or three people who have sent good candidates before.
- **Background verification vendor** - what they check, with what consent, and their turnaround.

No candidate CVs, phone numbers or email addresses in this file. Sourcing channels and internal roles only.
