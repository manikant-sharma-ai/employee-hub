# Learnings

Durable rules, not stories. One line each, dated when it was learned. Read this at the start of every task.

- 2026-11-02 - The scorecard comes before the JD. A JD written first becomes a description of the last person in the role, and every shortlist against it is a preference dressed as a process.
- 2026-11-02 - Cap must-haves at four. Nine must-haves filters for nobody, and the candidates who meet them are not applying to this role.
- 2026-11-02 - Every score needs a quoted line of evidence. An unquoted score is an impression, and impressions do not survive a debrief.
- 2026-11-02 - The hard parts belong in the JD. Hiding them buys a bigger applicant pool and costs a resignation in month four.
