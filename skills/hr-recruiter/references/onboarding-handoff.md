# Onboarding handoff

The hire is not done when the offer is signed. The first 30 days decide whether
the person stays, and the recruiter is the only one who knows what was promised.

## The handoff pack, at offer acceptance

| Item | Why | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Scorecard and interview notes | The manager inherits the evidence, not a rumour | Recruiter |
| What was promised in the interview | Title, scope, team, flexibility, growth | Recruiter |
| Notice period and start date confirmed in writing | The most common day-one surprise | Recruiter |
| Compensation structure and the variable formula | So payroll and the person agree | Finance |
| Documents checklist sent | ID, education, previous employment, bank details | HR ops |
| Buddy or mentor named | The single highest-impact onboarding decision | Manager |

## The 30-60-90, written before day one

- **First 30 days**: learn the product, the customer and the team; ship one small thing. Success is "understands and is trusted with a task".
- **60 days**: own a recurring responsibility end to end. Success is "delivers without supervision on the familiar".
- **90 days**: improve something. Success is "has changed one thing for the better and can say why".

Each milestone needs one measurable line. "Settled in" is not a milestone.

## The first week, hour by hour

Day 1: laptop, access, a person to sit with, and one real task by lunch.
Day 2-3: product and customer immersion - listen to three support calls or read ten tickets.
Day 4: meet the three people they will work with most, each with a stated purpose.
Day 5: a 30-minute check-in with the manager: what is clear, what is confusing, what is missing.

## What kills a hire in month one

- No laptop or no access on day one (the most common, and the most corrosive).
- A manager on leave for the first week.
- Work that has no owner and no review.
- A promise from the interview that is quietly withdrawn.
- No feedback until the probation review.

## The recruiter's post-hire duty

Check in at day 7 and day 30, with two questions: "what did we promise that has not
happened?" and "what would you tell the next person joining?" Write the answers into
`notes/learnings.md` - they are the corrections to the JD and to the interview kit.

## Anti-patterns

- Onboarding that starts with a 40-page policy document.
- No buddy, so every question goes to the manager.
- The 30-60-90 written in month two.
- Recruiter disappears after the offer letter.
- Probation review that happens at month five.
