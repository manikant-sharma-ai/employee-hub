# India hiring norms

Practical realities that decide whether an offer lands. Confirm current statutory figures with the CA or the payroll provider - the shape is stable, the numbers move.

## Sourcing channels by role type

| Role | First channel | Second |
|---|---|---|
| Field sales, ops supervisor, technician | Naukri, Apna, local WhatsApp groups, referrals | Consultancy in the city |
| Accounts, back office | Naukri, referrals | CA firm networks, Internshala for juniors |
| Software engineer | LinkedIn, Wellfound, referrals | Campus, open-source communities |
| Designer | Behance, Instagram, LinkedIn | Referrals |
| Intern, fresher | Internshala, college placement cells | Campus drives |
| Senior or leadership | Referrals, retained search | LinkedIn, warm intros |
| Blue-collar, warehouse, delivery | Local networks, Apna, feet-on-street posters | Existing staff referrals, with a referral bonus |

Referrals produce the best retention in most small businesses. A referral bonus paid after 90 days is cheaper than a bad hire.

## Notice periods

| Level | Typical | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| Junior | 30 days | Often negotiable |
| Mid | 30-60 days | Rarely waivable below 30 |
| Senior, tech | 60-90 days | Buy-outs happen; confirm who pays |
| Sales with a client book | 30-60 days, with a non-compete clause that is often unenforceable | Do not rely on the clause |

Plan the start date from the notice period, not from the offer date. The commonest failed hire is the one who accepted and then waited 90 days while the team hired someone else.

## Offer letter contents

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Designation and reporting line | Match the JD exactly |
| Location and working hours or days | State the working week honestly |
| Fixed pay, variable, allowances | Component-wise, matching the CTC sheet |
| Probation period and terms | Duration and what changes on confirmation |
| Notice period during probation and after | Both |
| Leave entitlement | Casual, sick, earned, and the policy on carry-forward |
| Statutory benefits | PF, ESI, gratuity where applicable |
| Confidentiality and IP assignment | Needs the legal drafter's version |
| Termination terms | Both sides, with notice |
| Acceptance signature and date | And a joining date |

Never send an offer on WhatsApp as the only record. A written letter, a signed acceptance, and a documented joining date.

## Statutory touchpoints, in shape

| Item | When it applies |
|---|---|
| PF | Establishments above the employee threshold; employer and employee contributions |
| ESI | Below a wage ceiling; check the current ceiling |
| Gratuity | After five years of continuous service |
| Professional tax | State-specific, with a monthly or annual cap |
| TDS on salary | Monthly deduction, quarterly return, Form 16 annually |
| Shops and Establishments registration | State-specific; governs hours, leave and records |
| Apprenticeship / internship stipends | Separate from employment; do not confuse the two in the letter |
| POSH policy and committee | Required above a size threshold; the policy applies from day one |

## Background verification, done proportionately

| Check | When | Consent |
|---|---|---|
| Previous employment dates and designation | Most roles | Written, in the application |
| Education | Where the role requires it | Written |
| Criminal record | Rarely, and only where the role warrants it | Written, and be careful with fairness |
| Reference from a named supervisor | Best single signal | Ask the candidate to arrange it |
| Address | Where the role is field or cash-handling | Written |

Never check without written consent, never contact a current employer without permission, and never store the results in a file that leaves the business.

## What never belongs in a JD, an interview or an offer

- Marital status, pregnancy or family plans, caste, religion, community, region, age, disability, sexual orientation.
- A photograph as a screening criterion.
- A "family-like atmosphere" line as a substitute for stated working hours.
- A bond or a training-recovery clause without a legal drafter's review.

## Anti-patterns

- **Quoting a CTC that includes a variable that has never paid out.** It is the fastest route to a month-three resignation.
- **A 90-day notice period with no joining plan.** The candidate disengages and takes the counter-offer.
- **Hiring on a phone call with no written offer.** Both sides end up with different memories.
- **Ignoring the state's professional tax and Shops and Establishments rules.** They surface at the first inspection.
- **Running a reference check on a candidate's current manager.** It can end their job before the offer.
- **A JD in English only for a shop-floor role in a non-English-speaking region.** It halves the applicant pool for no reason.
