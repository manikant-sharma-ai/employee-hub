# Community to revenue

Engagement is not revenue, and the gap between them is a handoff nobody makes. This file is about making it, without turning the comment section into a shop.

## The rule

**Answer in public, transact in private, hand over with context.** A public reply that sells reads as desperate. A DM that answers reads as service and converts at several times the rate.

## What a buying signal actually looks like

| Signal | Strength | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| "Price?" or "How much" | High | Ready to compare, not necessarily to buy |
| "DM me" / "details please" | High | Wants a private conversation |
| "Do you deliver to <city>" | Medium-high | Logistics is the last objection |
| "Do you have bulk / wholesale rates" | High, and worth more | B2B enquiry, route to `sales-outreach` |
| "Is COD available" | Medium | Trust objection, not a price objection |
| "Following, looks great" | Low | Audience, not a lead |
| A tag - "look at this @friend" | Low, but wide | Someone is recommending you; do not sell into it |

## The handoff, in four fields

Every buying signal leaves the community queue as a line, not a feeling:

```
Handle / name:
What they asked (verbatim):
What I replied:
Next step + owner:
```

Without the verbatim question, the salesperson opens with a generic message and the signal dies. With it, the first line can be "you asked whether we ship the 3-seater to Kochi - yes, 6 days, ₹2,400 freight."

## Turning a batch into a channel

- **Answer the top three questions publicly and pin them.** The fourth time someone asks the price, the answer should already be at the top of the thread.
- **Create one saved reply per real question**, not one per comment. Six saved replies cover most batches; forty cover none.
- **Track the question mix weekly.** If 40% of comments are "where is my order", the problem is the delivery notification, not the community.
- **Count enquiries, not likes.** A weekly line - comments triaged, buying signals, handoffs accepted by sales - is the only number that shows this role paying for itself.

## What not to do

- Do not put a price in every public reply; it trains the audience to haggle in public.
- Do not ask people to "DM for price" when the price is public on the site. It reads as a trap and loses the buyer who will not DM.
- Do not hand a signal to sales without saying what was already promised in public. The customer will quote you.
- Do not run a giveaway to build a list and then treat the entrants as leads. They are not; they came for the prize.
- Do not follow, like and comment on a prospect's personal posts to warm them up. It is noticed, and it is disliked.

## The monthly read

| Metric | Why it matters | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|
| Median first-response time | The only number the audience experiences | Rising while volume is flat |
| Buying signals per 100 comments | Is the audience actually a market | Under 2 |
| Handoffs accepted by sales | Is the handoff real | Under half accepted |
| Enquiries closed | Does the role pay for itself | Zero, two months running |
| Removals per 1,000 comments | Is moderation proportionate | Spiking without a volume spike |

Report the ones you cannot measure as unmeasured. A community manager who invents a conversion rate is worse than one who says the CRM was not connected.
