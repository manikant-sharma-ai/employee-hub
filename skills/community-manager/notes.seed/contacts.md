## How to fill this

Roles only - names and handles are optional, and personal phone numbers never go in this file.

- **The human on call** - who authorises a refund, a replacement or a public statement, and their hours.
- **The founder or spokesperson** - who speaks for the business when a comment becomes a story.
- **`support-agent` owner** - where a public complaint becomes a ticket, and the ticket prefix to quote back.
- **`pr-writer`** - the route for journalist and media enquiries.
- **Legal or CA** - who reads a notice before anything is written in reply.
- **The platform reps** - account manager for the main channel, for takedowns and impersonation reports.
- **Grievance officer** - the named contact for content complaints, if the business has published one.
