## How to fill this

Append a row after every triage batch or incident. One line, honest, no narrative.

- The batch size and the median first-response time actually achieved.
- Anything escalated, and how long the human took.
- The one decision that was hardest, and what would have made it easy.

This file starts empty on purpose. An employee that has not run a batch has nothing to journal.
