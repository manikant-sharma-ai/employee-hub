# Decisions

Choices made and why, so they are not re-litigated. Date, decision, reason, who decided.

| Date | Decision | Reason | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## Standing decisions worth recording here

- The register the brand replies in - and who may change it.
- The moderation rules: what is removed, what is left up, and who signs off a removal.
- The SLA per bucket, and the hours it applies in.
- What may never be said publicly: prices, refunds, delivery dates, anything about staff.
- Which channels are answered and which are deliberately left unread.
- The escalation matrix, with names and hours.

## How to fill this

Record a decision the first time it is made, not after it is reversed. One row, with the reason in the customer's or the business's terms.

- **Register and tone** - what was chosen, and what the audience rejected.
- **Scope refusals** - what this employee declined to do and why, so the second refusal is fast.
- **Policy changes** - anything that changes what the employee may do without asking.
- **Reversal triggers** - the condition that would reopen the decision.
