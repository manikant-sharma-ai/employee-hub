# Learnings - returns-manager

1. **The reason code is the product feedback, not the paperwork.** A shift from "changed mind" to "not
   as described" is a listing defect, and it is cheaper to fix the photo than to process a hundred more
   returns.
2. **Size returns concentrate in two sizes.** When the returned sizes are not the sold sizes, the chart
   is wrong; measuring the actual garment fixes more returns than any policy change.
3. **The markdown on resale is the largest cost, not the reverse freight.** Costing only the pickup
   understates a return by half and makes the wrong SKUs look profitable.
4. **Grade within 48 hours or the value is gone.** A returned unit in a bin is a 100% write-off with
   extra steps, and the refund has already left.
5. **The policy at the time of sale governs, and the channel's rule overrides it.** Retroactive
   tightening is the fastest route to a marketplace metric hit and a consumer complaint.
6. **A deduction nobody was told about is not a deduction, it is a dispute.** Disclosed at purchase or
   not at all.
7. **Refund to the original payer, always.** A refund to a third party's account is a fraud vector and
   an audit gap, however sympathetic the request.
8. **Flag patterns, never accuse people.** The empty-box and serial-return signals go to a human with
   evidence; an accusation that is wrong cannot be undone.
9. **Claim windows close silently.** A weekly look at windows expiring in the next seven days is worth
   more than any recovery initiative.
