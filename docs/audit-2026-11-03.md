## What was missing, and what was done

1. **The daily cadence had no mechanism.** `registry.json` (`"cadence": "5 employees daily at 06:00 IST"`), `README.md` and `docs/daily-drop.md` all promised a 06:00 drop. Nothing in the repo could fire at 06:00 - no workflow, no script. Fixed: `.github/workflows/daily-drop.yml` at `30 0 * * *` UTC plus `scripts/daily_drop.py`, which reads `BACKLOG.md` and opens an issue with the day's five roles and the procedure as a checklist. It does not write skills by machine; that is a deliberate limit.
2. **No validation on push.** The validator existed and was manual. Fixed: `.github/workflows/validate.yml` runs `scripts/validate_repo.py` and the new `scripts/validate_manifests.py` on every push and PR.
3. **Manifest drift, unmonitored.** `plugin.json` and `marketplace.json` were at 1.3.0 while `registry.json` was at 1.4.0, and the publisher name differed between the two ("Pawan Sharma" vs "Manikant Sharma"). Fixed: all three at 1.5.0, one publisher, and a validator that fails the build on future drift.
4. **No hire desk.** Twenty employees and no dispatcher. Fixed: `skills/employee-hub-router` with `references/role-map.md` (twelve confused pairs, five recurring sequences).
5. **Design work stopped at the poster.** `graphic-designer` says "not for interactive screens (use ui-designer)" and `ui-designer` did not exist - a dead reference. Fixed: `skills/ui-designer`.
6. **Nobody owned the calendar or the replies.** Fixed: `skills/social-media-manager`.
7. **No email role.** `sales-outreach` covers outbound to strangers; nothing covered a consented list. Fixed: `skills/email-marketer`.
8. **No testing role.** Fixed: `skills/qa-tester`.
9. **No localisation role**, in an India-first hub. Fixed: `skills/translator-localiser`.
10. **Scheduling was a single README row.** Fixed: `docs/scheduling.md` - four ways to fire a job, exact lines, the weekly and monthly review layer, and the rule that a schedule does not grant connector access.
11. **No contribution contract.** Fixed: `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
12. **Catalog gaps.** No Adobe, Premiere, DaVinci, Inkscape, Mailchimp, SendGrid, Zoho Campaigns, WhatsApp campaign tools, Looker Studio, Metabase, Sigma, BrowserStack, Playwright or Bhashini. Fixed in `core/connector-catalog.md`, plus a fifth "how to choose" rule about the licence rather than the tool.
13. **README drift.** "150 roles queued" (there were 128). Fixed, along with the router, the scheduling page and both validators.

## The one structural risk

Every skill claims an employee "checks its connectors first". Nothing in the repo
can verify that a given host actually runs the preflight. The standard is enforced
by instruction and by the validator's link check, not by the runtime. A skill that
skips the preflight at runtime will still pass `validate_repo.py`. That is the
ceiling of a prompt-only design, and it is worth knowing rather than fixing.
