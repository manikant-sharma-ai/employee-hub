## The five-a-day mix

Each morning's drop is balanced rather than alphabetical:

- **2 revenue-facing** - something that brings money in or stops it leaking (sales, marketing, listings, pricing)
- **2 operations** - something that saves hours every week (finance, ops, support, data)
- **1 founder-leverage** - something the founder would otherwise do personally and badly (strategy, research, decisions)

That mix is what makes the hub useful to a businessman on day 10, not just impressive on day 1.

## Rules for picking

1. Prefer a role whose absence costs a founder time every week.
2. Never ship two employees whose trigger phrases collide - if they would fight over the same request, the second routes through the first.
3. If a role needs a connector the user has not connected, ship it anyway with the connector listed under *Tools it assumes*, and say so plainly.
4. Every employee must be usable on its first day with at least one connector the user already has.
