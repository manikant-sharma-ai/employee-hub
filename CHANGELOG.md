- **`community-manager`** (`skills/community-manager`, v1.0.0, L2, revenue-facing) - the first employee whose output is published rather than delivered, which is why it is the first one built around a triage sort instead of a workflow. The whole job is the sort: six buckets, and urgency scored separately from risk, because a polite refund question is urgent and safe while a public safety claim is neither. The rule that shapes the rest is that criticism stays up and spam comes down - deleting a negative comment converts a complaint into a scandal, and the screenshot always exists. `reply-craft-in-indian-voices.md` carries the register ladder (आप / aap / bhai / English-only) and the rule that script and romanisation never mix inside one reply, with per-language notes that say plainly that Marathi is not Hindi and that purist Tamil for "download" reads as a textbook. `moderation-and-escalation.md` writes the moderation policy before the incident, names the IT Rules, 2021 grievance-officer obligation without quoting a section number, and gives the five-field escalation that actually reaches a person. `scripts/triage_inbox.py` is the rule table as code - 40-odd keyword rules in priority order, a risk score, an SLA per bucket, and a `--self-test` that asserts the burn complaint outranks the price question. The boundary against `support-agent` is written into both descriptions: the ticketed inbox has the order history and the refund authority, the comment section has the audience.

- **`email-marketer`** (`skills/email-marketer`, v1.0.0, L3, revenue-facing) - the revenue role that pays for itself fastest, and the one where the fastest way to lose money is to send more. Built on the rule that consent is a record and not a memory, with the DPDP framing in `list-hygiene-and-consent.md` and a refusal, stated in the guardrails and tested in an eval, to write copy for a purchased list. Two decisions carry most of the value: the cadence and the exit are set before any copy is written, because a sequence without an exit is a drip that becomes spam; and the subject and preview are written as one sentence split across two fields, because written separately they repeat each other and waste the most valuable line in the email. `deliverability-and-measurement.md` states the uncomfortable part - opens are inflated 15-40% by privacy features and scanners, so clicks and replies are the real signal, and a 12% open rate on a 400-person B2B list in India is a good send rather than a broken one. `scripts/sequence_linter.py` catches what is mechanical before a human reads anything: missing unsubscribe, missing sender identity, gaps under 24 hours, subject over 60 characters, unknown merge tags, duplicate subjects, spam-trigger phrases - twelve checks, a broken fixture and a clean one in the self-test.

- **`qa-tester`** (`skills/qa-tester`, v1.0.0, L2, operations) - the operations role that closes a loop the hub had been promising since the product manager shipped: `product-manager` already routed "test plans and bug reports" here, and the reference had been dead for a day. Risk-based by construction - likelihood times cost, money and data loss first, everything else in a queue - because a release tested for two hours on the right four paths is safer than one tested for two days on everything equally. `edge-case-matrix.md` is written for Indian inputs specifically: ₹ amounts with paise, ten-digit mobiles with +91, names in Devanagari and Tamil, addresses with no house number, UPI that times out after the debit. `scripts/edge_matrix.py` generates a pairwise covering array instead of a hand-written list, and reports the coverage as a number - nine cases instead of seventy-two on the reference spec, with every pair covered and asserted in the self-test. The boundary is verification versus implementation: it will reproduce, report and re-test, and it will not write the patch.

- **`translator-localiser`** (`skills/translator-localiser`, v1.0.0, L1, operations) - the operations role that makes the hub usable in the language the customer actually reads. Built on the split between translating the words and reproducing the effect: a legal clause is literal, a headline is transcreated, and the decision is per string rather than per document. `indian-language-registers.md` is a working note rather than a grammar - the register ladder, script versus romanised, and per-language notes that name the two failures that recur: Sanskritised Hindi in a consumer app, and Hindi shipped to Maharashtra because everyone assumed it would do. `glossary-and-terminology.md` makes the glossary the cheapest quality intervention available and gives the transliteration rule - if the audience already says the English word, transliterate it. `scripts/glossary_check.py` is the mechanical pass that has to run before a reviewer sees anything: placeholder parity and order, glossary drift, script mixing across eight Indic ranges, western digit grouping, expansion beyond the language allowance - and the honesty line is in the output contract, because not every language this can draft in has a native reviewer behind it.

- **`pricing-analyst`** (`skills/pricing-analyst`, v1.0.0, L1, founder-leverage) - the founder-leverage role, and the one the hub was missing rather than merely queuing: `finance-ops` and `bookkeeper` both record money and neither decides what the number should be. It starts at the cost to serve, bottom-up, because every pricing argument that starts at a competitor's price ends in a number nobody can defend. The floor is computed before any recommendation, GST is treated as a pass-through rather than revenue, and returns and RTO are modelled per unit rather than as a percentage of revenue - the single most under-modelled cost in Indian D2C. `discount-and-negotiation-policy.md` carries the table that ends most discount arguments: on a 30% contribution margin, a 10% discount needs 50% more volume to stand still. `scripts/margin_model.py` does the arithmetic and prints its own inputs - contribution, break-even units, the price floor, the volume uplift a discount requires, and what a 5% price rise is worth - with the reference numbers asserted in the self-test.

Each carries four craft references, one asset template, a deterministic script with a passing `--self-test`, five eval cases covering happy path, thin brief, missing connector, wrong tool and handoff, and a `notes.seed/` that is column headers and a "how to fill this" section with nothing invented in it.

- **`seo-specialist`** (`skills/seo-specialist`, v1.0.0) - the revenue role the queue had been holding for three drops, and shaped so intent is read off the results page rather than off the keyword's wording, because "custom furniture" looks informational and converts as commercial. The workflow starts at Search Console rather than at a keyword tool: queries at position 5-20 with high impressions are the cheapest wins in any account, and a keyword export is where most SEO work goes wrong first. Winnability is written as arithmetic - the referring domains of the weakest page in the top five against this site's - so "we cannot win this query" becomes a finding rather than an opinion, and the role says plainly when the market searches on IndiaMART or in a WhatsApp group and organic is a rounding error. `content-briefs-that-rank.md` exists because the brief is where SEO either helps a writer or ruins a page, and it carries the rule that word count is read from the SERP and never from a rule of thumb. `india-search-behaviour.md` covers Hinglish register, script variants, price-first and "near me" intent, and the map pack that outranks anything on-page for local queries. The description hands the article to `content-writer` and crawl, index and speed to `seo-technical`.

- **`presentation-designer`** (`skills/presentation-designer`, v1.0.0) - the second revenue role, and deliberately split from `graphic-designer` on the only axis that matters: this one owns the argument, that one owns the visual system, template and single-slide design. Built on the title-run test - read only the slide titles and the deck either argues or it does not - because it is the one check that catches a document wearing slides. The workflow fixes the time budget before the slide count, since a 30-slide deck in a 15-minute slot means the ask is never reached, and every deck gets a decision sentence written before the first slide. `data-slides-and-charts.md` makes the chart a function of the comparison rather than a decoration, carries the bridge that must reconcile to the rupee, and states the Indian convention for crore and lakh so a board pack is not a currency salad. `speaker-notes-and-delivery.md` writes notes as six fields rather than a script, because a presenter reading a script flattens, and `reviewing-a-deck.md` is the twenty-minute gate that catches the number that contradicts the other number.

- **`bookkeeper`** (`skills/bookkeeper`, v1.0.0) - the first operations role, and the one `finance-ops` had already promised. It starts from the bank statement rather than the pile of bills, because the statement is the only list that is complete by construction, and it bans the suspense ledger outright: an unmatched item becomes an exception with a question and an owner, which is the same information with a person attached. Two rules carry most of the value - book gateway payouts gross with the fee as an expense, and never plug a difference to make something balance - because both errors survive every close that follows them. `voucher-entry.md` gives the debit-and-credit shape for the nine transactions a small business actually has, with TDS on the taxable value and IGST versus CGST+SGST as a filing error rather than a rounding error. `common-entry-errors.md` is the fifteen-minute self-check that stops the accountant sending the whole period back, and the guardrail list ends where the role ends: recording a payment is not making one, and the close belongs to `finance-ops`.

- **`legal-drafter`** (`skills/legal-drafter`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, and the one in this repo with the most careful boundary, because the failure mode is a confident draft that reads as advice. Every draft ships with a review header, a plain-language summary sheet and a five-item risk list, so the lawyer's review is a twenty-minute conversation rather than a re-draft. It is built around the acceptance test and the "not included" list, which prevent more disputes than any liability clause, and it refuses to quote a stamp duty rate, a statutory threshold or a section number as fact - the mechanism is described and the number is routed to the CA. `india-contract-basics.md` covers the mechanics that decide whether a good draft is usable: stamping, e-signature audit trails, arbitration clauses with no seat, the TDS clause that stops a vendor claiming short payment, and the honest note that post-employment non-competes are generally unenforceable here. It says when a clear email confirmation beats a badly signed five-page contract.

- **`product-manager`** (`skills/product-manager`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage role, and the one the hub was missing rather than merely queuing: deciding what not to build is the work a founder does at 11pm and badly. Shaped so the cut is the product - every plan arrives 40% too big, and the deferred column carries a trigger, because "later" without a trigger never happens and the same request returns every quarter. Prioritisation is a written rule - value times confidence over effort, with reversibility as the stated tie-breaker - so the tenth decision is consistent with the first and the argument moves from status to inputs. `discovery-and-evidence.md` ranks evidence and puts a founder's hunch last, with six questions about the past that produce a problem statement in the customer's own words. `story-slicing.md` bans horizontal slicing, and `launch-and-after.md` treats a launch as thirteen checklist lines with the two read dates and the decision rule set before the numbers arrive.

**Standard wiring:** all five link `core/connector-preflight.md` before the first substantive step and `core/memory-and-notes.md` plus `core/performance-tracking.md` before the output contract. Each carries six craft references, one asset template, seeded `notes/` (learnings, contacts, decisions, journal for 2026-11) and its own `scripts/validate_skill.py`. Every description carries literal trigger phrases and a 'Not for' boundary that names the sibling to use instead. `scripts/validate_repo.py` passes with zero errors and zero warnings.

The cadence rule that every touch must add information exists because the commonest failure in outbound is four reminders dressed as follow-ups. The ask ladder puts the meeting request at touch three, since asking in touch one is the single biggest cause of silence. `crm-hygiene.md` defines each stage by its exit artefact, because a pipeline where a reply counts as a lead produces a forecast nobody believes. `india-outbound-norms.md` is what makes the role usable here: WhatsApp template approval and the 24-hour window, TRAI DND on calls, the financial-year-end and Diwali windows, and the gatekeeper reality in Indian B2B. The description hands blog and social content to `content-writer` and the timed nudge to `follow-up-sequencer`, so the three never fight over "write an email".

- **`video-editor`** (`skills/video-editor`, v1.0.0) - built around the one honest constraint in this hub: it can read a transcript, sample frames and run deterministic operations, but it cannot watch motion in real time. So the role is designed to produce a cut list a person or a pipeline executes, and the guardrail says so in the first person. The workflow reads the transcript before the footage, because the hook is almost never the sentence the speaker opened with. `short-form-structure.md` gives the four beats and a duration table by platform and intent, so a 42-minute webinar becomes eight clips rather than one long one. `audio-and-music.md` carries loudness targets and the four common recording failures, because viewers forgive a soft picture and not bad sound. Captions are treated as the primary text track - reading speed, line length, safe zones, and a Hinglish policy that says match the register rather than translate it. The thumbnail is explicitly handed to `graphic-designer` and the audio-only edit to `voice-and-audio`.

- **`finance-ops`** (`skills/finance-ops`, v1.0.0) - the most consequential skill in the repo, and therefore the one with the narrowest mandate. It prepares; the CA decides and files; nothing is paid, filed or transferred. Two rules carry most of the value: never plug a difference (a plugged number survives into every future reconciliation), and book the gross sale with the gateway fee separately rather than the net payout, which otherwise understates revenue and hides the cost every month. The reconciliation bridge is written as a shape with an itemised difference, not a total that either matches or does not. The close is a 17-line checklist with a cut-off and a days-to-close metric, because a close that drifts a day later each month is a process failure that presents as a people problem. The exception list is page three of the pack, not an appendix - it is the only page an owner acts on. `controls-and-fraud-checks.md` puts the vendor bank-detail change at the top, since that email is the most common successful fraud against an Indian SMB, and requires a phone call to a number from the original contract. GST is handled as readiness - 2B matching, TDS workings, evidence to keep - with thresholds and dates labelled as things to confirm, not facts.

- **`hr-recruiter`** (`skills/hr-recruiter`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, structured so the scorecard exists before the JD and the JD is written from it. That order is the whole skill: a JD written first becomes a description of the last person in the role. Must-haves are capped at four, because nine filters for nobody and the people who meet all nine are not applying. Screening is scored 2/1/0/-1 against each criterion with one quoted line of evidence, and the passes run cheapest-filter-first so 200 applications is a two-hour job. The work-sample table is the strongest signal in the pack, with the rule that anything over two hours gets paid. `candidate-comms-and-offers.md` exists because ghosting is the most damaging and cheapest-to-fix behaviour in hiring, and it shows CTC as a structure with a take-home line - the omission that produces a resignation in month two. `india-hiring-norms.md` covers notice-period reality, PF/ESI and professional tax in shape rather than as figures, POSH from day one, and the checks that need written consent. The hard-parts section of the JD is treated as the section that saves six months, and the guardrails forbid screening on any protected attribute, including a photograph.

- **`brand-strategist`** (`skills/brand-strategist`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage role, and the one the repo had already promised: `content-writer` ships with "not for deciding what the company stands for (use brand-strategist)" in its description, so leaving it unbuilt would have meant a dead reference inside a shipped skill. It is built on the rule that a position must name a real alternative and an exclusion - if the sentence still fits when a competitor's name is substituted, it is a description, not a position. The worked example runs the same role through a weak and a strong column so the difference is visible rather than asserted. `messaging-hierarchy.md` goes from six words to a proof point per pillar, with a rule that a pillar without proof changes or the proof gets built - never softened into vagueness, which is how brands end up saying nothing. `naming.md` includes meaning checks across the market's actual languages rather than only Hindi, and states plainly that a search is not a clearance opinion. The tone guide is rules with before-and-after lines, because an archetype cannot be applied to a sentence. Testing is five real buyers and a comprehension pass condition of three of five, before any spend.

- **`support-agent`** (`skills/support-agent`, v1.0.0) - the first operations role, built around one rule: never say "processed" when you mean "initiated", and never say either unless a system in front of you did it. That single guardrail prevents the most damaging support failure, a customer told their refund is done who then has to be disappointed twice. Triage is defined by money and risk rather than by how angry the customer sounds, because loud is not severe and triaging by volume puts the wrong tickets first. The authority ladder is written as a table to be filled in with the business's real limits, and the default when it is empty is that every monetary commitment needs approval - a safe default rather than an optimistic one. The India reference covers WhatsApp's 24-hour window, Hinglish register, COD refunds versus card reversals and marketplace off-platform rules, all of which cause real losses when ignored.

- **`data-analyst`** (`skills/data-analyst`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, and deliberately adjacent to `research-analyst` rather than overlapping: this one works on the business's own numbers, that one on the outside world, and both descriptions say so. Built to prevent two specific failures - the number that does not tie to the accounting system, and the metric defined after seeing which version looks better - which is why the definition gets five written lines before any computation and the readout carries a data-quality log. Decomposition is the centre of the craft file because "why did revenue drop" is the question most often answered with a single guessed cause and no arithmetic. `statistical-honesty.md` exists because the confident wrong number usually comes from a percentage on nine rows or a correlation reported as a cause, and both survive review because they look like data.

## 2026-10-31 (evening) - renamed to Employee Hub, v1.1.0

**The repo is now `employee-hub`.** Same content, new name, because the product is the employees, not the skills.

**New: the Employee Standard (`core/`).** Every employee now follows four obligations, and `scripts/validate_repo.py` fails a skill that does not wire them in.

- `core/connector-preflight.md` - check access before answering. Say the job in one line, list the capabilities it needs, check which tools are actually in front of you, then answer in one of four states: all connected (do the work), partly connected (do that part completely, name the gap, ask for the specific connector), nothing connected (name two or three connectors, ranked, with what each unlocks), or connected-but-wrong-tool (do the best available thing, then recommend the better one with a reason). Fixed three-line answer format. Includes the clothing-brand worked example and the India-first ranking - IndiaMART for B2B surplus, Shopify for D2C, Razorpay for collections, Shiprocket for last mile.
- `core/connector-catalog.md` - job to connector map, ranked for a business in India, with an explicit warning that the catalog does not mean anything is connected.
- `core/memory-and-notes.md` - four notes files per employee (learnings, contacts, journal, decisions), when to read, when to write, what a good note is.
- `core/performance-tracking.md` - one calendar event per task, weekly review, monthly scorecard with seven metrics, and the monthly self-audit that fixes one thing.
- `core/employee-standard.md` - the four obligations and the one-line test for whether they were followed.

**Every skill now ships with memory.** `skills/<employee>/notes/` seeded with learnings, contacts, decisions and a dated journal. `ai-engineer` is seeded with three real rules from building it.

**`ai-engineer` v1.1.0** - added the connector preflight section, the notes and self-tracking section, and a routing row that sends any data-or-action task through the preflight first.

**Cadence changed from one employee a day to five**, at 06:00 IST, in a fixed mix: two revenue-facing, two operations, one founder-leverage. Backlog expanded from 30 to 150 roles - thirty days of runway.
