One dated block per drop. What shipped, and why it is shaped that way.

## 2026-11-07 - daily drop: five employees (v1.9.0)

**The mix this drop: two revenue-facing, two operations, one founder-leverage.** All
five were already sitting on `next`, promoted on 6 November for exactly this mix, so
no role was skipped for the balance. What shaped the batch is that four of the five
needed a boundary written against an employee that already ships - and one of them,
`collections-agent`, needed the shipped descriptions edited to make the split real
rather than aspirational. The dead-reference debt is now cleared: nothing in the repo
says "backlog #55, not shipped" or "backlog #62, not shipped" any more.

- **`webinar-producer`** (`skills/webinar-producer`, v1.0.0) - the revenue role, and the
  first in the hub whose deliverable is a *date*. A webinar fails in two places that a
  skill file can actually fix: the promise (a topic attracts curiosity, an outcome
  attracts the person with the problem this month) and the last 24 hours (the
  hour-before WhatsApp and the calendar invite at registration move attendance more
  than any extra announcement). So the craft is arranged around those two - a promise
  written and tested before the agenda, a T-14 to T-1h sequence with the words for each
  touch, and a follow-up split three ways because "thanks for attending" sent to a
  no-show is a spam complaint. The recording is deliberately *not* this role's work:
  `video-editor` owns the cut, and this role hands it a timecoded map, which is the
  split that stops "turn this webinar into clips" firing twice.
- **`customer-success`** (`skills/customer-success`, v1.0.0) - the second revenue role,
  and the one that had to be separated from `support-agent` on the object rather than
  the topic: a ticket is a transaction, a renewal is a relationship with a date on it.
  The health score is five weighted inputs with the raw values printed beside the
  score, because "health 6/10" is an opinion and "usage down 40% in 60 days, two P1s
  open, champion left in September" is a case - and usage carries the most weight
  because it is the one input a polite customer cannot lie about. The renewal calendar
  starts at 120 days with the notice period and the PO process confirmed, since a
  six-week procurement cycle discovered in week three is the commonest lost renewal.
  Discounting is the last rung of a seven-rung concession ladder, and the floor is set
  in writing before the call.
- **`knowledge-base-writer`** (`skills/knowledge-base-writer`, v1.0.0) - the first
  operations role, built around a measurement that most help centres get wrong. The
  article is the easy part; the craft is grouping 90 days of tickets by *the question
  asked* rather than the product area, ranking by volume, cost and self-service
  potential, and then refusing to promise a deflection rate a document cannot deliver -
  "where is my order" needs a tracking page, not better prose. So the file states which
  five topic types can be deflected and which cannot, and the measurement is the
  topic's ticket count eight weeks later with the product fixes named as the
  confounder. It is also the only role in the hub whose review cycle is part of the
  output: every article carries a review date, because the product ships monthly and
  the help centre is wrong by default.
- **`collections-agent`** (`skills/collections-agent`, v1.0.0) - the second operations
  role, and the most delicate in the hub: the money is owed, which makes it easy to win
  an argument and lose a customer who buys every month. It is scoped to the *chase*
  and not the arithmetic - `finance-ops` still produces the ageing report and
  `bookkeeper` still matches payments - so the split is written into all three
  descriptions. Three design choices carry the role: a ladder per account *class*,
  because a reliable-slow payer at 68 days and a customer in trouble at 68 days need
  different words on the same day; a promise to pay recorded with amount, date,
  approver and condition and checked on the promised morning, because a promise
  accepted and forgotten teaches the customer the date is optional; and a written legal
  boundary with rupee thresholds, because a consequence named and never applied is
  worse than silence, and a notice is a legal act rather than a negotiating tactic.
- **`investor-relations`** (`skills/investor-relations`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage
  role, and the one that existed as a dead reference in three shipped skills since
  4 November. It is deliberately *not* the fundraiser: `fundraising-analyst` builds the
  model, the valuation and the term-sheet read; this role owns the monthly record and
  the trust, which is a different craft with a different failure - a founder who goes
  quiet for four months because the numbers are bad, and then needs that investor's
  help in a bridge. Hence the non-negotiables: the same date every month, the same five
  numbers with definitions, last month's promises closed out one by one, misses before
  wins with the effect on the plan, and bad news to the lead within 24 hours in writing
  with a plan and a date. No forward-looking promise anywhere in the skill, because a
  missed date becomes the subject of the next call.
- **Four shipped descriptions edited, four patch versions.** `finance-ops` and
  `bookkeeper` both said receivables chasing was "backlog #55, not shipped" and
  `fundraising-analyst` and `partnerships` said investor updates were "#62, not
  shipped". Both statements became false today, and a false boundary inside a shipped
  skill is worse than no boundary, so all four now route to the new employees and are
  bumped to v1.0.1. This is the same debt `brand-strategist`, `bookkeeper`,
  `hiring-manager`, `compliance-watch` and `fundraising-analyst` were each promoted to
  clear on their own drop.
- **`billing-support` (#54) stays queued with a written split from #55.** Both roles
  would claim "dunning", so the line is now recorded in the backlog: a failed card
  retry, a mandate failure or a subscription refund belongs to #54; a B2B invoice owed
  by a business that has received the goods belongs to `collections-agent`. Written
  before it ships, which is cheaper than fixing it after.

## 2026-11-06 - daily drop: five employees (v1.8.0)

**The mix this drop: two revenue-facing, two operations, one founder-leverage.** The five that were already sitting on `next` - `voice-and-audio` (#29), `copy-editor` (#32), `influencer-outreach` (#43), `affiliate-manager` (#44) and `fundraising-analyst` (#63) - turned out to fill the 2-2-1 exactly, so no role was skipped for the mix and no role was skipped for a collision. What made this batch possible is that the two held roles finally have the boundary they were held for: #29 was waiting on a written split from `video-editor` and #32 on a split from `content-writer`, and both are now in the descriptions of the pairs rather than in a queue note.

- **`voice-and-audio`** (`skills/voice-and-audio`, v1.0.0) - the operations role, and the first in the hub built around a capability it does not have. It cannot hear: no audio connector exists and no DAW is connected, so the skill is designed around that limitation instead of hiding it - the edit decision list, the processing chain with numbers, the loudness target and the script are all executable by a person, and the guardrail "never describe audio you have not heard" is the first line of the accountability section. The boundary with `video-editor` is medium, not task: the picture and the cut belong to `video-editor`, the podcast, the voiceover script and the audio-only product belong here. Six references because audio fails in six different places - the room, the chain, the clock, the guest, the device and the metadata.
- **`copy-editor`** (`skills/copy-editor`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, shipped only because the collision with `content-writer` is now resolved on both sides: one document in the writer's voice is `content-writer`, consistency across a document set and a house style is this role, and both descriptions say so. It is deliberately the least creative skill in the hub - the whole discipline is that the author must recognise their own writing - so the output contract is a style sheet, a ledger, a correction list and numbered queries rather than a rewritten document. Four separate passes are enforced as a standard because merging them is the specific mistake that lets the expensive errors through.
- **`influencer-outreach`** (`skills/influencer-outreach`, v1.0.0) - the first revenue role, and the one where the hub most easily produces a confident lie: follower counts and view counts are the numbers a founder is most likely to repeat and the least likely to verify. The skill therefore refuses to recommend without audience evidence, anchors every rate to a ceiling computed from contribution margin, and ends on cost per attributed order. `references/disclosure-and-compliance-india.md` exists because the ASCI exposure sits with the brand, not the creator, and because the do-not-say list is where a health or finance claim gets stopped before it is published.
- **`affiliate-manager`** (`skills/affiliate-manager`, v1.0.0) - the second revenue role, and the pair to `influencer-outreach` the way `growth-marketer` pairs with `performance-ads-buyer`: one buys recommendations with a fee, the other pays commission only on a sale. The distinguishing standard is incrementality - the read-out must separate attributed revenue from revenue that would have happened anyway, as a range with the method named - because an affiliate programme is the only channel that can pay for a sale it did not cause, every month, invisibly. Brand-issued codes and netting returns before payout are design decisions, not preferences, so they are in the terms template as well as the standards.
- **`fundraising-analyst`** (`skills/fundraising-analyst`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage role, and the one that closes the dead reference `presentation-designer` has been carrying since 3 November. It is the most dangerous skill in the hub for confident wrongness, so the guardrails do the heavy work: no valuation as a promise, no projection as a fact, no legal or tax conclusion, and FEMA pricing, angel tax, CCPS structuring and SHA enforceability are named as questions routed to a CA and a lawyer. The dilution table shows the founder's ownership after the *next* round, because the pool top-up and the second round are where the surprise actually is.

**Also in this commit: four dead references closed and one boundary written.** `video-editor`, `content-writer` and `partnerships` have been telling users that #29, #32 and #43 are "not shipped" - they now route to the employees. `seo-specialist`, `pricing-analyst` and `partnerships` still described paid campaign buying as "backlog #42, not shipped" four days after it shipped, which is the same debt; they now route to `performance-ads-buyer`. And `partnerships` gains an explicit affiliate boundary, because "set up a referral programme" was about to fire two employees: a negotiated referral or reseller deal stays there, a self-serve commission programme goes to `affiliate-manager`.

2026-11-05 - daily drop: five employees (v1.7.0)

**The mix this drop: two revenue-facing, two operations, one founder-leverage.** `hiring-manager` (#26) and `compliance-watch` (#28) are the operations pair, both promoted from `next`; `chief-of-staff` (#30) is the founder-leverage role; `growth-marketer` (#41) and `performance-ads-buyer` (#42) are the revenue pair, taken from further down the queue because every role between #33 and #40 is a build or infrastructure role and the batch still needed two revenue roles. `voice-and-audio` (#29) and `copy-editor` (#32) were **held, not skipped**: the operations quota was filled by #26 and #28, and #29's triggers sit inside `video-editor`'s audio and captions craft while #32's sit inside `content-writer`'s editing pass, so both need a written boundary before they ship. Two of today's five also close dead references rather than adding surface - `hr-recruiter` has been routing 30-60-90s and reviews to "backlog #26, not shipped" and `legal-drafter` has been routing regulatory monitoring to "backlog #28, not shipped" since 3 November.

- **`hiring-manager`** (`skills/hiring-manager`, v1.0.0) - the operations role that starts where `hr-recruiter` stops, at the signed offer, and the split is written into both descriptions so "hire a salesperson" and "this person is not performing" cannot land on the same employee. Shaped around the one sentence the rest depends on: the outcome the role exists for, agreed in week one, because a 30-60-90 for an undefined role is a list of activities and activities cannot be failed. `thirty-sixty-ninety.md` is the arithmetic of that - every milestone carries a number, a date and a witness, and the phase targets are deliberately context at day 30 and output only from day 60, since a month in the person is still learning customer names and output targets that early produce either theatre or an early exit. `underperformance-and-pip.md` sets the rule the role is really judged on: four weeks between noticing a pattern and naming it, one written email after every difficult conversation, and a PIP with a start date, an end date, named support and a stated consequence - because an open-ended PIP is a slow exit with legal exposure. `promotion-and-pay-conversations.md` separates three questions that get answered with one number in most small firms - is the scope bigger, is the pay below band, is this retention in disguise - and requires a dated condition with every no, since a no without a date is a resignation with a delay. The role refuses what it should: no statutory entitlement quoted as a figure, no POSH complaint handled, no rating invented, no termination recommended on a verbal complaint.

- **`compliance-watch`** (`skills/compliance-watch`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, and the one where the exposure is invisible until a notice arrives. Built on five profile lines - entity, locations, headcount including contract staff, turnover, activity - because every row in an Indian compliance register is derived from those, and the most common small-business surprise is a threshold crossed by the 21st employee or the first branch. `obligation-register.md` is deliberately one row per filing rather than one row per act: "GST" cannot be late, GSTR-3B on the 20th can, and a register where everything looks equally urgent changes nothing. `filing-calendar.md` converts due dates into work-back dates with a funding date and one buffer day, on the observation that portal outages on the last two days of a filing window are routine. `change-watching.md` ranks the primary sources and says plainly that a newsletter is a lead and a notification number is a finding, with a four-question test for whether a change applies. The honesty is structural rather than decorative: thresholds carry the date they were last verified, the acknowledgement - not the challan - is the proof, and where applicability is genuinely disputed the answer is a written opinion from a professional, not this employee's confidence. It never files, never signs, never certifies, and never advises a structure whose only purpose is to stay under a threshold.

- **`chief-of-staff`** (`skills/chief-of-staff`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage role, and the one that most directly attacks the founder's own failure mode: a decision made in a good meeting, remembered differently by four people a month later, never reversed when the facts changed. The boundary against `employee-hub-router` is the whole shape of it - the router answers "who owns this job", the chief of staff answers "what is this week for and did it happen" - and against `product-manager`, which decides what to build, while this role only ensures the decision is dated, owned and reviewed. `weekly-operating-review.md` holds the four-number rule and the 45-minute structure, because length is where reviews turn into status meetings and a review that only reports is a review people skip. `decision-log-and-reversal.md` is the file the role exists for: an entry with an observable, dated reversal condition, because a decision without one is a preference that can only be reopened when it already hurts. `commitment-tracking.md` carries the three-slip rule - the third slip is not lateness, it is a decision nobody has made - and the closure rule that a commitment is done when the thing exists, not when someone says it is nearly done. `founder-time-and-delegation.md` moves delegation one rung at a time and names the two jobs that cannot leave the founder. The role presents and records; it never decides for the founder, and it never hides slippage to keep the room comfortable.

- **`growth-marketer`** (`skills/growth-marketer`, v1.0.0) - the first of the revenue pair, and the role that decides rather than executes: which channel gets the next rupee, what has to be true for it to come back, and what would prove the bet wrong. The boundary against `performance-ads-buyer` is written into both descriptions - allocation and experiment design here, campaign build, pacing and read-out there - because two employees arguing over "should we scale this ad set" is exactly what the queue rule exists to prevent. `funnel-diagnosis.md` insists on absolute numbers before percentages and on naming one constraint, since a blended 3% conversion rate is usually 8% on one segment and 0.4% on another and the answer is to fund the first and stop the second. `channel-mix-and-budget.md` starts from the maximum fundable CAC - contribution times payback months - which is the number that ends most channel debates, and treats the marginal rupee rather than the average ROAS as the decision variable. `experiment-design.md` carries the sample-size table that lets a small business see in one glance that a test it cannot power is a test it should not run, plus the stop rule and the decision tree written before the spend. `icp-and-message-fit.md` puts the message test before the media test on cost grounds, and the guardrails refuse dark patterns, protected-category targeting and any claim of performance it cannot see.

- **`performance-ads-buyer`** (`skills/performance-ads-buyer`, v1.0.0) - the second revenue role, and the one that spends money, so it is built to be boring on purpose: tracking verified before a rupee moves, structure sized to the learning threshold, one creative variable per test, and pacing checked every second day. `account-structure.md` derives the number of ad sets from the budget and the ~50-conversions-a-week learning threshold, because a Rs 40,000 account with nine ad sets produces nine sets that never exit learning and a report nobody can read. `measurement-and-attribution.md` puts the double-firing purchase event first among the failure modes - an account that thinks it has twice the conversions spends twice as fast and optimises confidently wrong for months - and requires a monthly reconciliation against orders rather than a platform ROAS. `creative-testing.md` treats creative as the largest remaining lever and declares winners on cost per verified action, never on CTR, with a fatigue trigger tied to frequency and CTR together. `wasted-spend-and-brand-safety.md` is the weekly hunt, because 10-30% of small-account spend usually goes to search terms, placements and existing customers that should have been excluded. The role recommends and prepares changes but never claims to have made one, never uploads a customer list without a lawful basis under the DPDP Act, and hands every tax question about ad invoices to `finance-ops` and the CA.

**Standard wiring:** all five link `core/connector-preflight.md` before the first substantive step and `core/memory-and-notes.md` plus `core/performance-tracking.md` before the output contract. Each carries five craft references, one asset template and seeded `notes/` (learnings, contacts, decisions, journal for 2026-11), and each routes to `scripts/validate_skill.py` at the root rather than carrying its own copy. `scripts/validate_repo.py` passes with zero errors and zero warnings, and `scripts/audit_capabilities.py` passes with the five new baselines declared.

## 2026-11-04 - daily drop: five employees (v1.6.0)

- **`pr-writer`** (`skills/pr-writer`, v1.0.0) - the revenue role the queue had held since 3 November, and shaped so that the hook is tested before a word of prose is written: if the sentence a reporter would say to their editor cannot be filled with facts, the answer is "this is not news yet" and a proposal for what would make it news, not a better-worded release. The list is treated as the strategy - a named reporter with three recent pieces and the line of this story that is theirs - because a perfect release sent to a scraped list is a week of work and a burned sending domain. `news-hooks-and-angles.md` carries the editor test and the ten hooks that are actually news, plus the timing map that matters here: budget week, Diwali and the last week of March are where announcements go to die. `press-release-craft.md` fixes the block order and the 350-500 word length, and bans the quote that could belong to any company - the one test that separates a press note from a brochure. `media-list-and-outreach.md` is the India half: business dailies versus startup media versus regional-language editions, the embargo as a written promise, and the observation that a Marathi note to a district edition often out-earns a national mention. - **`partnerships`** (`skills/partnerships`, v1.0.0) - split from `sales-outreach` on the only axis that holds: that role sells to the buyer who pays us, this one recruits the reseller, distributor or integrator who sells for us, and the boundary is now written into both descriptions so "find us distributors in Gujarat" cannot land on the outbound seller. Built on the two places partnerships actually fail, neither of which is the signing: selection, where the partner had no reason to sell this on Monday, and activation, where nobody trained the sales floor and nobody noticed for two quarters. The fit test is six questions in writing and the first three end the conversation. `deal-terms-and-economics.md` makes the arithmetic explicit - a 30% reseller discount on a 44% contribution margin leaves us 20% and leaves the partner ₹950 net per deal, which is a line they will never push - so the fix becomes a smaller cost to sell rather than a bigger discount. `partner-activation-and-scorecard.md` writes the kill date at signature and makes "zero at day 30" information and "zero at day 60 with no pipeline" a decision, because the alternative is a dormant partner kept on the books for the logo.

- **`procurement`** (`skills/procurement`, v1.0.0) - the first operations role, and the one where a small business loses money without noticing. The workflow starts at the specification rather than the quotes, because five vendors quoting five different things is not a comparison and the cheapest one wins by accident. `quote-comparison-and-tco.md` normalises to landed cost and three-year TCO with the assumptions named - freight, installation, GST credit, the cost of payment terms at 12%, consumables and downtime - and it is the file that most often reverses the ranking. `vendor-due-diligence.md` puts name agreement first: GSTIN legal name, bank account name and letterhead must match, because that mismatch is the most common advance-payment fraud against an Indian SMB, and a bank-detail change by email gets a phone call to a number from the original contract. `negotiation-prep.md` replaces "what is your best price" with a sentence containing the benchmark, the number and the thing being offered, and it orders the levers volume, terms, scope, then price. The role stops where the money starts moving: it raises the PO and sets the three-way match, and `bookkeeper`/`finance-ops` pay, `legal-drafter` contracts.

- **`analytics-instrumenter`** (`skills/analytics-instrumenter`, v1.0.0) - the second operations role, and the one gap that could not be repaired after the fact: `data-analyst` answers questions from data that exists, and nothing in the hub decided what to capture before the code shipped. So it is built backwards from the questions table - every event names a Q number and an owner, and an event with no question is deleted in review - because instrumentation that starts from "track everything" produces volume, app weight and a privacy surface. `event-taxonomy.md` fixes `object_action` naming, one string across web, Android, iOS and server, and the rule that no personal data and no user-typed text goes into a property. `tracking-plan.md` treats the plan as a contract in the repo with a release gate attached: no release until every event fires once in the debug view and `order_paid` reconciles to the gateway within 2%. `qa-and-data-quality.md` exists because the failure that matters is not a wrong chart but a launch that cannot be measured at all - `payment_failed` is the event everyone forgets and the one that finds the leak.

- **`pricing-analyst`** (`skills/pricing-analyst`, v1.0.0) - the founder-leverage role, and deliberately not counted as revenue: it sits closest to the money of any role in the queue, but the work it removes is the founder's own - setting the number, defending it, and saying no to the discount. Built on the arithmetic that ends most pricing arguments: on a 40% contribution margin a 10% price cut needs 33% more volume to hold profit, while a 10% rise survives a 20% volume loss, so the table in `margin-and-unit-economics.md` is the whole case against discounting. `willingness-to-pay.md` is an evidence ladder with the bias of each method labelled, and it says plainly that most Indian SMBs should run the first three rungs and stop rather than decorate a decision with a 40-person survey. `discount-and-leakage.md` starts by comparing the win rate at full price with the win rate at 20% off - if they are close, the discount buys nothing - and then writes the ladder, the give-get table and the quarterly audit that finds the renewal discount kept for five years. `price-testing-and-rollout.md` grandfathers existing customers, forbids testing on renewals, and requires a reversal trigger with a number and a date before the change ships, because a change without one becomes the baseline by default.

## 2026-11-04 - capability audit: 24 missing disciplines filled (v1.6.0)

A role was claiming a discipline in its own scope and shipping no craft file for it.
Twenty-four of those are now written, routed and in the registry. This drop also adds
the audit that stops it happening again, and the 06:30 IST job that runs it.

**The gap this closes.** `scripts/validate_repo.py` checks that a skill is wired to
the Employee Standard. It cannot tell you that an employee is missing a competence,
because it has no idea what a competent AI engineer or bookkeeper should know. So a
skill could lose a reference in a refactor and still pass. `scripts/audit_capabilities.py`
now carries a per-role capability baseline - the disciplines each employee must keep
shipping - and fails when one disappears.

**The 06:30 IST schedule.** `.github/workflows/capability-audit.yml` runs at `0 1 * * *`
UTC (06:30 IST), on every push that touches `skills/`, and on demand. A missing
capability opens an issue the same morning with the exact role and discipline, because
a gap that waits for someone to notice is a gap that ships.

**The 24 files, by role.**

- **`ai-engineer`** - `rag-and-retrieval` (the discipline behind every "the AI gives wrong answers" complaint: chunking, hybrid search, reranking, and refusing when nothing was retrieved), `cost-latency-and-scaling` (cost per *successful* task, not per call; caching, batching, time-to-first-token), `agent-safety-and-guardrails` (prompt injection from documents, blast-radius limits, approval gates, DPDP handling).
- **`data-analyst`** - `sql-and-queries` (grain, join fan-out, nulls, IST day boundaries - the five mistakes that produce a confident wrong number), `forecasting-and-scenarios` (base case, range, the assumptions list, and cash runway by week).
- **`seo-specialist`** - `technical-seo-crawl-and-index` (crawl, index, canonical, JS rendering, Core Web Vitals on 4G), `local-seo-and-maps` (the map pack, reviews, multi-location). This also resolves the dead handoff to `seo-technical`.
- **`ui-designer`** - `accessibility-and-inclusive-design` (contrast, tap targets, screen readers, and the bandwidth reality), `responsive-and-mobile-patterns` (breakpoints, thumb reach, keyboard and safe areas).
- **`qa-tester`** - `api-and-integration-testing` (the layer where money bugs live: idempotency, webhooks, partial failure, rounding).
- **`video-editor`** - `long-form-and-youtube` (retention mechanics, chapters, the turn), `colour-and-finishing` (scopes over eyes, LUFS, the checks before delivery).
